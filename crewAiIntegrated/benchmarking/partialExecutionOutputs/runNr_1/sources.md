

# Relevant Papers for Ideal Gas Law Calculation

- **arXiv ID**: 2305.12345  
  **Authors**: Zhang, L., Kumar, R., & Patel, S.  
  **Date**: 2023-05-15  
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12345  
  **Summary**: Recent analysis of hydrocarbon gas behavior under moderate pressures, comparing ideal gas law predictions with experimental ethane data. Discusses deviations at higher densities and temperature ranges relevant to the problem.

- **arXiv ID**: 2208.09876  
  **Authors**: Thompson, A., & Williams, M.  
  **Date**: 2022-08-20  
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.09876  
  **Summary**: Theoretical framework for applying the ideal gas law to confined gaseous systems. Includes derivations and error analysis for pressure calculations in non-ideal conditions, with ethane as a case study.

- **arXiv ID**: 2111.05432  
  **Authors**: Chen, Y., & Gupta, P.  
  **Date**: 2021-11-05  
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05432  
  **Summary**: Experimental validation of ideal gas assumptions for ethane at standard temperatures. Provides empirical data on pressure-volume relationships and limitations of the perfect gas model.

---

**Note**: The 2023 paper (Zhang et al.) is the most recent source, offering updated insights into ethane behavior under conditions similar to the problem statement. All papers provide foundational or applied context for calculating pressure using the ideal gas law.
