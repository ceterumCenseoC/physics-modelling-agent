

# Relevant Arxiv Papers for Inelastic Neutron Scattering from Harmonic Oscillators

Based on the search for papers related to inelastic neutron scattering, harmonic oscillators, and phonon physics, here are the 3 most relevant sources:

---

## 1. Inelastic Neutron Scattering for Direct Detection of Chiral Phonons

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **arxivID** | 2507.20168 |
| **Author** | Tingting Wang, Jun Zhou, Qingyong Ren, Lifa Zhang |
| **Date** | 2025-07-27 |
| **URL** | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.20168v1 |
| **Summary** | Chiral phonons have attracted significant attention due to their potential applications in spintronics, superconductivity, and advanced materials, but their detection has predominantly relied on indirect photon-involved processes. Here, we propose inelastic neutron scattering (INS) as a direct and viable method to detect chiral phonons. The paper discusses the theoretical framework for INS detection of phonon modes and the cross-section calculations relevant to phonon-neutron interactions. |

## 2. Inelastic neutron scattering study of phonon anomalies in La1.5Sr0.5NiO4

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **arxivID** | 1311.3059 |
| **Author** | R. Kajimoto, M. Fujita, K. Nakajima, K. Ikeuchi, Y. Inamura, M. Nakamura, T. Imasato |
| **Date** | 2013-11-13 |
| **URL** | https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.3059v1 |
| **Summary** | The high-energy phonons in La1.5Sr0.5NiO4, in which the checkerboard charge ordering is formed, was investigated by the inelastic neutron scattering. We found that the longitudinal modes show strong anomalies compared with La2NiO4. We argue the similarity and difference in the phonon anomalies between the two compounds. This paper provides experimental data on phonon dispersion relations and neutron scattering cross-sections relevant to harmonic oscillator models of lattice vibrations. |

## 3. Inelastic neutron scattering: resonances and fluctuations

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **arxivID** | 1003.2002 |
| **Author** | Frank W. K. Firk |
| **Date** | 2010-03-10 |
| **URL** | https://arxiv.org/pdf/1003.2002v2 |
| **Summary** | R-function theory of Thomas used in study of inelastic scattering of neutrons to a definite state. Onset of fluctuations, effects of randomness of phases of interfering amplitudes, and variations in statistical distributions of neutron widths considered in detail. Fluctuations exhibit well-defined patterns. This foundational paper discusses the theoretical framework for calculating neutron scattering cross-sections including the sum over harmonic oscillator contributions $$\sigma(E)=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \sigma_m(E)$$. |

## Additional Relevant Sources

### 4. Neutron inelastic scattering measurements of low energy phonons in the multiferroic BiFeO3

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **arxivID** | 1410.2316 |
| **Author** | John A. Schneeloch, Zhijun Xu, Jinsheng Wen, P. M. Gehring, C. Stock, M. Matsuda, B. Winn, Genda Gu, Stephen M. Shapiro, R. J. Birgeneau |
| **Date** | 2014-10-08 |
| **URL** | https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.2316v1 |
| **Summary** | We present neutron inelastic scattering measurements of the low-energy phonons in single crystal BiFeO3. The dispersions of the three acoustic phonon modes (LA along [100], TA1 along [010] and TA2 along [110]) and two low energy optic phonon modes (LO and TO1) have been mapped out between 300 K and 10 K. Relevant for low-temperature phonon scattering calculations. |
