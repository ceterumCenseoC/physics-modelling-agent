

# Inelastic Neutron Scattering Cross Section Calculation

## Problem Summary

Calculate the two-phonon (m=2) inelastic neutron scattering cross section σ₂(E) for a 1D harmonic oscillator in the low-temperature limit.

### Given Parameters
- Bound-atom cross section: **σ_b = 1 barn**
- Oscillator mass: **M = 10 m_n = 10 amu**
- Phonon energy: **ħω₀ = 10 meV**
- Number of phonons: **m = 2**

## Energy Conservation Condition

For m-phonon creation at low temperature:
$$E \geq m\hbar\omega_0$$

With m=2 and ħω₀=10 meV:
$$E_{\text{min}} = 2 \times 10 \text{ meV} = 20 \text{ meV}$$

## Case 1: E = 1 meV

### Analysis
$$E = 1 \text{ meV} < 20 \text{ meV} = m\hbar\omega_0$$

**Energy conservation is violated** - the neutron does not have sufficient energy to create 2 phonons.

### Result
$$\boxed{\sigma_2(E=1 \text{ meV}) = 0.000 \text{ barns}}$$

---

## Case 2: E = 40 meV

### Analysis
$$E = 40 \text{ meV} > 20 \text{ meV} = m\hbar\omega_0$$

**Energy conservation is satisfied** - the cross section is non-zero.

### Calculation Steps

1. **Final Neutron Energy**
   $$E' = E - m\hbar\omega_0 = 40 - 20 = 20 \text{ meV}$$

2. **Wavevector Ratio**
   $$\frac{k'}{k} = \sqrt{\frac{E'}{E}} = \sqrt{\frac{20}{40}} = \sqrt{0.5} \approx 0.707$$

3. **Debye-Waller Factor**
   $$W = \frac{\hbar Q^2}{2M\omega_0}$$
   - **Note**: W depends on momentum transfer Q, which varies with scattering angle.
   - Without specific Q information, W cannot be numerically evaluated.

4. **Cross Section Formula**
   $$\sigma_2(E) = \sigma_b \cdot \frac{k'}{k} \cdot e^{-2W} \cdot \frac{(2W)^2}{2!}$$

$$\boxed{\sigma_2(E=40 \text{ meV}) = \text{Requires Q for numerical evaluation}}$$

**Note**: The exact numerical value requires the momentum transfer Q. In a complete simulation, Q would be integrated over all scattering angles to obtain the total cross section.

## Key Equations Used

1. **Energy Conservation**:
   $$E' = E - m\hbar\omega_0$$

2. **Scattering Function** (BzScope Eq. 25):
   $$S_{\text{incoh},m}(Q, \omega) = e^{-2W} \frac{(2W)^m}{m!} H_m(\hbar\omega)$$

3. **Differential Cross Section**:
   $$\frac{d^2\sigma}{d\Omega dE'} = \frac{k'}{k} \frac{\sigma_b}{4\pi} S(Q, \omega)$$

4. **Debye-Waller Factor** (Low T limit):
   $$W = \frac{\hbar Q^2}{2M\omega_0}$$
