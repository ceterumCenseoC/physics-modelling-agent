

# Formulas Extracted from ArXiv Papers

## 1. Boltzmann theory of the inverse Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional Rashba gas (arXiv:2601.02473)

### Formula 1: Rashba Hamiltonian (Eq. 1)
$$\hat{H} = \frac{k^2}{2m} + \alpha\hat{z} \cdot (\sigma \times k)$$

### Formula 2: Energy Dispersion (Eq. 2)
$$\varepsilon^\nu_k = \frac{k^2}{2m} + \nu k \alpha$$

### Formula 3: Electric Current in High-Density Regime (Eq. 17)
$$I_c(\mu \geq 0) = \frac{4h_y m \pi \alpha}{\sqrt{m(2\mu + m\alpha^2)}\tau} \frac{1}{L_x m \pi + 4\sqrt{m(2\mu + m\alpha^2)}\tau}$$

## 2. Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models (arXiv:2503.20712)

### Formula 1: Rashba Hamiltonian (Eq. 1)
$$\hat{H} = \frac{p^2}{2m} + \alpha\hat{z} \cdot (p \times \vec{\sigma})$$

### Formula 2: Spin Density in High-Density Regime (Eq. 8)
$$M_y = \frac{\mu_b |e| \tau}{2\pi} m\alpha [\hat{z} \times E]_y$$

### Formula 3: Edelstein Susceptibility with Mass Anisotropy (Eq. 12)
$$\frac{\chi_{xy}}{\chi_0}(r_m) = \frac{4\pi m_x \alpha r_m}{1 + \sqrt{r_m}}$$

## 3. Out-of-plane Edelstein effects: Electric-field induced magnetization in p-wave magnets (arXiv:2501.01888)

### Formula 1: First-order Perturbed Fermi Distribution (Eq. 1)
$$f^{(1)} = \frac{e\tau}{\hbar} E \cdot \nabla_k f^{(0)}$$

### Formula 2: Magnetization Formula (Eq. 2)
$$M = \frac{e\tau}{\hbar} g\mu_B \int \frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3} S(k) f^{(1)}$$

### Formula 3: Magnetoelectric Susceptibility (Eq. 13)
$$\alpha^{ME}_{zx} = -\frac{g\mu_B m}{2\pi \hbar^3 W} J$$

## 4. Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect (arXiv:1506.08330)

### Formula 1: Time-dependent Hamiltonian (Eq. 1)
$$H(p, t) = \frac{1}{2m}[p + eA(t)]^2 - \alpha([p + eA(t)] \times \sigma) \cdot \hat{z}$$

### Formula 2: Non-adiabaticity Parameter (Eq. 12)
$$\gamma \equiv \frac{eE}{\alpha p_F^2} = \frac{eEL_s}{E_F}$$

### Formula 3: Total Edelstein Spin Polarization (Eq. 29)
$$S_y(\tau) = \frac{2\alpha n}{v_F} \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta_p \left( |u_p(\tau - \tau_p)|^2 - \frac{1}{2} \right)$$
