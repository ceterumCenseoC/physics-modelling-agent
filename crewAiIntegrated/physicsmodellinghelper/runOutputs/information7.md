

# Formulas from Edelstein Effect - Rashba Fermion Papers

## Paper 1: Boltzmann theory of the inverse Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional Rashba gas (arXiv:2601.02473)

### Formula 1: Rashba Hamiltonian
$$\hat{H} = \frac{k^2}{2m} + \alpha\hat{z} \cdot (\sigma \times k)$$
**Source:** Eq. (1), Section II

### Formula 2: Energy Dispersion Relation
$$\varepsilon^\nu_k = \frac{k^2}{2m} + \nu k \alpha$$
**Source:** Eq. (2), Section II

### Formula 3: Electric Current in High-Density Regime (HDR)
$$I_c(\mu \geq 0) = \frac{4h_y m \pi \alpha \sqrt{m(2\mu + m\alpha^2)}\tau}{L_x m \pi + 4\sqrt{m(2\mu + m\alpha^2)}\tau}$$
**Source:** Eq. (17), Section IV

## Paper 2: Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models (arXiv:2503.20712)

### Formula 1: Rashba Hamiltonian
$$\hat{H} = \frac{p^2}{2m} + \alpha\hat{z} \cdot (p \times \vec{\sigma})$$
**Source:** Eq. (1), Introduction

### Formula 2: Spin Density in High-Density Regime (HDR)
$$m_y = \frac{\mu_b|e|E_x}{4\pi}(\bar{\tau}_+ k^+_F - \bar{\tau}_- k^-_F)$$
**Source:** Eq. (4), Section "DEE in Isotropic Rashba Model"

### Formula 3: Edelstein Susceptibility in Anisotropic Case
$$\frac{\chi_{xy}}{\chi_0}(r_m) = \frac{4\pi m_x \alpha r_m}{1 + \sqrt{r_m}}, \quad \frac{\chi_{xy}}{\chi_0}(r_\alpha) = \frac{4\pi m \alpha_x r_\alpha}{1 + r_\alpha}$$
**Source:** Eq. (12), Section "DEE in Anisotropic Case"

## Paper 3: Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction (arXiv:2307.02872)

### Formula 1: Total Magnetic Moment per Unit Cell
$$m = -\frac{\mu_B}{\hbar A_0} A_s \sum_{nk} f_{nk}(g_s s_{nk} + g_l l_{nk})$$
**Source:** Eq. (1), Section II

### Formula 2: Orbital Moment Expectation Value (Modern Theory)
$$l_{nk} = \frac{ie}{2\mu_B g_l} \langle \frac{\partial u_{nk}}{\partial k} | \times (\varepsilon_k - H_0) | \frac{\partial u_{nk}}{\partial k} \rangle$$
**Source:** Eq. (3), Section II

### Formula 3: Spin Moment Expectation Value for Equal Effective Masses
$$s_{nk} = n_1 \frac{\hbar}{2} \hat{e}_\phi$$
**Source:** Eq. (11), Section III.1

---

### Formula 4: Orbital Moment for Equal Effective Masses
$$l_{nk} = -n_1 \frac{e c t^2 \alpha_-}{\mu_B g_l (\alpha_-^2 k^2 + 4t^2)} \hat{e}_\phi$$
**Source:** Eq. (12), Section III.1
