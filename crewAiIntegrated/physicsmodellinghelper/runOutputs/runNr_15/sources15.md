

# Edelstein Effect Papers for Rashba Fermion Modeling

## 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
- **Arxiv ID:** 2503.20712
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** Investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. Analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. Studies how this effect depends on the effective mass, Fermi velocity, and other relevant parameters. This is the most directly relevant paper for calculating Edelstein effect at the Gamma point with parameter dependencies.

## 2. **Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect**
- **Arxiv ID:** 1506.08330
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Summary:** Provides theoretical framework for the Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. Discusses how current-driven spin polarization occurs in the perpendicular direction and examines both linear and nonlinear response regimes. Essential for understanding the fundamental physics of how electric field magnitude affects spin polarization.

## 3. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
- **Arxiv ID:** 2307.02872
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** Examines the spin Edelstein effect for generating spin polarization from charge current in systems without inversion symmetry. Also covers orbital Edelstein effect predictions for various systems with broken inversion symmetry. Provides insights into how magnetization direction depends on the applied electric field orientation and system parameters.

## 4. **Boltzmann theory of the inverse Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional Rashba gas**
- **Arxiv ID:** 2601.02473
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2026-01-05
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.02473v1
- **Summary:** Investigates the inverse Edelstein effect in a non-homogeneous system consisting of a ferromagnetic layer coupled to a Rashba two-dimensional electron gas. Within a semiclassical Boltzmann framework, derives analytical expressions for charge and spin currents and analyzes their dependence on various model parameters including Fermi velocity and chirality.

## 5. **Spin accumulation at nonmagnetic interface induced by direct Rashba Edelstein effect**
- **Arxiv ID:** 1805.05523
- **Authors:** Florent Auvray, Jorge Puebla, Mingran Xu, Bivas Rana, Daisuke Hashizume, Yoshichika Otani
- **Date:** 2018-05-15
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.05523v1
- **Summary:** Describes how electrons moving in an electric field experience a momentum-dependent magnetic field that couples to electron angular momentum (spin). Explains the physical phenomenon permitting generation of spin polarization from charge current (Edelstein effect), leading to spin accumulation at nonmagnetic interfaces.

### Key Parameters to Consider for Model Building:

Based on these papers, the Edelstein effect for a Rashba fermion at the Gamma point depends on:

| Parameter | Effect on Edelstein Magnetization |
|-----------|-----------------------------------|
| **Electric Field Magnitude ($E$)** | Linear relationship in weak field regime: $M \propto E$ |
| **Electric Field Direction** | Determines magnetization orientation via spin-momentum locking |
| **Fermi Velocity ($v_F$)** | Inversely affects spin polarization magnitude |
| **Rashba Coupling Strength ($\alpha_R$)** | Directly proportional to spin splitting and Edelstein efficiency |
| **Chirality** | Determines helical spin texture direction |
| **Effective Mass ($m^*$)** | Affects density of states and response |

The fundamental relation can be expressed as:
$$M_i = \chi_{ij} E_j$$

where $\chi_{ij}$ is the Edelstein susceptibility tensor dependent on Rashba parameters and Fermi surface properties.
