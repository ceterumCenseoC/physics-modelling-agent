

# Edelstein Effect Papers for Rashba Fermion Modeling

Based on the Arxiv search results, here are the most relevant papers for building a model to calculate the Edelstein effect for Rashba fermions:

## 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
- **Arxiv ID:** `2503.20712`
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** Investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. Analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. Studies how this effect depends on relevant parameters including Fermi velocity and model anisotropy.

## 2. **Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect**
- **Arxiv ID:** `1506.08330`
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Summary:** Discusses current-driven spin polarization in a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling (Edelstein effect). Extends beyond linear response regime to study how spin polarization depends on drift velocity and applied electric field magnitude.

## 3. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
- **Arxiv ID:** `2307.02872`
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** Explores the spin Edelstein effect for generating spin polarization from charge current in systems without inversion symmetry. Also investigates current-induced orbital magnetization (orbital Edelstein effect) with broken inversion symmetry, relevant for understanding magnetization direction dependencies.

## 4. **Boltzmann theory of the inverse Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional Rashba gas**
- **Arxiv ID:** `2601.02473`
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2026-01-05
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.02473v1
- **Summary:** Derives analytical expressions for charge and spin currents within a semiclassical Boltzmann framework for Rashba 2D electron gas. Analyzes dependence on system parameters, providing theoretical foundation for modeling Edelstein effect calculations.

## 5. **Spin accumulation at nonmagnetic interface induced by direct Rashba Edelstein effect**
- **Arxiv ID:** `1805.05523`
- **Authors:** Florent Auvray, Jorge Puebla, Mingran Xu, Bivas Rana, Daisuke Hashizume, Yoshichika Otani
- **Date:** 2018-05-15
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.05523v1
- **Summary:** Describes the Rashba effect where electrons moving in an electric field experience a momentum-dependent magnetic field coupling to electron spin. Explains generation of spin polarization from charge current (Edelstein effect) and its physical mechanisms.

### Key Modeling Parameters to Consider:
$$\text{Edelstein Effect: } \mathbf{m} \propto \alpha_R \mathbf{E} \times \hat{z}$$

Where:
- $\alpha_R$: Rashba spin-orbit coupling strength
- $\mathbf{E}$: Applied electric field
- $\hat{z}$: Normal direction to 2D plane
- Fermi velocity $v_F$ and chirality affect the magnitude and direction of induced magnetization

The first three papers are **most critical** for your modeling task as they directly address Rashba fermions, magnetization computation, and parameter dependencies including chirality and Fermi velocity.
