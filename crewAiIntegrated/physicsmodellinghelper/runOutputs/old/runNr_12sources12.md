

# Edelstein-Effect Relevant Papers

Based on the search results, here are the most relevant papers for calculating the Edelstein effect for a Rashba fermion and computing magnetization under applied electric fields:

---

## 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
- **ArXiv ID:** 2503.20712
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** This paper directly investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. It analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. The study examines how this effect depends on relevant parameters including Fermi velocity and anisotropy, making it highly relevant for computing magnetization magnitude and direction under different applied electric field conditions.

---

## 2. **Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect**
- **ArXiv ID:** 1506.08330
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Summary:** This foundational work examines the Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. It addresses how current-driven spin polarization occurs in the perpendicular direction, extending beyond linear response regime. The paper provides theoretical framework for understanding how spin polarization depends on drift velocity, which directly relates to applied electric field magnitude and direction calculations.

---

## 3. **Boltzmann theory of the inverse Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional Rashba gas**
- **ArXiv ID:** 2601.02473
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2026-01-05
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.02473v1
- **Summary:** This paper derives analytical expressions for charge and spin currents within a semiclassical Boltzmann framework for a Rashba two-dimensional electron gas. It analyzes dependence on various model parameters, providing the mathematical foundation needed to calculate Edelstein effect for Rashba fermions at the Gamma point of the Brillouin zone and understand parameter dependencies like chirality and Fermi velocity.

## Key Findings for Model Building

These papers collectively provide the theoretical foundation for:

1. **Rashba Hamiltonian at $\Gamma$ point:** $H = \frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2m^*} + \alpha_R (\hat{z} \times \vec{k}) \cdot \vec{\sigma}$ where $\alpha_R$ is the Rashba coupling parameter

2. **Edelstein Effect Formula:** The induced spin polarization $\vec{S}$ relates to applied electric field $\vec{E}$ via:
   $$\vec{S} = \chi \vec{E}$$
   where $\chi$ depends on Fermi velocity $v_F$, Rashba parameter $\alpha_R$, and scattering time $\tau$

3. **Parameter Dependencies:**
   - Chirality affects spin texture orientation
   - Fermi velocity determines magnitude of response
   - Electric field direction determines magnetization direction (perpendicular relationship)

4. **Boltzmann Transport Approach:** The semiclassical framework allows calculation of current-induced spin polarization considering both intrinsic and extrinsic contributions
