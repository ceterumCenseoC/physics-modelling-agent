

# Edelstein Effect - Relevant Research Papers

## Top Sources for Rashba Fermion Edelstein Effect Calculations

### 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
- **arxivID:** 2503.20712
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** Directly investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using semiclassical Boltzmann approach. Analyzes magnetization arising from direct Edelstein effect with anisotropic Rashba model. Studies dependence on effective parameters including Fermi velocity and chirality. **Most relevant for calculating Edelstein effect at Gamma point.**

### 2. **Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect**
- **arxivID:** 1506.08330
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Summary:** Provides theoretical framework for current-driven spin polarization in 2D electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling (Edelstein effect). Covers linear response regime and extends to nonlinear effects. Essential for understanding electric field magnitude dependence.

### 3. **Boltzmann theory of the inverse Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional Rashba gas**
- **arxivID:** 2601.02473
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2026-01-05
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.02473v2
- **Summary:** Derives analytical expressions for charge and spin currents within semiclassical Boltzmann framework for Rashba 2DEG. Analyzes dependence on model parameters. Complements direct Edelstein effect calculations.

### 4. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
- **arxivID:** 2307.02872
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** Explores spin Edelstein effect for generating spin polarization from charge current in systems without inversion symmetry. Predicts current-induced orbital magnetization (orbital Edelstein effect) for systems with broken inversion symmetry.

### 5. **Spin accumulation at nonmagnetic interface induced by direct Rashba Edelstein effect**
- **arxivID:** 1805.05523
- **Authors:** Florent Auvray, Jorge Puebla, Mingran Xu, Bivas Rana, Daisuke Hashizume, Yoshichika Otani
- **Date:** 2018-05-15
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.05523v1
- **Summary:** Explains Rashba effect mechanism: electrons moving in electric field experience momentum-dependent magnetic field coupling to angular momentum (spin). Describes generation of spin polarization from charge current (Edelstein effect).

### 6. **Enhanced Edelstein effect and interdimensional effects in an electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling interface**
- **arxivID:** 1912.01804
- **Authors:** A. C. Zulkoskey, R. Dick, K. Tanaka
- **Date:** 2019-12-04
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.01804v1
- **Summary:** Examines bound-state and free-state contributions to density of states in 3D electron gas with 2D interface with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. Studies confinement effects on Edelstein response.

### 7. **Edelstein effects, spin-transfer torque, and spin pumping caused by pristine surface states of topological insulators**
- **arxivID:** 1901.06953
- **Authors:** Wei Chen
- **Date:** 2019-01-21
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.06953v2
- **Summary:** Investigates Edelstein effect using semiclassical approach for pristine surface states of 3D topological insulators. Shows combined effect of random impurity scattering and spin-momentum locking of gapless Dirac cone yields current-induced surface spin polarization.

### 8. **Orbitally Dominated Rashba-Edelstein Effect in Noncentrosymmetric Antiferromagnets**
- **arxivID:** 1905.08279
- **Authors:** Leandro Salemi, Marco Berritta, Ashis K. Nandy, Peter M. Oppeneer
- **Date:** 2019-05-20
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.08279v1
- **Summary:** Discusses Rashba-Edelstein effect where spin polarization is electrically induced in noncentrosymmetric systems. Provides mean to achieve current-induced staggered spin-orbit coupling manipulation.

## Key Parameters for Model Building

Based on these sources, to calculate the Edelstein effect for a Rashba fermion at the Gamma point, you should consider:

- **Rashba Hamiltonian:** $$H = \frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2m^*} + \alpha_R (\sigma \times k) \cdot \hat{z}$$
- **Fermi velocity ($v_F$):** Determines carrier mobility and response magnitude
- **Rashba coupling ($\alpha_R$):** Controls spin-orbit interaction strength
- **Electric field direction ($\vec{E}$):** Determines magnetization direction via spin-momentum locking
- **Chirality:** Affects helical spin texture and polarization direction
- **Fermi energy ($E_F$):** Sets carrier density and screening effects

The magnetization magnitude scales as: $$M \propto \tau \alpha_R e E_F v_F^{-1}$$ where $\tau$ is scattering time.
