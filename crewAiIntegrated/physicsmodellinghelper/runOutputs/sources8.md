

# Edelstein Effect Research Papers

Based on the search results, here are the 3 most relevant papers for calculating the Edelstein effect in Rashba fermion systems:

## 1. Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models

- **ArxivID:** 2503.20712
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** We investigate spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. We analyze the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. We study how this effect depends on the effective mass anisotropy, Fermi velocity, and other relevant parameters. This paper directly addresses the calculation of Edelstein effect for Rashba fermions and the dependence on model parameters.

## 2. Out-of-plane Edelstein effects: Electric-field induced magnetization in $p$-wave magnets

- **ArxivID:** 2501.01888
- **Authors:** Motohiko Ezawa
- **Date:** 2025-01-03
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.01888v1
- **Summary:** In-plane magnetization is induced by the Edelstein effect in the Rashba spin-orbit interaction system. However, out-of-plane magnetization is more useful for switching a ferromagnetic memory. We study analytically and numerically electric-field induced magnetization in $p$-wave magnets with the aid of the Boltzmann equation. This paper provides analytical methods for computing magnetization magnitude and direction under different electric field configurations.

## 3. Acoustic Rashba-Edelstein effect

- **ArxivID:** 2107.03115
- **Authors:** Takumi Funato, Mamoru Matsuo
- **Date:** 2021-07-07
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.03115v1
- **Summary:** We theoretically study the mechanical induction of the spin density via the Rashba spin-orbit interaction (SOI). The spin density in the linear response to lattice distortion dynamics is calculated based on the microscopic theory. We reveal that there are two mechanisms of spin induction: one is the electric-field induced spin polarization (Edelstein effect) and another is the acoustic-wave induced spin polarization. This paper provides theoretical framework for understanding the Edelstein effect mechanism.

## Key Findings for Model Building

For calculating the Edelstein effect for a Rashba fermion at the Gamma point:

1. **Magnetization Formula:** The Edelstein effect produces an in-plane magnetization proportional to the applied electric field: $$M \propto \alpha_R \times E$$ where $\alpha_R$ is the Rashba coupling strength.

2. **Parameter Dependencies:**
   - **Fermi velocity ($v_F$):** Higher $v_F$ increases the Edelstein response
   - **Rashba coupling ($\alpha_R$):** Directly proportional to magnetization magnitude
   - **Electric field direction:** Determines magnetization orientation (perpendicular to both $E$ and Rashba field)
   - **Chirality:** Affects the sign and magnitude of the induced spin polarization

3. **Model Considerations:** The semiclassical Boltzmann approach is commonly used, accounting for relaxation time $\tau$ and anisotropy in the Rashba model.
