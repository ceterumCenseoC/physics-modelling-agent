

# Edelstein Effect Sources for Rashba Fermion Modeling

## #1 Most Relevant Source

**Title:** Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models  
**Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro  
**Published:** 2025-03-26  
**PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1  
**ArXiv ID:** 2503.20712v1  
**Summary:** This paper investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. It analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. The study examines how this effect depends on effective parameters including Fermi velocity and Rashba coupling strength, which are directly relevant for computing magnetization magnitude and direction under applied electric fields at the Gamma point.

## #2 Highly Relevant Source

**Title:** Spin and Orbital Edelstein Effect in a Bilayer System with Rashba Interaction  
**Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson  
**Published:** 2023-07-06  
**PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2  
**ArXiv ID:** 2307.02872v2  
**Summary:** This work examines the spin Edelstein effect for generating spin polarization from charge current in systems without inversion symmetry. It predicts current-induced orbital magnetization (orbital Edelstein effect) for various systems with broken inversion symmetry. The paper provides theoretical framework for understanding how magnetization depends on system parameters and electric field direction.

## #3 Highly Relevant Source

**Title:** Enhanced Edelstein Effect and Interdimensional Effects in an Electron Gas with Rashba Spin-Orbit Coupling Interface  
**Authors:** A. C. Zulkoskey, R. Dick, K. Tanaka  
**Published:** 2019-12-04  
**PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.01804v1  
**ArXiv ID:** 1912.01804v1  
**Summary:** This paper examines bound-state and free-state contributions to the density of states in a three-dimensional electron gas with a two-dimensional interface with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. It provides theoretical calculations of the Edelstein effect magnitude and its dependence on confinement potential and Rashba coupling parameters.

## Key Parameters for Model Building

Based on these sources, the following parameters are critical for calculating the Edelstein effect for a Rashba fermion at the Gamma point:

| Parameter | Symbol | Role |
|-----------|--------|------|
| Rashba coupling strength | $\alpha_R$ | Determines spin-momentum locking strength |
| Fermi velocity | $v_F$ | Controls carrier dynamics |
| Fermi energy | $E_F$ | Sets chemical potential position |
| Electric field | $\mathbf{E}$ | Applied field direction and magnitude |
| Chirality | $\chi = \pm 1$ | Band helicity (inner/outer Rashba branches) |
| Relaxation time | $\tau$ | Scattering rate affecting response |

The magnetization $\mathbf{M}$ is expected to follow:
$$\mathbf{M} \propto \chi \alpha_R (\mathbf{E} \times \hat{z})$$

where $\hat{z}$ is the growth direction perpendicular to the 2D plane.
