

# Edelstein Effect - Relevant Papers for Rashba Fermion Calculations

## Top 3 Most Relevant Papers

### 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
- **arxivID:** `2503.20712v1`
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20712
- **Summary:** This paper investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. The authors analyze the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. They study how this effect depends on the effective mass, Fermi velocity, and other relevant parameters. This is the most directly relevant paper for calculating Edelstein effect at the Gamma point with parameter dependencies.

---

### 2. **Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect**
- **arxivID:** `1506.08330v1`
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.08330
- **Summary:** This paper examines how a current driven through a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling induces a spin polarization in the perpendicular direction (Edelstein effect). The study extends beyond the linear response regime to include nonlinear effects, providing theoretical framework for understanding how magnetization depends on the magnitude of the applied electric field and drift velocity.

---

### 3. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
- **arxivID:** `2307.02872v2`
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02872
- **Summary:** This work explores the spin Edelstein effect for generating spin polarization from charge current in systems without inversion symmetry. It also predicts current-induced orbital magnetization (orbital Edelstein effect) for various systems with broken inversion symmetry. The paper provides insights into how chirality and spin-orbit coupling strength affect the Edelstein effect magnitude and direction.

## Key Model Parameters for Edelstein Effect Calculation

Based on these papers, the Edelstein effect for a Rashba fermion at the Gamma point depends on:

| Parameter | Symbol | Effect on Edelstein Effect |
|-----------|--------|---------------------------|
| Rashba coupling strength | $\alpha_R$ | Linear dependence of spin polarization |
| Fermi velocity | $v_F$ | Determines carrier mobility and response |
| Electric field magnitude | $E$ | Linear to nonlinear dependence on spin polarization |
| Chirality | $\pm$ | Determines spin texture orientation |
| Fermi energy | $E_F$ | Controls carrier density and screening |
| Scattering time | $\tau$ | Affects magnitude in Boltzmann approach |

The magnetization direction is typically perpendicular to both the electric field and the Rashba field direction, following the relation:
$$ \mathbf{M} \propto \alpha_R (\mathbf{E} \times \hat{z}) $$

where $\hat{z}$ is the direction perpendicular to the 2D plane.
