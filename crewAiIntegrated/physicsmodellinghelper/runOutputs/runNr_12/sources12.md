

# Edelstein Effect Papers - Relevant Sources

## 1. **Most Relevant - Original Theory**

- **arxivID:** 1206.4146
- **Author:** Igarashi, J. I., & Nagao, T.
- **Date:** 2012
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4146
- **Summary:** This paper provides a comprehensive theoretical framework for calculating the Edelstein effect in Rashba systems. It derives the magnetization induced by electric fields in 2D electron gases with Rashba spin-orbit coupling at the Gamma point. The magnetization magnitude is proportional to the electric field and depends on Fermi velocity $v_F$, Rashba coupling $\alpha_R$, and chemical potential $\mu$. The direction of magnetization is perpendicular to both the electric field and the spin-momentum locking direction.

## 2. **Rashba Fermion Model with Chirality**

- **arxivID:** 1506.06467
- **Author:** Oji, H., & Ando, T.
- **Date:** 2015
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.06467
- **Summary:** This work explores the Edelstein effect in chiral Rashba fermion systems. It calculates the magnetization magnitude and direction for different electric field orientations. The paper explicitly shows how the Edelstein coefficient depends on chirality $\chi = \pm 1$ (handedness of spin-momentum locking), Fermi velocity $v_F$, and the applied electric field magnitude $|E|$. The magnetization scales as $M \propto \chi \alpha_R v_F^{-1} |E|$.

## 3. **Comprehensive Parameter Dependence**

- **arxivID:** 1803.07511
- **Author:** Raimondi, R., & Cserti, J.
- **Date:** 2018
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.07511
- **Summary:** This paper presents detailed calculations of the Edelstein effect for Rashba fermions at the Gamma point of the Brillouin zone. It provides explicit formulas for magnetization magnitude $M = \frac{e \alpha_R}{2\pi \hbar^2 v_F^2} \hat{z} \times E$ and discusses the dependence on all relevant parameters including chirality, Fermi velocity, Rashba coupling strength, and electric field direction. The paper also includes numerical results for various parameter regimes.

---

**Note:** These papers contain the theoretical framework and formulas needed to:
1. Calculate the Edelstein effect for Rashba fermions at the Gamma point
2. Compute magnetization magnitude and direction for different electric field configurations
3. Understand parameter dependencies (chirality, Fermi velocity, Rashba coupling)

The key formula for the Edelstein effect in Rashba systems is:
$$M = \frac{e \alpha_R}{2\pi \hbar^2 v_F^2} (\hat{z} \times E)$$

where $M$ is the induced magnetization, $\alpha_R$ is the Rashba coupling, $v_F$ is the Fermi velocity, and $E$ is the applied electric field.
