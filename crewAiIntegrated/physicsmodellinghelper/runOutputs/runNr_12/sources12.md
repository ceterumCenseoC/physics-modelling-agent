

# Relevant Papers for Edelstein Effect in Rashba Fermion Systems

## 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
- **arxivID:** 2503.20712
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** Investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. Analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. Studies how this effect depends on effective parameters including Fermi velocity and model anisotropy.

## 2. **Out-of-plane Edelstein effects: Electric-field induced magnetization in $p$-wave magnets**
- **arxivID:** 2501.01888
- **Authors:** Motohiko Ezawa
- **Date:** 2025-01-03
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.01888v1
- **Summary:** Studies analytically and numerically electric-field induced magnetization in $p$-wave magnets with the aid of the Edelstein effect. Addresses out-of-plane magnetization which is more useful for switching ferromagnetic memory. Provides analytical expressions for magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of applied electric field direction and magnitude.

## 3. **Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect**
- **arxivID:** 1506.08330
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Summary:** Investigates current-driven spin polarization in a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling (Edelstein effect). Extends beyond linear response regime to study nonlinear effects. Discusses the relationship between drift velocity, spin polarization, and Rashba coupling parameters including Fermi velocity.

## 4. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
- **arxivID:** 2307.02872
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** Examines both spin Edelstein effect and orbital Edelstein effect in systems with broken inversion symmetry and Rashba interaction. Provides theoretical framework for calculating current-induced spin and orbital magnetization in bilayer systems with Rashba coupling.

## 5. **Out-of-plane spin polarization from in-plane electric and magnetic fields**
- **arxivID:** cond-mat/0609078
- **Authors:** Hans-Andreas Engel, Emmanuel I. Rashba, Bertrand I. Halperin
- **Date:** 2006-09-05
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0609078v1
- **Summary:** Shows that joint effect of spin-orbit and magnetic fields leads to spin polarization perpendicular to the plane of a two-dimensional electron system with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. Addresses angle-dependent impurity scattering effects and provides analytical expressions for spin polarization as function of electric and magnetic field directions.
