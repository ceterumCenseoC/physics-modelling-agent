

# Edelstein Effect - Relevant Scientific Sources

## Primary Sources for Edelstein Effect Research

### 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Found:** Arxiv (2025-03-26)
- **Arxiv ID:** 2503.20712v1
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** We investigate spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. We analyze the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. We study how this effect depends on the effective mass and Fermi velocity parameters.

### 2. **Resonant Edelstein and inverse-Edelstein effects, charge-to-spin conversion, and spin pumping from chiral-spin modes**
- **Authors:** Mojdeh Saleh, Abhishek Kumar, Dmitrii L. Maslov, Saurabh Maiti
- **Found:** Arxiv (2025-01-27)
- **Arxiv ID:** 2501.15752v2
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.15752v2
- **Summary:** Spin-orbit coupling in systems with broken inversion symmetry gives rise to the Edelstein effect, which is the spin polarization induced by an electric field or current, and the inverse-Edelstein effect (also known as the spin-galvanic effect), which is the electric current induced by an oscillatory magnetic field.

### 3. **Spin Hall and Edelstein effects in a ballistic quantum dot with Rashba spin-orbit coupling**
- **Authors:** Alfonso Maiellaro, Francesco Romeo, Mattia Trama, Jacopo Settino, Claudio Guarcello, Carmine Antonio Perroni, Pawel Wójcik, Bartłomiej Szafran, Daniela Stornaiuolo, Marco Salluzzo, Thomas Sand Jespersen, Nicolas Bergeal, Manuel Bibes, Roberta Citro
- **Found:** Arxiv (2026-02-02)
- **Arxiv ID:** 2602.02036v1
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.02036v1
- **Summary:** We study spin-resolved transport in a ballistic quantum dot with Rashba spin-orbit coupling, focusing on charge-to-spin conversion and spin Hall effect. In the regime where the dot size is comparable to the Fermi wavelength, we identify a clear crossover from weak localization to weak antilocalization.

### 4. **Theory of Spin-Charge Coupled Transport in a Two-Dimensional Electron Gas with Rashba Spin-Orbit Interactions**
- **Authors:** A. A. Burkov, Alvaro S. Nunez, A. H. MacDonald
- **Found:** Arxiv (2003-11-13)
- **Arxiv ID:** cond-mat/0311328v3
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0311328v3
- **Summary:** We use microscopic linear response theory to derive a set of equations that provide a complete description of coupled spin and charge diffusive transport in a two-dimensional electron gas (2DEG) with the Rashba spin-orbit (SO) interaction. These equations capture a number of interrelated effects including the Edelstein effect.

### 5. **Spin Relaxation, Diffusion and Edelstein Effect in Chiral Metal Surface**
- **Authors:** Yuta Suzuki, Yusuke Kato
- **Found:** Arxiv (2022-12-08)
- **Arxiv ID:** 2212.04202v2
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.04202v2
- **Summary:** We study electron spin transport at spin-splitting surface of chiral-crystalline-structured metals and Edelstein effect at the interface, by using the Boltzmann transport equation beyond the relaxation time approximation. We first define spin relaxation time and spin diffusion length for two-dimensional systems.

### 6. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in spin-orbit coupled noncentrosymmetric superconductor**
- **Authors:** Satoshi Ando, Yukio Tanaka, Mario Cuoco, Luca Chirolli, Maria Teresa Mercaldo
- **Found:** Arxiv (2024-08-15)
- **Arxiv ID:** 2408.08151v2
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08151v2
- **Summary:** Superconductors without inversion symmetry can exhibit a non-zero magnetization when a supercurrent is present, leading to non-dissipative magnetoelectric effects. Here, we focus on understanding the relation between the spin and orbital properties of these effects in conventional spin-singlet noncentrosymmetric superconductors.

### 7. **Tunable spin and orbital Edelstein effect at (111) LaAlO$_3$/SrTiO$_3$ interface**
- **Authors:** Mattia Trama, Vittorio Cataudella, Carmine Antonio Perroni, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Found:** Arxiv (2022-07-15)
- **Arxiv ID:** 2207.07663v1
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.07663v1
- **Summary:** Converting charge current into spin current is one of the main mechanisms exploited in spintronics. One prominent example is the Edelstein effect, namely the generation of a magnetization in response to an external electric field, which can be realized in systems with lack of inversion symmetry.

### 8. **Edelstein and inverse Edelstein effects caused by the pristine surface states of topological insulators**
- **Authors:** Wei Chen
- **Found:** Arxiv (2019-07-21)
- **Arxiv ID:** 1907.10486v1
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.10486v1
- **Summary:** The Edelstein effect caused by the pristine surface states of three-dimensional topological insulators is investigated by means of a semiclassical approach. The combined effect of random impurity scattering and the spin-momentum locking of the gapless Dirac cone yields a current-induced surface spin polarization.

### 9. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Found:** Arxiv (2023-07-06)
- **Arxiv ID:** 2307.02872v2
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** The spin Edelstein effect has proven to be a promising phenomenon to generate spin polarization from a charge current in systems without inversion symmetry. In recent years, a current-induced orbital magnetization, called orbital Edelstein effect, has been predicted for various systems with broken inversion symmetry.

### 10. **Effects of electron correlation on resonant Edelstein and inverse-Edelstein effects**
- **Authors:** Mojdeh Saleh, Abhishek Kumar, Dmitrii L. Maslov, Saurabh Maiti
- **Found:** Arxiv (2025-01-23)
- **Arxiv ID:** 2501.14059v2
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.14059v2
- **Summary:** Spin-orbit coupling in systems with broken inversion symmetry gives rise to the Edelstein effect, which is the induced spin polarization in response to an applied electric field or current, and the inverse Edelstein effect, which is the induced electric current in response to an oscillatory magnetic field.

## Summary of Key Findings from Literature

- **Direct Edelstein Effect:** Spin polarization induced by electric field/current in systems with broken inversion symmetry
- **Inverse Edelstein Effect:** Electric current induced by oscillatory magnetic field (spin-galvanic effect)
- **Rashba Model Parameters:** Effect depends on Fermi velocity, effective mass, chirality, and spin-orbit coupling strength
- **Magnetization Direction:** Typically perpendicular to applied electric field due to spin-momentum locking
- **Theoretical Framework:** Boltzmann transport equation and linear response theory commonly used

These sources provide the scientific validity needed for calculating the Edelstein effect magnitude and direction for different electric field configurations and model parameters.
