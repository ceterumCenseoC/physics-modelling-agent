

# Edelstein Effect - Rashba Fermion Papers

## 1. Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models

- **arxivID:** 2503.20712
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20712
- **Summary:** We investigate spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. We analyze the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. We study how this effect depends on the effective mass anisotropy and Fermi velocity, providing analytical expressions for the Edelstein susceptibility. This paper is highly relevant for calculating the Edelstein effect for Rashba fermions and understanding parameter dependencies including chirality and Fermi velocity.

## 2. Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect

- **arxivID:** 1506.08330
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.08330
- **Summary:** It is well known that a current driven through a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling induces a spin polarization in the perpendicular direction (Edelstein effect). This phenomenon has been extensively studied in the linear response regime, i.e., when the average drift velocity is small compared to the Fermi velocity. This paper extends the theory to the nonlinear regime and provides fundamental theoretical framework for understanding how magnetization depends on the applied electric field magnitude and direction, which is essential for the requested calculations.

## 3. Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction

- **arxivID:** 2307.02872
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02872
- **Summary:** The spin Edelstein effect has proven to be a promising phenomenon to generate spin polarization from a charge current in systems without inversion symmetry. In recent years, a current-induced orbital magnetization, called orbital Edelstein effect, has been predicted for various systems with broken inversion symmetry. This paper provides detailed calculations of both spin and orbital Edelstein effects in Rashba systems, including the dependence on material parameters and electric field configurations, making it valuable for computing magnetization magnitude and direction under different electric field conditions.
