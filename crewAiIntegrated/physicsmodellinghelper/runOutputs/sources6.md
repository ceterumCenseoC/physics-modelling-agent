

# Edelstein-Effect Relevant Papers for Rashba Fermion Modeling

## Top 3 Most Relevant Sources

### 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
- **arxivID:** `2503.20712`
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** This paper investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. It analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. The study examines how this effect depends on Fermi velocity and other model parameters, providing analytical expressions for the Edelstein effect at the Gamma point. This is the most directly relevant paper for calculating magnetization magnitude and direction under different electric field configurations.

### 2. **Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect**
- **arxivID:** `1506.08330`
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Summary:** This paper studies the current-driven spin polarization in a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling (the Edelstein effect) in the linear response regime. It provides fundamental theoretical framework for understanding how the average drift velocity relates to spin polarization, including dependencies on chirality and Fermi velocity. The work extends to nonlinear regimes, offering comprehensive understanding of electric field magnitude effects on the induced magnetization.

### 3. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
- **arxivID:** `2307.02872`
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** This paper examines the spin Edelstein effect for generating spin polarization from charge current in systems without inversion symmetry, including current-induced orbital magnetization (orbital Edelstein effect). It provides detailed analysis of how spin and orbital magnetization depend on Rashba coupling strength, Fermi surface properties, and applied electric field direction. The work includes calculations relevant to the Gamma point of the Brillouin zone and parameter dependencies including chirality effects.

## Additional Relevant Sources

### 4. **Boltzmann theory of the inverse Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional Rashba gas**
- **arxivID:** `2601.02473`
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2026-01-05
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.02473v1
- **Summary:** Derives analytical expressions for charge and spin currents in a Rashba 2DEG within semiclassical Boltzmann framework, analyzing dependence on system parameters.

### 5. **Spin accumulation at nonmagnetic interface induced by direct Rashba Edelstein effect**
- **arxivID:** `1805.05523`
- **Authors:** Florent Auvray, Jorge Puebla, Mingran Xu, Bivas Rana, Daisuke Hashizume, Yoshichika Otani
- **Date:** 2018-05-15
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.05523v1
- **Summary:** Describes the Rashba effect physics where electrons moving in an electric field experience momentum-dependent magnetic field coupling to electron spin, enabling generation of spin polarization from charge current.
