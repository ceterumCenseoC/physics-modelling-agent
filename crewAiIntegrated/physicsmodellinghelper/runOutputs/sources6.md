

# Edelstein Effect Research Papers for Rashba Fermion Model

## **1. Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**

- **arxivID:** 2503.20712v1
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** Directly investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. Analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. Studies how this effect depends on effective parameters. This is the most relevant paper for calculating Edelstein effect at the Gamma point with parameter dependencies.

---

## **2. Spin and Orbital Edelstein Effect in a Bilayer System with Rashba Interaction**

- **arxivID:** 2307.02872v2
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** Examines the spin Edelstein effect for generating spin polarization from charge current in systems without inversion symmetry. Also discusses current-induced orbital magnetization (orbital Edelstein effect) for systems with broken inversion symmetry. Provides theoretical framework for magnetization magnitude and direction calculations.

## **3. Theory of Spin-Charge Coupled Transport in a Two-Dimensional Electron Gas with Rashba Spin-Orbit Interactions**

- **arxivID:** cond-mat/0311328v3
- **Authors:** A. A. Burkov, Alvaro S. Nunez, A. H. MacDonald
- **Date:** 2003-11-13
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0311328v3
- **Summary:** Uses microscopic linear response theory to derive equations for coupled spin and charge diffusive transport in a 2DEG with Rashba spin-orbit interaction. This foundational paper provides the theoretical framework for calculating Edelstein effect and its dependence on model parameters.

## **6. Nonlinear Spin and Orbital Edelstein Effect in WTe2**

- **arxivID:** 2412.02938v1
- **Authors:** Xing-Guo Ye, Peng-Fei Zhu, Wen-Zheng Xu, Tong-Yang Zhao, Zhi-Min Liao
- **Date:** 2024-12-04
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.02938v1
- **Summary:** Discusses how current-induced shift of the Fermi contour in k-space leads to spin polarization (Edelstein effect) which depends linearly on applied current. ## **Expected Results Summary**

The Edelstein effect magnetization $\mathbf{M}$ for Rashba fermions at the Gamma point can be expressed as:

$$\mathbf{M} = \chi \frac{e\alpha_R}{\hbar^2} \mathbf{E} \times \hat{z} \cdot f(E_F, v_F, \tau)$$

Where:
- $\mathbf{E}$ is the applied electric field
- $\hat{z}$ is the normal to the 2D plane
- $\chi$ is the chirality parameter
- $f(E_F, v_F, \tau)$ is a function of Fermi energy, Fermi velocity, and scattering time

The magnetization direction is perpendicular to both the electric field and the spin-orbit coupling axis, with magnitude scaling linearly with electric field strength in the linear response regime.
