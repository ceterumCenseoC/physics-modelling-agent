

# Edelstein Effect - Scientific Sources

## 1. Observation of the orbital inverse Rashba–Edelstein effect
**Authors:** Anas El Hamdi, Jean-Yves Chauleau, Margherita Boselli, Clémentine Thibault, Cosimo Gorini, Alexander Smogunov, Cyrille Barreteau, Stefano Gariglio, Jean-Marc Triscone, Michel Viret  
**Where it was found:** Nature Physics, Volume 19, pages 1855–1860 (2023)  
**Summary:** This paper observes spin-to-charge conversion at a LaAlO₃/SrTiO₃ interface and demonstrates that its orbital contribution dominates over the spin contribution. The analysis of in-plane anisotropy and gate voltage dependence of angular momentum conversion into charge highlights salient features of orbital Rashba splitting. Results open the door to broader use of pure orbital angular momentum effects and confirm the potential of the orbital degree of freedom for information storage and processing. This work directly addresses the inverse Edelstein Effect with orbital contributions.

## 2. Spin polarization of conduction electrons induced by electric current in two-dimensional asymmetric electron systems
**Authors:** V. M. Edelstein  
**Where it was found:** Solid State Communications, Volume 73, pages 233–235 (1990)  
**Summary:** This is the foundational theoretical paper that first predicted the Edelstein Effect, describing how electric current in two-dimensional asymmetric electron systems can induce spin polarization. This work established the theoretical basis for all subsequent Edelstein Effect research and is the origin of the effect's name.

## 3. Spin and orbital Edelstein effects in a two-dimensional electron gas: theory and application to SrTiO₃ interfaces
**Authors:** A. Johansson, B. Göbel, J. Henk, M. Bibes, I. Mertig  
**Where it was found:** Physical Review Research, Volume 3, 013275 (2021)  
**Summary:** This paper provides theoretical treatment of both spin and orbital Edelstein effects in two-dimensional electron gases, with specific application to SrTiO₃ interfaces. It develops the microscopic theory connecting Rashba-type spin-orbit coupling to current-induced spin polarization and orbital angular momentum effects.

## 4. Microscopic theory of the inverse Edelstein effect
**Authors:** K. Shen, G. Vignale, R. Raimondi  
**Where it was found:** Physical Review Letters, Volume 112, 096601 (2014)  
**Summary:** This work develops a microscopic theory of the inverse Edelstein effect, providing detailed understanding of how spin polarization can be converted to charge current in systems with spin-orbit coupling. It establishes the theoretical framework for spin-to-charge conversion efficiency calculations.

## 6. Signatures of Kramers-Weyl fermions in the charge density wave material (TaSe₄)₂I
**Authors:** Soyeun Kim, Robert C. McKay, Nina Bielinski, Junehu Park, Chengxi Zhao, Meng-Kai Lin, Joseph A. Hlevyack, Xuefei Guo, Sung-Kwan Mo, Peter Abbamonte, T.-C. Chiang, André Schleife, Daniel P. Shoemaker, Barry Bradlyn, Fahad Mahmood  
**Where it was found:** Communications Materials, Volume 6, Article 227 (October 20, 2025)  
**Summary:** This paper identifies Kramers-Weyl fermions in a chiral charge density wave material using helicity-dependent ARPES. The work involves strong spin-orbit coupling in chiral crystals, which is closely related to the symmetry-breaking conditions that enable Edelstein Effect phenomena. It provides experimental evidence for topological fermions with spin textures that are relevant to understanding spin-momentum locking in Edelstein systems.
