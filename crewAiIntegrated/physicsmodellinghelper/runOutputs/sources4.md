

# Edelstein-Effect Sources for Rashba Fermion Model

## 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Published:** 2025-03-26
- **ArXiv ID:** 2503.20712v1
- **PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Found:** ArXiv search
- **Summary:** Investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. Analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. Studies how this effect depends on the effective mass, Rashba coupling strength, and Fermi energy. Contains analytical expressions for current-induced spin polarization that can be used to build models for magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of applied electric field.

## 2. **Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect**
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Published:** 2015-06-27
- **ArXiv ID:** 1506.08330v1
- **PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Found:** ArXiv search
- **Summary:** Discusses current-driven spin polarization in a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling (Edelstein effect). Extends beyond linear response regime to study nonlinear effects. Provides theoretical framework for understanding how spin polarization depends on drift velocity, which is directly related to applied electric field. Contains derivations showing dependence on Fermi velocity and Rashba coupling parameters.

## 3. **Boltzmann theory of the inverse Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional Rashba gas**
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Published:** 2026-01-05
- **ArXiv ID:** 2601.02473v1
- **PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.02473v1
- **Found:** ArXiv search
- **Summary:** Derives analytical expressions for charge and spin currents in a Rashba two-dimensional electron gas within a semiclassical Boltzmann framework. Analyzes dependence on various parameters including Rashba coupling strength, Fermi velocity, and scattering time. Provides complementary information to the direct Edelstein effect calculation with explicit parameter dependencies.

## 4. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Published:** 2023-07-06
- **ArXiv ID:** 2307.02872v2
- **PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Found:** ArXiv search
- **Summary:** Investigates both spin and orbital Edelstein effects in systems with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. Discusses current-induced orbital magnetization in addition to spin polarization. Contains calculations showing how magnetization depends on Fermi velocity, chirality of Rashba bands, and applied electric field direction. Useful for understanding both spin and orbital contributions to the Edelstein effect.

## 5. **Spin accumulation at nonmagnetic interface induced by direct Rashba Edelstein effect**
- **Authors:** Florent Auvray, Jorge Puebla, Mingran Xu, Bivas Rana, Daisuke Hashizume, Yoshichika Otani
- **Published:** 2018-05-15
- **ArXiv ID:** 1805.05523v1
- **PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.05523v1
- **Found:** ArXiv search
- **Summary:** Describes how the Rashba effect permits generation of spin polarization from charge current (Edelstein effect). Provides experimental and theoretical analysis of spin accumulation at interfaces. Contains information about how spin polarization magnitude and direction depend on current direction and magnitude, which relates to applied electric field.

## 6. **Spin and orbital-to-charge conversion in noncentrosymmetric materials: Hall versus Rashba-Edelstein effects**
- **Authors:** Diego Garcia Ovalle, Aurelien Manchon
- **Published:** 2025-11-12
- **ArXiv ID:** 2511.09511v3
- **PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.09511v3
- **Found:** ArXiv search
- **Summary:** Develops a general formalism for spin- and orbital-to-charge conversion in nonmagnetic materials with broken inversion symmetry. Treats Hall effect and Rashba-Edelstein effect contributions on equal footing. Contains macroscopic theory that can be used to calculate magnetization from applied electric field, with explicit parameter dependencies on Fermi velocity and Rashba coupling strength.

## 7. **Spin Hall and Edelstein effects in a ballistic quantum dot with Rashba spin-orbit coupling**
- **Authors:** Alfonso Maiellaro, Francesco Romeo, Mattia Trama, Jacopo Settino, Claudio Guarcello, Carmine Antonio Perroni, Pawel Wójcik, Bartłomiej Szafran, Daniela Stornaiuolo, Marco Salluzzo, Thomas Sand Jespersen, Nicolas Bergeal, Manuel Bibes, Roberta Citro
- **Published:** 2026-02-02
- **ArXiv ID:** 2602.02036v1
- **PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.02036v1
- **Found:** ArXiv search
- **Summary:** Studies spin-resolved transport in systems with Rashba spin-orbit coupling, focusing on charge-to-spin conversion. Identifies crossover from weak localization to weak antilocalization. Contains calculations of spin polarization as function of system parameters including Fermi wavelength, which relates to Fermi velocity and can inform Edelstein effect calculations at the Gamma point.

## 8. **Orbitally Dominated Rashba-Edelstein Effect in Noncentrosymmetric Antiferromagnets**
- **Authors:** Leandro Salemi, Marco Berritta, Ashis K. Nandy, Peter M. Oppeneer
- **Published:** 2019-05-20
- **ArXiv ID:** 1905.08279v1
- **PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.08279v1
- **Found:** ArXiv search
- **Summary:** Investigates Rashba-Edelstein effect where spin polarization is electrically induced in noncentrosymmetric systems. Discusses orbital contributions to the effect. Contains information about how spin-orbit coupling and band structure parameters affect the magnitude of induced spin polarization.

## 9. **Slow-phonon control of spin Edelstein effect in Rashba $d$-wave altermagnets**
- **Authors:** Mohsen Yarmohammadi, Jacob Linder, James K. Freericks
- **Published:** 2025-10-02
- **ArXiv ID:** 2510.02451v2
- **PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.02451v2
- **Found:** ArXiv search
- **Summary:** Investigates induced spin polarization in two-dimensional $d$-wave altermagnets using Rashba coupling model. Employs calculations of how slow lattice vibrations influence both intrinsic and externally induced spin polarizations. Contains theoretical framework for calculating spin polarization dependence on electric field and material parameters.

## 10. **Enhanced Edelstein effect and interdimensional effects in an electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling interface**
- **Authors:** A. C. Zulkoskey, R. Dick, K. Tanaka
- **Published:** 2019-12-04
- **ArXiv ID:** 1912.01804v1
- **PDF Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.01804v1
- **Found:** ArXiv search
- **Summary:** Examines bound-state and free-state contributions to density of states in three-dimensional electron gas with two-dimensional interface with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. Contains calculations of how confinement affects the Edelstein effect magnitude and provides parameter dependencies for Rashba coupling strength and interface potential.

---

**Note:** These sources provide the theoretical framework, analytical expressions, and parameter dependencies needed to calculate the Edelstein effect for a Rashba fermion at the Gamma point of the Brillouin zone. The most relevant papers for building your model are #1, #2, #3, and #6, as they contain explicit analytical expressions for magnetization as a function of electric field, Fermi velocity, and Rashba coupling parameters.
