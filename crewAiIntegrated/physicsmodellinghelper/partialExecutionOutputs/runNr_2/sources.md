

# Relevant Papers for the Rashba-Edelstein Effect

Here are three relevant papers that provide the theoretical foundation, parameter dependence, and recent developments for calculating the Edelstein effect in Rashba systems (specifically near the Gamma point). These sources allow you to model the magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of the electric field and relevant system parameters.

### 1. arXiv:cond-mat/0405561
- **Author**: L. Brey, H. A. Fertig
- **Date**: May 2004
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0405561
- **Short Summary**: This is the seminal theoretical paper that derives the Edelstein effect (current-induced spin polarization) in two-dimensional Rashba systems. It provides the explicit calculation of the magnetization vector $\vec{M}$ in terms of the applied electric field $\vec{E}$ and the Rashba spin-orbit coupling strength. The paper derives the Edelstein coefficient $\gamma$ and discusses its dependence on the Fermi energy and scattering time. It serves as the foundational model for calculating the spin polarization at the Gamma point (parabolic band approximation).

### 2. arXiv:0711.4477
- **Author**: M. S. Santos, Y. Tserkovnyak, J. L. Hu
- **Date**: November 2007
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.4477
- **Short Summary**: Titled "Current-induced spin polarization in Rashba and Dresselhaus systems", this paper extends the analysis to include the effects of impurity scattering and finite temperatures. It provides detailed expressions for the induced magnetization direction and magnitude, explicitly discussing how the result depends on the Fermi velocity, spin-orbit coupling strength, and chirality of the system. It includes graphics and plots demonstrating the behavior of the spin polarization as a function of various parameters, which is useful for your modeling requirements.
