### 2. Theory of Spin-Charge Coupled Transport in a Two-Dimensional Electron Gas with Rashba Spin-Orbit Interactions
- **arXiv ID:** cond-mat/0311328
- **Authors:** A. A. Burkov, Alvaro S. Nunez, A. H. MacDonald
- **Date:** 2003-11-13
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0311328v3
- **Summary:** This foundational paper uses microscopic linear response theory to derive a complete set of equations describing coupled spin and charge diffusive transport in a 2DEG with Rashba spin-orbit interaction. It captures interrelated effects including the Edelstein effect and provides the theoretical framework necessary for calculating magnetization from applied electric fields. The paper establishes the fundamental relationships between electric field, spin polarization, and system parameters like spin-orbit coupling strength.

## Key Parameters for Modeling (Based on Papers)

The papers collectively identify these critical parameters for modeling the Edelstein effect in Rashba systems:

- **Spin-orbit coupling strength ($\alpha_R$):** Determines the magnitude of spin splitting and directly affects magnetization
- **Fermi velocity ($v_F$):** Influences the relationship between applied electric field and resulting spin polarization
- **Chirality:** Affects the direction of induced magnetization relative to current flow
- **Electric field direction and magnitude:** Determines both the magnitude and direction of induced spin polarization
- **Fermi energy ($E_F$):** Sets the occupation of Rashba-split bands and affects the overall response

The most recent paper (2503.20712) appears to provide the most comprehensive framework for the specific modeling task requested, with explicit treatment of parameter dependencies and graphical representations of results.
