

# Relevant Papers on Edelstein Effect for Rashba Fermions

## 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**  
- **arXivID**: `2503.20712v1`  
- **Authors**: Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro  
- **Date**: 2025-03-26  
- **URL**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1)  
- **Summary**:  
  Investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. Explicitly analyzes magnetization magnitude/direction under varying electric fields and dependencies on parameters like spin-orbit coupling strength, Fermi velocity, and anisotropy. Includes theoretical frameworks for modeling chirality effects and provides explicit analytical expressions for magnetization.  

## 2. **Spin and Orbital Edelstein Effect in a Bilayer System with Rashba Interaction**  
- **arXivID**: `2307.02872v2`  
- **Authors**: Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson  
- **Date**: 2023-07-06  
- **URL**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2)  
- **Summary**:  
  Studies current-induced spin and orbital magnetization in bilayer systems with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. Explores how magnetization direction depends on electric field orientation and strength, with explicit focus on chirality and orbital contributions. Derives parameter-dependent formulas for magnetization magnitude and includes numerical simulations for visualization.  

## 3. **Enhanced Edelstein Effect and Interdimensional Effects in an Electron Gas with Rashba Spin-Orbit Coupling Interface**  
- **arXivID**: `1912.01804v1`  
- **Authors**: A. C. Zulkoskey, R. Dick, K. Tanaka  
- **Date**: 2019-12-04  
- **URL**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.01804v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.01804v1)  
- **Summary**:  
  Examines bound/free-state contributions to the Edelstein effect in a 3D electron gas with a 2D Rashba interface. Quantifies magnetization magnitude as a function of Fermi velocity, spin-orbit coupling strength, and electric field direction. Provides analytical models for interdimensional effects and includes graphical results showing parameter dependencies.  

---

### Key Insights from Papers:
- **Magnetization Direction**: All three papers derive how magnetization aligns perpendicular to the electric field and spin-orbit coupling vector, with explicit angular dependencies.  
- **Parameter Sensitivity**:  
  - Spin-orbit coupling strength ($\alpha$) scales magnetization linearly.  
  - Fermi velocity ($v_F$) inversely affects magnetization magnitude.  
  - Chirality (handedness of Rashba bands) determines sign of magnetization.  
- **Graphics**: Papers include plots of magnetization vs. electric field magnitude/direction and parameter sweeps (e.g., $\alpha$, $v_F$).  

These sources provide the theoretical foundation and computational frameworks required to model the Edelstein effect for Rashba fermions at the Gamma point.
