

# Relevant Papers for Edelstein Effect in Rashba Fermion Systems

## 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
- **arxivID:** `2503.20712`
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** This paper investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. It analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. The study examines how the effect depends on effective mass, Fermi energy, and spin-orbit coupling strength, providing explicit analytical expressions and numerical results for magnetization magnitude and direction under applied electric fields. This is the most directly relevant paper for your research topic.

## 2. **Spin and Orbital Edelstein Effect in a Bilayer System with Rashba Interaction**
- **arxivID:** `2307.02872`
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** This paper focuses on the spin Edelstein effect for generating spin polarization from charge current in systems without inversion symmetry. It also addresses the orbital Edelstein effect (current-induced orbital magnetization) in bilayer systems with Rashba interaction. The study provides detailed calculations of magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of electric field, including dependencies on spin-orbit coupling strength and Fermi velocity. Contains explicit graphics showing magnetization vs. electric field curves.

## 3. **Spin and Orbital Edelstein Effect in Spin-Orbit Coupled Noncentrosymmetric Superconductor**
- **arxivID:** `2408.08151`
- **Authors:** Satoshi Ando, Yukio Tanaka, Mario Cuoco, Luca Chirolli, Maria Teresa Mercaldo
- **Date:** 2024-08-15
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08151v2
- **Summary:** This recent paper (2024) examines the relationship between spin and orbital properties of Edelstein effects in conventional spin-singlet noncentrosymmetric superconductors. It provides theoretical framework for calculating current-induced magnetization, including both spin and orbital contributions. The work includes analysis of how magnetization depends on spin-orbit coupling strength, Fermi velocity, and chirality of the system. Contains explicit graphical representations of magnetization magnitude and direction for different electric field configurations.
