

# Relevant Papers for Edelstein Effect in Rashba Fermion Systems

## 1. Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models
- **arXivID:** 2503.20712
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** This paper investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. It analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, considering both isotropic and anisotropic Rashba models. The study explicitly examines how the effect depends on Fermi velocity, chirality, and other model parameters. Contains analytical expressions and graphical representations showing magnetization magnitude and direction as functions of applied electric field. Ideal for calculating Edelstein effect at the Gamma point with parameter dependencies.

## 2. Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect
- **arXivID:** 1506.08330
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Summary:** This foundational paper provides the theoretical framework for current-driven spin polarization in two-dimensional electron gases with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. It covers both linear response regime and nonlinear extensions. The work derives expressions for spin polarization induced by electric fields, including dependence on Fermi velocity and Rashba coupling strength. Contains explicit formulas for magnetization magnitude and direction that can be applied to Rashba fermions at the Gamma point.

## 3. Spin accumulation at nonmagnetic interface induced by direct Rashba Edelstein effect
- **arXivID:** 1805.05523
- **Authors:** Florent Auvray, Jorge Puebla, Mingran Xu, Bivas Rana, Daisuke Hashizume, Yoshichika Otani
- **Date:** 2018-05-15
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.05523v1
- **Summary:** This paper directly addresses the direct Rashba Edelstein effect, explaining how electrons moving in an electric field experience momentum-dependent magnetic fields that couple to electron spin. It provides theoretical calculations for spin polarization generation from charge current in Rashba systems. The work includes analysis of how spin accumulation depends on electric field direction and magnitude, with explicit consideration of Rashba coupling parameters. Contains graphical representations showing magnetization behavior under different conditions.

## Key Theoretical Framework for Calculation

Based on these papers, the Edelstein effect for a Rashba fermion at the Gamma point can be calculated using:

### Rashba Hamiltonian at Gamma Point:
$$H = \frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2m^*} + \alpha_R (\sigma_x k_y - \sigma_y k_x)$$

### Spin Polarization (Edelstein Effect):
$$\langle \sigma \rangle = \frac{e \tau \alpha_R}{\hbar} \mathbf{E} \times \hat{z}$$

### Magnetization Magnitude:
$$M = \frac{e \tau \alpha_R n}{\hbar} |\mathbf{E}|$$

Where:
- $\alpha_R$ = Rashba coupling strength
- $\tau$ = scattering time
- $n$ = carrier density
- $\mathbf{E}$ = applied electric field
- Fermi velocity $v_F = \hbar k_F/m^*$ affects the magnitude

### Parameter Dependencies:
- **Chirality:** Determines spin texture direction (clockwise/counter-clockwise)
- **Fermi velocity:** Scales the magnitude of spin polarization
- **Electric field direction:** Magnetization direction follows $\mathbf{E} \times \hat{z}$
- **Electric field magnitude:** Linear relationship in weak field regime

These three papers provide comprehensive theoretical foundations, analytical expressions, and graphical examples necessary for building a complete model of the Edelstein effect in Rashba fermion systems.
