Units of the Formulas for the Edelstein Effect in Rashba Fermions

The units of the formulas for the Edelstein effect in Rashba fermions are as follows:

1. **Magnetization (M):**
   $$ \mathbf{M} = \chi \mathbf{E} \times \hat{z} $$
   - **Units of M:** A/m (Amperes per meter).
   - **Units of χ:** S (Siemens).
   - **Units of E:** V/m (Volts per meter).

2. **Proportionality Factor:**
   $$ |\mathbf{M}| \propto \frac{\alpha_R e \tau}{\hbar^2} |\mathbf{E}| $$
   - **Units of α_R:** J·m (Joule·meter).
   - **Units of e:** C (Coulombs).
   - **Units of τ:** s (seconds).
   - **Units of ħ:** J·s (Joule·seconds).
   - **Units of E:** V/m (Volts per meter).

The units are consistent when the Edelstein susceptibility (χ) is expressed in Siemens (S), ensuring that the magnetization (M) has the correct units of A/m when multiplied by the electric field (E) in V/m.
