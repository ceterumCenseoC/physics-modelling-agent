

# Edelstein Effect Research Papers for Rashba Fermion Models

## Top 3 Most Relevant Sources

### 1. Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models
- **arxivID:** `2503.20712`
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** This paper directly investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. The authors analyze the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account anisotropic Rashba models. This work is highly relevant for calculating the Edelstein effect at the Gamma point and understanding how magnetization depends on electric field direction, magnitude, and model parameters including chirality and Fermi velocity.

### 2. Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect
- **arxivID:** `1506.08330`
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Summary:** This theoretical work examines how a current driven through a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling induces spin polarization perpendicular to the current (Edelstein effect). The paper extends beyond linear response regime and provides analytical expressions for spin polarization as a function of drift velocity, which is directly related to applied electric field. This is essential for computing magnetization magnitude and direction dependencies on electric field parameters.

### 3. Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction
- **arxivID:** `2307.02872`
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** This paper investigates both spin Edelstein effect and orbital Edelstein effect in systems with Rashba interaction and broken inversion symmetry. The authors provide theoretical framework for current-induced spin polarization and orbital magnetization, including dependencies on system parameters. This is valuable for understanding how chirality and Fermi velocity affect the Edelstein effect response.

## Key Parameters for Model Building

Based on these sources, the Edelstein effect for Rashba fermions at the Gamma point depends on:

$$ \mathbf{M} = \chi \mathbf{E} \times \hat{z} $$

Where:
- **$\mathbf{M}$** = Induced magnetization/spin polarization
- **$\mathbf{E}$** = Applied electric field
- **$\chi$** = Edelstein susceptibility tensor
- **$\hat{z}$** = Direction perpendicular to 2D plane

### Critical Model Parameters:
1. **Rashba coupling strength ($\alpha_R$)** - Determines spin splitting magnitude
2. **Fermi velocity ($v_F$)** - Affects carrier response to electric field
3. **Chirality** - Determines spin texture winding direction
4. **Fermi energy ($E_F$)** - Sets carrier density and available states
5. **Scattering time ($\tau$)** - Affects relaxation and magnitude of effect

The magnetization magnitude scales approximately as:

$$ |\mathbf{M}| \propto \frac{\alpha_R e \tau}{\hbar^2} |\mathbf{E}| $$

The direction follows the spin-momentum locking characteristic of Rashba systems, perpendicular to both the current direction and the structural inversion asymmetry axis.
