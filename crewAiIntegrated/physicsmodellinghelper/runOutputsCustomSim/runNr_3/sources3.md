

# Relevant Papers on Edelstein-Effect for Rashba Fermion Modeling

## 1. Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models
- **arXiv ID:** 2503.20712
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** Investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. Analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. Studies how this effect depends on the effective mass, Rashba coupling strength, and Fermi energy. This paper is highly relevant for calculating magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of applied electric field and model parameters.

## 2. Theory of the nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein effect
- **arXiv ID:** 1506.08330
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** 2015-06-27
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Summary:** Theoretical framework for the Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. Discusses how current drives spin polarization in the perpendicular direction. Extends beyond linear response regime to nonlinear effects, providing analytical expressions that relate spin polarization to electric field strength, Fermi velocity, and Rashba coupling parameter. Essential for understanding parameter dependencies.

## 3. Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction
- **arXiv ID:** 2307.02872
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** Explores both spin Edelstein effect and current-induced orbital magnetization in systems with broken inversion symmetry. Provides theoretical treatment of how spin polarization and orbital magnetization depend on Rashba coupling, chirality, and Fermi level. Discusses the relationship between applied electric field direction and resulting magnetization orientation, crucial for modeling different field directions.

## 4. Boltzmann theory of the inverse Edelstein effect in a two-dimensional Rashba gas
- **arXiv ID:** 2601.02473
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2026-01-05
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.02473v1
- **Summary:** Derives analytical expressions for charge and spin currents within a semiclassical Boltzmann framework for a Rashba 2D electron gas. Analyzes dependence on Rashba coupling strength, Fermi velocity, and scattering time. While focused on inverse Edelstein effect, the formalism provides fundamental relationships applicable to direct Edelstein effect calculations.

## 5. Edelstein effects, spin-transfer torque, and spin pumping caused by pristine surface states of topological insulators
- **arXiv ID:** 1901.06953
- **Authors:** Wei Chen
- **Date:** 2019-01-21
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.06953v2
- **Summary:** Investigates Edelstein effect using a semiclassical approach for gapless Dirac cone systems. Examines combined effects of random impurity scattering and spin-momentum locking. Provides analytical results for current-induced surface spin polarization as a function of electric field, Fermi energy, and scattering parameters. Relevant for understanding parameter dependencies at the Gamma point.

### Key Parameters for Model Building ($\Gamma$-point Rashba Fermion):

| Parameter | Symbol | Effect on Edelstein Magnetization |
|-----------|--------|-----------------------------------|
| Rashba coupling strength | $\alpha_R$ | Linear scaling of spin polarization |
| Fermi velocity | $v_F$ | Inverse relationship with magnetization magnitude |
| Fermi energy | $E_F$ | Determines carrier density and response |
| Electric field | $\vec{E}$ | Direction determines magnetization orientation ($\vec{M} \perp \vec{E}$) |
| Chirality | $\chi = \pm 1$ | Determines sign of spin polarization |
| Scattering time | $\tau$ | Linear dependence in Boltzmann regime |

The magnetization direction follows: $$\vec{M} \propto \alpha_R \tau (\vec{E} \times \hat{z})$$

where $\hat{z}$ is the growth direction perpendicular to the 2D plane.
