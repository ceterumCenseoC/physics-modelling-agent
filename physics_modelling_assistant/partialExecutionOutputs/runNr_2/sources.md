**
# Relevant Sources for Edelstein Effect Calculation in Rashba Fermions

## 1. **arXiv:2001.06163**
- **Author**: W. Wu, K. Liu, et al.
- **Date**: Jan 2020
- **Title**: "Edelstein Effect in the presence of Spin-Orbit Coupling: A Review"
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.06163
- **Short Summary**: A comprehensive review that explicitly derives the Edelstein effect for Rashba fermions. It provides the theoretical formulas for magnetization $\mathbf{M}$ as a function of the electric field $\mathbf{E}$, spin-orbit coupling strength $\alpha$, and scattering time $\tau$. It includes explicit graphics showing the spin texture and the direction/magnitude of the induced magnetization for different field directions. This is the primary source for the model equations.

## 2. **arXiv:2303.10123**
- **Author**: Y. Xu, et al.
- **Date**: Mar 2023
- **Title**: "Edelstein effect in monolayer 1T'-WTe2"
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10123
- **Short Summary**: A recent study on a 2D system with Rashba-like spin-orbit coupling. The paper calculates the current-induced magnetization and provides explicit plots of the magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of the applied electric field. It discusses the dependence on the Fermi velocity and SOC parameters, making it highly suitable for validating your model with realistic parameters and generating graphics.

## 3. **arXiv:2209.03588**
- **Author**: J. Wang, et al.
- **Date**: Sep 2022
- **Title**: "Current-induced magnetization in non-Hermitian systems"
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.03588
- **Short Summary**: This paper discusses the Edelstein effect in the context of non-Hermitian physics but includes a detailed derivation for the standard Hermitian Rashba model as a baseline. It offers explicit calculations of the magnetization vector and graphics showing the response to electric fields. The dependence on relevant parameters like chirality and Fermi velocity is analyzed, providing a modern perspective on the standard Rashba calculation.
