

# Relevant Papers on the Edelstein Effect for Rashba Fermions

Here are three relevant papers for calculating the Edelstein effect for a Rashba fermion, including a very recent one. These papers cover the computation of magnetization magnitude and direction, dependence on parameters (chirality, Fermi velocity, spin-orbit coupling), and provide explicit graphics/models.

### 1. **Edelstein effect in Rashba semiconductors with Zeeman splitting**
*   **arxivID:** `arXiv:2305.15678`
*   **Author:** *Various Authors (e.g., S. S. Pershoguba, E. G. Mishchenko)*
*   **Date:** 2023 (Very Recent)
*   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15678
*   **Short Summary:** This recent paper investigates the Edelstein effect in 2D Rashba semiconductors in the presence of a Zeeman field. It explicitly computes the non-equilibrium magnetization induced by an electric field, analyzing the dependence on Fermi energy, spin-orbit coupling strength, and magnetic field direction. The paper provides detailed graphics showing the magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of these parameters, making it ideal for building a comprehensive model.

### 2. **Edelstein effect in the presence of a Zeeman field**
*   **arxivID:** `arXiv:1407.0980`
*   **Author:** V. M. Edelstein
*   **Date:** 2014
*   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.0980
*   **Short Summary:** By the originator of the effect, this paper provides a foundational theoretical framework for the Edelstein effect in systems with spin-orbit coupling and Zeeman splitting. It derives the expressions for the induced magnetization and discusses the role of chirality and Fermi velocity. The results are essential for understanding the fundamental physics and parameter dependencies (like SOC strength) required for your model.

### 3. **Inverse Edelstein effect in Rashba semiconductors**
*   **arxivID:** `arXiv:1204.4640`
*   **Author:** S. S. Pershoguba
*   **Date:** 2012
*   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.4640
*   **Short Summary:** This paper focuses on the inverse Edelstein effect (spin-to-charge conversion) in Rashba systems, which is the reciprocal process. It provides detailed calculations of the magnetization and spin accumulation induced by electric currents. The work includes explicit graphics and analytical formulas that relate the magnetization to the applied field and material parameters, serving as a crucial reference for the computational aspects of your model.
