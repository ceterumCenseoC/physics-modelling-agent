

# Relevant Papers on the Edelstein Effect in Rashba Systems

1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
   - **arxivID**: 2503.20712
   - **Author**: Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
   - **Date**: 2025-03-26
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20712
   - **Short Summary**: *Very recent.* Investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. Analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an anisotropic Rashba model. Explicitly studies how the effect depends on the electric field magnitude/direction, spin-orbit coupling strength, and other model parameters, providing direct theoretical frameworks and graphics for modeling the Gamma point behavior.

2. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
   - **arxivID**: 2307.02872
   - **Author**: Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
   - **Date**: 2023-07-06
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02872
   - **Short Summary**: Explores both spin and orbital Edelstein effects in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction. Discusses how charge currents induce spin and orbital magnetization in systems with broken inversion symmetry. Provides valuable insights into the theoretical calculation of magnetization direction and magnitude as a function of Rashba coupling strength and applied electric fields.

3. **Enhanced Edelstein effect and interdimensional effects in an electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling interface**
   - **arxivID**: 1912.01804
   - **Author**: A. C. Zulkoskey, R. Dick, K. Tanaka
   - **Date**: 2019-12-04
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01804
   - **Short Summary**: Examines bound-state and free-state contributions to the density of states in a 3D electron gas with a 2D interface featuring Rashba spin-orbit coupling. Analyzes the enhanced Edelstein effect and confinement effects, offering a foundational quantum mechanical model for calculating induced magnetization in Rashba systems and its dependence on interface parameters and electric fields.
