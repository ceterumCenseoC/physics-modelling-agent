

# Relevant Sources for Calculating the Edelstein Effect in Rashba Fermions

- **arxivID**: `2503.20712`
- **Author**: Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **date**: 2025-03-26
- **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20712
- **short summary**: *This very recent paper investigates the direct Edelstein effect in 2D Rashba electron gases using a semiclassical Boltzmann approach. It explicitly calculates the induced magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of the applied electric field, analyzing both isotropic and anisotropic Rashba models. The study provides explicit graphics demonstrating how the Edelstein magnetization depends on key model parameters, including spin-orbit coupling strength, Fermi velocity, and effective mass, making it highly suitable for building the requested computational model.*

- **arxivID**: `2307.02872`
- **Author**: Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **date**: 2023-07-06
- **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02872
- **short summary**: *This work explores both the spin and orbital Edelstein effects in bilayer systems featuring Rashba interaction. It offers a detailed theoretical framework for computing current-induced magnetization, distinguishing between spin and orbital contributions. The authors analyze how the magnetization vector's magnitude and direction evolve with varying electric field strengths and spin-orbit coupling parameters, providing crucial insights into the microscopic origins of the effect near the Gamma point.*

- **arxivID**: `1912.01804`
- **Author**: A. C. Zulkoskey, R. Dick, K. Tanaka
- **date**: 2019-12-04
- **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01804
- **short summary**: *This paper examines the enhanced Edelstein effect and interdimensional phenomena in a three-dimensional electron gas confined by a two-dimensional Rashba interface. It derives analytical and numerical expressions for the induced spin polarization and magnetization arising from an applied electric field. The study explicitly details the dependence of the Edelstein effect on the Rashba spin-orbit coupling strength, Fermi velocity, and confinement potential, offering valuable baseline equations for modeling the effect at the band edge.*
