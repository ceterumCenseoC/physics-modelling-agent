

# Relevant Papers on Edelstein Effect for Rashba Fermions

## 1. Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models
- **arXivID:** 2503.20712
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** 2025-03-26
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** This is the most recent paper directly addressing the Edelstein effect in 2D Rashba electron gas using semiclassical Boltzmann approach. The authors analyze magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, considering anisotropic Rashba models. The paper explicitly studies how the effect depends on effective parameters and should contain graphics showing magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of electric field. This paper is particularly relevant as it directly addresses the calculation of Edelstein effect for Rashba fermions with parameter dependence analysis.

## 2. Nonlinear spin and orbital Edelstein effect in WTe2
- **arXivID:** 2412.02938
- **Authors:** Xing-Guo Ye, Peng-Fei Zhu, Wen-Zheng Xu, Tong-Yang Zhao, Zhi-Min Liao
- **Date:** 2024-12-04
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.02938v1
- **Summary:** This recent paper investigates current-induced spin polarization in materials with Rashba states. It discusses how the Edelstein effect depends linearly on applied current and explores nonlinear contributions. The paper should provide explicit graphics showing magnetization magnitude and direction for different electric field configurations, making it highly relevant for understanding the parameter dependence of the Edelstein effect in Rashba systems.

## 3. Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction
- **arXivID:** 2307.02872
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** 2023-07-06
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** This paper focuses on the spin Edelstein effect in systems with broken inversion symmetry and Rashba interaction. It examines current-induced orbital magnetization (orbital Edelstein effect) in bilayer systems. The study should provide insights into how magnetization depends on spin-orbit coupling strength, Fermi velocity, and electric field direction, with explicit graphics showing these dependencies. This paper complements the other two by providing additional theoretical framework for Rashba-based Edelstein effect calculations.

---

**Note:** These three papers provide comprehensive coverage of the Edelstein effect in Rashba systems, with the first being the most recent (2025) and directly addressing isotropic and anisotropic Rashba models. All three papers should contain the necessary theoretical framework and graphics to build a model for calculating magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of electric field parameters and model parameters like spin-orbit coupling strength and Fermi velocity.
