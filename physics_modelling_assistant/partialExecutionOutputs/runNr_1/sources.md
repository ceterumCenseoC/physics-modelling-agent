

# Edelstein Effect in Rashba Fermion Systems: Key Research Sources

## **Selected Papers for Edelstein Effect Modeling**

### 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models** (Most Recent)
- **arXiv ID:** `2503.20712`
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** March 26, 2025
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** This paper directly investigates spin-to-charge conversion via the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. It analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, taking into account an **anisotropic Rashba model**. The study explicitly examines how the effect depends on effective parameters including **Fermi velocity, spin-orbit coupling strength, and chirality**. The paper includes **explicit graphics** showing magnetization magnitude and direction as a function of applied electric field direction and magnitude, making it ideal for building a quantitative model.

### 2. **Spin and Orbital Edelstein Effect in a Bilayer System with Rashba Interaction**
- **arXiv ID:** `2307.02872`
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Date:** July 6, 2023
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** This work provides a comprehensive analysis of the **spin Edelstein effect** in systems with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. It discusses the generation of spin polarization from charge currents and the relationship between spin and orbital magnetization. The paper includes detailed calculations of **magnetization magnitude and direction** for different electric field configurations, with explicit consideration of **Rashba coupling strength** and Fermi surface properties. Contains **numerical results and graphics** showing parameter dependencies.

### 3. **Nonlinear Spin and Orbital Edelstein Effect in WTe2** (Very Recent)
- **arXiv ID:** `2412.02938`
- **Authors:** Xing-Guo Ye, Peng-Fei Zhu, Wen-Zheng Xu, Tong-Yang Zhao, Zhi-Min Liao
- **Date:** December 4, 2024
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.02938v1
- **Summary:** This recent paper examines the Edelstein effect in materials with **Rashba states** and topological surface states. It addresses both linear and nonlinear responses to applied electric fields, with explicit discussion of **magnetization direction and magnitude** as a function of current direction. The paper includes **experimental and theoretical graphics** showing how the effect depends on **Fermi energy, spin-orbit coupling strength, and field orientation**. Provides valuable insights for modeling parameter dependencies in Rashba systems.
