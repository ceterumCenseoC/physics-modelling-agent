

# Relevant Sources for Edelstein Effect in Rashba Fermions

- **arxivID**: 2503.20712
  - **Author**: Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
  - **Date**: 2025-03-26
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20712
  - **Short Summary**: This very recent paper investigates the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using the semiclassical Boltzmann approach. It explicitly analyzes the magnetization arising from the direct Edelstein effect, studying its dependence on effective parameters such as the Fermi velocity and spin-orbit coupling strength. The authors distinguish between isotropic and anisotropic Rashba models, providing a theoretical framework for calculating magnetization magnitude and direction under applied electric fields, which directly addresses the requirements for building a model of the Rashba fermion at the Gamma point.

- **arxivID**: 2602.02036
  - **Author**: Alfonso Maiellaro, Francesco Romeo, Mattia Trama, Jacopo Settino, Claudio Guarcello, Carmine Antonio Perroni, Pawel Wójcik, Bartłomiej Szafran, Daniela Stornaiuolo, Marco Salluzzo, Thomas Sand Jespersen, Nicolas Bergeal, Manuel Bibes, Roberta Citro
  - **Date**: 2026-02-02
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02036
  - **Short Summary**: This extremely recent study focuses on spin-resolved transport in a ballistic quantum dot with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. It examines the charge-to-spin conversion (Edelstein effect) and spin Hall effect. The paper is highly relevant for understanding the generation of spin polarization (magnetization) in confined Rashba systems and discusses the crossover regimes, offering insights into the dependence of the effect on system parameters and providing explicit graphics of the spin response.

- **arxivID**: 2307.02872
  - **Author**: Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
  - **Date**: 2023-07-06
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02872
  - **Short Summary**: This paper explores both the spin and orbital Edelstein effects in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction. It predicts current-induced orbital magnetization alongside spin polarization. The work is useful for calculating magnetization magnitudes and understanding how the Edelstein effect manifests in multi-layer Rashba systems, considering the interplay between spin-orbit coupling and electric fields. It provides a comprehensive view of the magnetization components generated by the electric field.
