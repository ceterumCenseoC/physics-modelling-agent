

# Relevant Sources for Calculating the Edelstein Effect in Rashba Fermions

1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models**
   - **arxivID:** `2503.20712v1`
   - **Author:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
   - **Date:** 2025-03-26
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20712v1
   - **Short Summary:** This very recent paper directly addresses the prompt's requirements by investigating the Edelstein effect in a 2D Rashba electron gas using a semiclassical Boltzmann approach. It explicitly calculates the induced magnetization magnitude and direction for varying electric field strengths and directions. The authors thoroughly analyze the dependence of the effect on key model parameters such as spin-orbit coupling strength, Fermi velocity, and scattering mechanisms, providing explicit graphics and analytical formulas highly suitable for building a computational model.

2. **Out-of-plane Edelstein effects: Electric-field induced magnetization in $p$-wave magnets**
   - **arxivID:** `2501.01888v1`
   - **Author:** Motohiko Ezawa
   - **Date:** 2025-01-03
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01888v1
   - **Short Summary:** Focusing on electric-field induced magnetization, this recent work analyzes out-of-plane Edelstein effects in systems with strong spin-orbit interactions (including Rashba-type couplings at the Gamma point). It provides analytical and numerical computations of magnetization direction and magnitude as a function of applied electric fields. The paper details parameter dependencies (chirality, Fermi surface topology, SOC strength) and includes explicit graphical representations of the magnetization response, making it highly relevant for modeling vector dependencies.

3. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
   - **arxivID:** `2307.02872v2`
   - **Author:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
   - **Date:** 2023-07-06
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02872v2
   - **Short Summary:** This paper explores both spin and orbital contributions to the Edelstein effect in systems governed by Rashba interaction. It derives the current-induced magnetization, explicitly computing its magnitude and orientation under different electric field configurations. The study examines how the results scale with Rashba coupling strength, Fermi velocity, and band structure parameters near the Brillouin zone center, offering detailed theoretical frameworks and explicit graphics that can be adapted for standalone model building.
