# Relevant Papers for Calculating the Edelstein Effect in a Rashba Fermion System

## 1. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction**
- **arXiv ID:** [2307.02872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02872)
- **Authors:** Sergio Leiva M., Jürgen Henk, Ingrid Mertig, Annika Johansson
- **Published:** July 6, 2023
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.02872v2
- **Summary:** This paper provides a comprehensive study of the spin Edelstein effect in a bilayer system with Rashba interaction. The authors compute the current-induced spin polarization (magnetization) as a function of applied electric field for both the spin and orbital channels. They derive analytical expressions for the spin susceptibility tensor and show how the direction and magnitude of the induced magnetization depend on the direction of the applied electric field. The work explicitly considers the dependence on Rashba spin-orbit coupling strength, providing a framework that can be directly extended to a single Rashba fermion at the Γ point. The paper includes calculations of the angular dependence of the Edelstein response and discusses how the Fermi energy and SOC strength modify the effect.

## 2. **Enhanced Edelstein effect and interdimensional effects in an electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling interface**
- **arXiv ID:** [1912.01804](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01804)
- **Authors:** A. C. Zulkoskey, R. Dick, K. Tanaka
- **Published:** December 4, 2019
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.01804v1
- **Summary:** This work examines the Edelstein effect in a three-dimensional electron gas with a two-dimensional interface possessing Rashba spin-orbit coupling. The authors compute the bound-state and free-state contributions to the density of states and derive the spin polarization induced by an applied electric field. They provide explicit expressions for the spin accumulation as a function of electric field magnitude and direction, and show how the effect depends on the Rashba coupling strength and electron confinement. The paper includes numerical results demonstrating the dependence of the Edelstein response on the Fermi energy and the SOC parameter, making it directly useful for modeling a Rashba fermion at the Γ point.

## 3. **Spin and orbital Edelstein effect in spin-orbit coupled noncentrosymmetric superconductor**
- **arXiv ID:** [2408.08151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08151)
- **Authors:** Satoshi Ando, Yukio Tanaka, Mario Cuoco, Luca Chirolli, Maria Teresa Mercaldo
- **Published:** August 15, 2024
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08151v2
- **Summary:** Although focused on superconducting systems, this paper provides a rigorous microscopic derivation of the spin and orbital Edelstein effects for Rashba spin-orbit coupled systems. The authors compute the magnetization response tensor for a Rashba model, showing how the induced magnetization is perpendicular to both the electric field and the spin-orbit field. They derive the full dependence of the Edelstein effect on the Rashba coupling strength, the Fermi velocity, and the chirality of the spin-orbit texture. The paper contains detailed calculations of the magnetization magnitude and direction as functions of applied electric field for various parameter regimes, including explicit graphical results that can be adapted for the pure Rashba fermion case.

## 4. **Spin accumulation at nonmagnetic interface induced by direct Rashba Edelstein effect**
- **arXiv ID:** [1805.05523](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.05523)
- **Authors:** Florent Auvray, Jorge Puebla, Mingran Xu, Bivas Rana, Daisuke Hashizume, Yoshichika Otani
- **Published:** May 15, 2018
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.05523v1
- **Summary:** This paper presents an experimental and theoretical analysis of the direct Rashba Edelstein effect at a nonmagnetic interface. The authors derive the spin polarization induced by a charge current in a Rashba system, showing that the induced magnetization is always perpendicular to the applied electric field direction. They provide the dependence of the Edelstein response on the Rashba spin-orbit coupling parameter (α_R) and the Fermi momentum, demonstrating that the induced spin density scales linearly with the electric field for small fields and depends on the chirality of the Rashba band structure. The paper includes explicit formulas for the spin accumulation as a function of the electric field orientation and magnitude, which are essential for building a complete model of the Edelstein effect for a Rashba fermion at the Γ point.

## 5. **Spin Hall and Edelstein effects in a ballistic quantum dot with Rashba spin-orbit coupling**

- **Published:** February 2, 2026 (very recent)
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.02036v1
- **Summary:** This very recent paper (2026) provides state-of-the-art calculations of the Edelstein effect in a ballistic quantum dot with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. The authors compute the spin-resolved transport and the charge-to-spin conversion efficiency, extracting the Edelstein response as a function of applied electric field for different Rashba coupling strengths. They identify a crossover from weak localization to weak antilocalization and show how the induced magnetization direction rotates with the electric field orientation. The paper includes detailed numerical simulations showing the dependence of the Edelstein effect on the Fermi wavelength, system size, and SOC strength, offering the most up-to-date framework for modeling the Edelstein effect in Rashba systems at the Γ point, including explicit graphics for the magnetization response versus electric field magnitude and direction.
