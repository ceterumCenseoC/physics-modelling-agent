## 1. **Edelstein Effect in Isotropic and Anisotropic Rashba Models** (Most Recent — 2025)
- **arXiv ID:** 2503.20712
- **Authors:** Irene Gaiardoni, Mattia Trama, Alfonso Maiellaro, Claudio Guarcello, Francesco Romeo, Roberta Citro
- **Date:** March 26, 2025
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.20712v1
- **Summary:** This paper provides a comprehensive semiclassical Boltzmann treatment of the Edelstein effect (spin-to-charge conversion) in a 2D Rashba electron gas. The authors compute the magnetization induced by an applied electric field for both isotropic and anisotropic Rashba models, and analyze how the effect depends on key model parameters such as the spin-orbit coupling strength, chirality, and Fermi surface anisotropy. They derive explicit expressions for the field-induced spin polarization and discuss its magnitude and direction as a function of the applied field. This paper is directly relevant for computing the magnetization as a function of electric field magnitude and direction, and for generating the required graphics.

## 2. **Theory of the Nonlinear Rashba-Edelstein Effect**
- **arXiv ID:** 1506.08330
- **Authors:** Giovanni Vignale, I. V. Tokatly
- **Date:** June 27, 2015
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08330v1
- **Summary:** This foundational paper extends the linear-response Edelstein effect to the nonlinear regime in a 2D electron gas with Rashba spin-orbit coupling. The authors derive the field-induced spin polarization beyond linear response, showing that at finite drift velocities, the magnetization acquires corrections that depend on the magnitude of the applied electric field. They provide explicit analytical results for the nonlinear magnetization, showing that its direction and magnitude depend nontrivially on the field strength and the Rashba coupling parameter (α). This paper is essential for calculating the magnetization magnitude and direction for different magnitudes of the applied electric field, and for understanding how the result scales with the spin-orbit coupling strength.

## 3. **Enhanced Edelstein Effect and Interdimensional Effects in an Electron Gas with Rashba Spin-Orbit Coupling Interface**
- **arXiv ID:** 1912.01804
- **Authors:** A. C. Zulkoskey, R. Dick, K. Tanaka
- **Date:** December 4, 2019
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.01804v1
- **Summary:** This work examines the Edelstein effect in a three-dimensional electron gas with a 2D Rashba interface, analyzing both bound-state and free-state contributions to the density of states. The authors compute the electric-field-induced magnetization in this quasi-2D system and demonstrate that the effect is enhanced compared to the pure 2D case. They show how the magnetization depends on the confinement potential and the Rashba coupling parameter, providing insight into how the effect varies with the model parameters. This is useful for understanding how confinement and dimensionality affect the Edelstein response, and for connecting the Rashba fermion model at the Γ point to realistic interface systems.

## 4. **Theory of Spin Pumping and Inverse Rashba-Edelstein Effect in a Two-Dimensional Electron Gas**
- **arXiv ID:** 2409.14267
- **Authors:** Masaki Yama, Mamoru Matsuo, Takeo Kato
- **Date:** September 21, 2024 (v2)
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.14267v2
- **Summary:** This recent paper (2024) investigates spin transport in a junction of a ferromagnetic insulator and a 2DEG with both Rashba and Dresselhaus spin-orbit interactions. The authors present a theoretical framework for the inverse Rashba-Edelstein effect and spin pumping, providing detailed calculations of the spin-charge conversion efficiency. The paper includes derivations of the spin accumulation and charge current induced by spin pumping, and discusses how the Rashba coupling strength and the chirality of the spin-orbit field affect the response. While focused on the inverse effect, the formalism and parameter dependence analysis are directly transferable to the direct Edelstein effect calculation, particularly for understanding chirality and spin-orbit coupling strength dependence.

## 5. **Electric-Dipole Spin Resonances**
- **arXiv ID:** 1812.01721
- **Authors:** E. I. Rashba, V. I. Sheka
- **Date:** November 29, 2018
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.01721v1
- **Summary:** This paper by the discoverer of the Rashba effect provides a comprehensive review of electric-dipole-induced spin phenomena, including the Edelstein effect. The authors discuss the microscopic origin of the Rashba spin-orbit coupling and derive the spin polarization induced by an electric field in Rashba systems. The paper gives the Hamiltonian for Rashba fermions at the Γ point, discusses the spin texture of the Fermi surface, and derives the linear response of the spin density to an applied electric field. It provides the essential theoretical background for understanding how the magnetization arises from the spin-momentum locking, and how the magnitude depends on the Fermi velocity and the spin-orbit coupling constant.
