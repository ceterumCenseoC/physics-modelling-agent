

# Relevant Sources for Hatsugai-Kohmoto Model Scattering Rate Research

Here are the 4 most relevant papers for investigating the temperature dependence of the scattering rate phase space integral in the Hatsugai-Kohmoto (HK) model, particularly in the strong correlation limit. The list includes a very recent paper from 2025.

### 1. Electrical Transport in the Hatsugai-Kohmoto Model
*   **arxivID:** `2407.00156v1`
*   **Author:** Daniele Guerci, Giorgio Sangiovanni, Andrew J. Millis, Michele Fabrizio
*   **Date:** 2024-06-28
*   **URL:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00156](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00156)
*   **Short Summary:** This is the most critical source for the requested problem. It explicitly addresses electrical transport properties in the HK model. The authors demonstrate that standard Kubo formulas fail to reproduce correct thermodynamic susceptibilities and transport coefficients in HK-type models due to their specific momentum-space interactions. This paper provides essential insights into how scattering rates and transport phenomena must be calculated correctly in this non-trivial interacting system, warning against standard perturbative assumptions.

### 2. Twisting the Hubbard model into the Momentum-Mixing Hatsugai-Kohmoto Model
*   **arxivID:** `2401.08746v5`
*   **Author:** Peizhi Mai, Jinchao Zhao, Gaurav Tenkila, Nico A. Hackner, Dhruv Kush, Derek Pan, Philip W. Phillips
*   **Date:** 2024-01-16
*   **URL:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.08746](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.08746)
*   **Short Summary:** This paper explores the theoretical mapping between the standard Hubbard model and the momentum-mixing Hatsugai-Kohmoto model. It provides detailed context on the interaction terms and the specific "momentum-mixing" nature of the HK Hamiltonian, which is crucial for understanding the phase space integrals involved in the scattering processes described in the problem setup.

### 3. Solving the SU($N$) Orbital Hatsugai-Kohmoto Model
*   **arxivID:** `2503.10760v3`
*   **Author:** Nico A. Hackner, Peizhi Mai, Philip W. Phillips
*   **Date:** 2025-03-13
*   **URL:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10760)
*   **Short Summary:** As a very recent paper (March 2025), this work presents an advanced approach to solving the SU($N$) orbital extension of the HK model. It introduces techniques for realistically simulating the physics of the SU($N$) Hubbard model using the orbital HK framework. The exact solution methods and cluster approximations developed here are highly valuable for constructing accurate models of the scattering phase space in the strong coupling limit ($U \gg W$).

### 4. Chaotic-Integrable Transition for Disordered Orbital Hatsugai-Kohmoto Model
*   **arxivID:** `2411.08496v2`
*   **Author:** Ying-Lin Li, Chen-Te Ma, Po-Yao Chang
*   **Date:** 2024-11-13
*   **URL:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08496)
*   **Short Summary:** This paper investigates the transition between chaotic and integrable behaviors in disordered orbital HK models. It draws connections to the Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev (SYK) model and analyzes spectral statistics (such as the spectral form factor). Understanding these spectral properties is important for validating the density of states assumptions used in the energy phase space integrals of the scattering rate.
