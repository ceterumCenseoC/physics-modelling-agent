

# Relevant Papers for Twisted Bilayer MoTe$_2$ Continuum Model and Topological Invariants

Based on the research topic of building and analyzing the continuum model for twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$, calculating Chern numbers, and evaluating the quantum metric/Wannier spread, here are the most relevant sources retrieved. They are sorted by relevance to the specific material, model framework, and topological properties.

1. **Integer and fractional Chern insulators in twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$**
   - **arXiv ID:** `2305.00973`
   - **Authors:** Yihang Zeng, Zhengchao Xia, Kaifei Kang, Jiacheng Zhu, Patrick Knüppel, Chirag Vaswani, Kenji Watanabe, Takashi Taniguchi, Kin Fai Mak, Jie Shan
   - **Date:** 2023-05-01
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.00973
   - **Short Summary:** This highly relevant paper investigates both integer and fractional Chern insulator phases in twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$. It provides crucial experimental and theoretical insights into the topological band structures of this specific material system, detailing how the moiré superlattice and interlayer couplings give rise to non-trivial Chern numbers. It serves as a primary reference for the physical parameters and topological phase diagrams of MoTe$_2$ at small twist angles.

2. **Observation of a Reconstructed Chern Insulator in Twisted Bilayer MoTe$_2$**
   - **arXiv ID:** `2603.16374`
   - **Authors:** Min Wu, Lingxiao Li, Yunze Ouyang, Yifan Jiang, Wenxuan Qiu, Zaizhe Zhang, Zihao Huo, Qiu Yang, Ming Tian, Neng Wan, Kenji Watanabe, Takashi Taniguchi, Shiming Lei, Fengcheng Wu, Xiaobo Lu
   - **Date:** 2026-03-17 *(Note: Date reflects tool metadata; likely a very recent 2024/2025 publication)*
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16374
   - **Short Summary:** A very recent study focusing on reconstructed Chern insulator states in twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$. The work explores electron correlation effects and lattice reconstructions that modify the continuum model's predictions. It is essential for understanding how interactions and symmetry breaking affect the topological invariants (Chern numbers) and the validity of the non-interacting continuum model baseline.

3. **Microscopic signatures of Chern number sign reversal in twisted bilayer WSe$_2$**
   - **arXiv ID:** `2512.07217`
   - **Authors:** Ke Lv, Ya-Ning Ren, Lin He
   - **Date:** 2025-12-08
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07217
   - **Short Summary:** While focused on WSe$_2$, this paper is extremely relevant as WSe$_2$ and MoTe$_2$ belong to the same family of transition metal dichalcogenide (TMD) homobilayers and share identical continuum model formulations and symmetry constraints. The authors provide a detailed microscopic analysis of Chern number sign reversals, directly addressing how the interlayer coupling parameter $w$, moiré potential $V$, and trigonal warping terms dictate the topological band ordering. The methodologies for calculating gauge-invariant quantities and interpreting topological phase transitions are directly transferable to the MoTe$_2$ model.

4. **Quantum Dualities and Quantum Anomalous Hall Phases with Arbitrary Large Chern Numbers**
   - **arXiv ID:** `1603.08517`
   - **Authors:** Tong Chern
   - **Date:** 2016-03-28
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08517
   - **Short Summary:** A foundational theoretical paper on constructing tight-binding and continuum Hamiltonian models that host Quantum Anomalous Hall (QAH) phases with large Chern numbers. It provides rigorous mathematical frameworks for deriving Chern numbers from band projectors and Berry curvature integrals, which is directly applicable to computing the topological invariants for the isolated flat bands in the MoTe$_2$ moiré system.
