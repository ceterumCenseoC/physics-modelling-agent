

# Relevant Papers on Fefferman-Graham Ambient Metrics and Obstruction Tensors

1. **arxivID:** 2510.21646  
   **Author:** Marc Mars, Gabriel Sánchez-Pérez  
   **Date:** 2024-10-24  
   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21646  
   **Short Summary:** A very recent study investigating the asymptotic structure of the Fefferman-Graham ambient metric. It proves that every straight ambient metric admits a conformal completion with a well-defined null infinity and relates the asymptotic expansion of the metric at infinity to that at the homothetic hypersurface. Highly relevant for modern treatments of ambient metric expansions and pole structures.

2. **arxivID:** math/0408229  
   **Author:** A. Rod Gover, Lawrence J. Peterson  
   **Date:** 2004-08-18  
   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408229  
   **Short Summary:** Focuses explicitly on the (Fefferman-Graham) ambient obstruction tensor, a conformally invariant symmetric trace-free 2-tensor on even-dimensional manifolds. It details the conformal deformation complex and provides foundational insights into the properties and residues of obstruction tensors, directly applicable to the problem's core concepts.

3. **arxivID:** 1609.02371  
   **Author:** Ian M Anderson, Thomas Leistner, Andree Lischewski, Pawel Nurowski  
   **Date:** 2016-09-08  
   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.02371  
   **Short Summary:** Examines conformal Walker metrics and the linear Fefferman-Graham equations. It discusses the embedding of manifolds with conformal structures into pseudo-Riemannian ambient manifolds and the order-by-order solution of the Ricci-flatness condition, which is crucial for deriving higher-order expansion coefficients and analyzing the tensor relations.

4. **arxivID:** 1608.06875  
   **Author:** Matthias Hammerl, Katja Sagerschnig, Josef Šilhan, Arman Taghavi-Chabert, Vojtěch Žádník  
   **Date:** 2016-08-24  
   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.06875  
   **Short Summary:** Demonstrates that Patterson-Walker metrics admit a global and explicit Fefferman-Graham ambient metric. This provides a concrete class of conformal structures to test and verify the behavior of metric expansions and pole structures at even dimensions, useful for checking the coefficients for $k=2,3$.

5. **arxivID:** 0707.1737  
   **Author:** Ruben Manvelyan, Karapet Mkrtchyan, Ruben Mkrtchyan  
   **Date:** 2007-07-12  
   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.1737  
   **Short Summary:** Re-derives conformally invariant powers of the Laplacian using the $(d+2)$-dimensional Fefferman-Graham ambient space approach. It connects "holographic" anomalies to the ambient metric expansion coefficients, offering physical context for the residues and obstruction tensors in the expansion series.

6. **arxivID:** 0904.0186  
   **Author:** Thomas Leistner, Pawel Nurowski  
   **Date:** 2009-04-01  
   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.0186  
   **Short Summary:** Presents conformal structures in signature (3,2) with exceptional Lie group holonomy ambient metrics. It explicitly constructs the metrics and discusses their conformal properties, serving as a valuable reference for explicit calculations and verifying tensor proportionalities in ambient geometry.
