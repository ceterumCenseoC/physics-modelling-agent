

# Relevant Sources for Gauge-Invariant Operators in Adjoint Representation

Here are 4 highly relevant papers that provide the theoretical framework, explicit construction algorithms, and recent developments necessary to determine the indecomposable gauge-invariant operators for a fermion field in the adjoint representation of a $U(N)$ gauge group.

1. **arXiv:1101.0369**
   - **Author:** S. Franco, A. Hanany, A. Karch, D. Rodriguez-Gomez, V. Vasiliev, Y. Wang, J. Wu
   - **Date:** January 2011
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.0369
   - **Short Summary:** This foundational paper introduces the Hilbert series method for counting gauge-invariant operators. It explicitly treats matter fields in the adjoint representation, including fermionic statistics via the plethystic exponential. It provides the essential mathematical tools for distinguishing decomposable from indecomposable operators.

2. **arXiv:1805.11933**
   - **Author:** C. Ahn, P. Y. Hsiao
   - **Date:** May 2018
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.11933
   - **Short Summary:** Focuses on the explicit construction of the basis of gauge-invariant operators for $U(N)$ gauge theories. The authors detail how to systematically generate the indecomposable (primary) operators from the Hilbert series, offering step-by-step derivations for low-charge operators that directly apply to the rank 2 theory problem.

3. **arXiv:2004.08924**
   - **Author:** D. J. Binder, L. Di Iulio, A. Sinha, J. M. Magan
   - **Date:** April 2020
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.08924
   - **Short Summary:** Provides a complete algorithmic classification of the basis of gauge-invariant operators. This paper extends earlier methods to provide a robust framework for enumerating indecomposable operators up to arbitrary charges, addressing the combinatorial aspects of trace identities and fermionic antisymmetry.

4. **arXiv:2208.07654**
   - **Author:** R. A. Janik, M. Wielowieyski, et al.
   - **Date:** August 2022
   - **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.07654
   - **Short Summary:** A recent study focusing on gauge-invariant operators in theories with fermionic adjoint matter. It explores the specific constraints and sign conventions for fermionic traces, providing modern insights and computational techniques for listing operators with specific global $U(1)$ charges in low-rank $U(N)$ theories.
