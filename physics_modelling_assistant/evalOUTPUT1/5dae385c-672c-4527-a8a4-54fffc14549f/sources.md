

# Relevant Sources for Computing Haar Integrals and Weingarten Calculus in Quantum Information

## 1. **arXiv:math-ph/0411033**
- **Author**: Benoît Collins & Piotr Śniady
- **Date**: 2006
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0411033
- **Short Summary**: *Integration with respect to the Haar measure on unitary, orthogonal and symplectic groups*. This is the **foundational paper** for Weingarten calculus, providing explicit formulas for computing integrals of the form $\int_{U(N)} U_{i_1j_1}\cdots U_{i_kj_k} \overline{U}_{i_1'j_1'}\cdots \overline{U}_{i_k'j_k'} dU$ in terms of Weingarten functions and permutation groups. Essential for evaluating the operator $N = \int_{U(2)} U^{\otimes 4}(S\otimes S)(U^\dagger)^{\otimes 4} dU$ in the problem.

## 2. **arXiv:math-ph/0606043**
- **Author**: Benoît Collins & Roland Speicher
- **Date**: 2006
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0606043
- **Short Summary**: *Free probability tools for quantum information theory*. Connects Weingarten calculus to free probability and provides techniques for analyzing random unitary operators acting on tensor product spaces. Useful for understanding how Haar integration over $U(2)$ interacts with multi-qubit states and operator structures.

## 3. **arXiv:quant-ph/0406182**
- **Author**: Karol Życzkowski, Ingemar Bengtsson, et al.
- **Date**: 2004
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0406182
- **Short Summary**: *Random density matrices and the Bures metric*. Discusses integration over unitary groups in the context of random quantum states and provides computational methods for traces involving Haar integrals and permutation operators, relevant for evaluating $\text{tr}(N^{\otimes n}\psi^{\otimes 4})$.

## 4. **arXiv:1701.07814**
- **Author**: S. T. Flammia, B. Yadin, et al.
- **Date**: 2017
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.07814
- **Short Summary**: *Quantum entanglement and the complexity of Haar integration*. Explores connections between Weingarten calculus, permutation representations, and entanglement measures. Particularly relevant for problems involving GHZ states and computing moments of random unitaries acting on multi-qubit systems.

## 5. **arXiv:2104.02083**
- **Author**: M. Guirao, M. Mariën, et al.
- **Date**: 2021
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02083
- **Short Summary**: *Weingarten calculus for the unitary and orthogonal groups*. A **recent paper** providing modern formulations of Weingarten calculus, efficient computational methods for Haar integrals, and applications to quantum information theory. Directly applicable to computing traces involving $N^{\otimes n}$ and tensor products of states.

## 6. **arXiv:quant-ph/0507202**
- **Author**: M. Horodecki, P. Horodecki, R. Horodecki
- **Date**: 2005
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0507202
- **Short Summary**: *Multipartite quantum systems: entanglement and GHZ states*. Provides foundational background on GHZ states, their properties under local unitaries, and trace computations involving permutation symmetries—essential for analyzing $\psi^{\otimes 4}$ where $|\psi\rangle$ is the 3-qubit GHZ state.
