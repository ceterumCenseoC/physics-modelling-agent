

# Relevant Sources for Minimal Permutation Factorizations

The following papers are relevant to the problem of computing configurations of permutations under minimal decomposition constraints (where $d(g) = d(g_1) + d(g_2)$). They cover the theory of minimal factorizations of the n-cycle, transposition distances, and recent algorithmic approaches.

1.  **Trajectories in random minimal transposition factorizations**
    *   **arxivID:** 1810.07586
    *   **Author:** Valentin Féray, Igor Kortchemski
    *   **Date:** 2018-10-17
    *   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.07586
    *   **Short Summary:** This paper is highly relevant as it explicitly studies random minimal factorizations of the n-cycle into $n-1$ transpositions. It provides context on the structure and properties of geodesic factorizations in the symmetric group, which corresponds to the minimal decomposition condition $d(g) = d(g_1) + d(g_2)$ used in the problem.

2.  **Factorizations of permutations into star transpositions**
    *   **arxivID:** math/0610640
    *   **Author:** John Irving, Amarpreet Rattan
    *   **Date:** 2006-10-21
    *   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0610640
    *   **Short Summary:** The authors give a compact expression for the number of factorizations of any permutation into a minimal number of transpositions of the form $(1, i)$. This work generalizes results on counting minimal decompositions, which is central to calculating the number of configurations $a_n(t)$.

3.  **Permutations with non-decreasing transposition array and pattern avoidance**
    *   **arxivID:** 2111.11527
    *   **Author:** Fufa Beyene, Roberto Mantaci
    *   **Date:** 2021-11-22
    *   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.11527
    *   **Short Summary:** This paper discusses bijections associating permutations with "transposition arrays," which represent the decomposition of a permutation as a product of transpositions. It identifies specific subsets of permutations based on these decompositions, offering combinatorial insights relevant to the splitting/merging process.

4.  **Plane permutations and applications to a result of Zagier-Stanley and distances of permutations**
    *   **arxivID:** 1502.07674
    *   **Author:** Ricky X. F. Chen, Christian M. Reidys
    *   **Date:** 2015-02-26
    *   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.07674
    *   **Short Summary:** The authors introduce plane permutations and discuss distances of permutations. The concept of distance relates directly to $d(g)$, the minimal number of transpositions, providing theoretical background on the metric space of permutations under transposition generation.

5.  **Multiset permutation generation by transpositions**
    *   **arxivID:** 2309.11781
    *   **Author:** Hans Jakob Rivertz
    *   **Date:** 2023-09-21
    *   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.11781
    *   **Short Summary:** A very recent paper (2023) that proposes a new algorithm for generating permutations using transpositions. While focused on generation algorithms, it reinforces the relevance of transposition-based operations and constraints in current permutation research.
