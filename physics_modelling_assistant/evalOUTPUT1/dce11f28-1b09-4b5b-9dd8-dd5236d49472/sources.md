

# Relevant Papers on PXP Model and Quantum Many-Body Scars

## 1. Exact Quantum Many-Body Scar States in the Rydberg-Blockaded Atom Chain
- **arXiv ID**: 1810.00888
- **Author**: Cheng-Ju Lin, Olexei I. Motrunich
- **Date**: 2018-10-01
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00888
- **Short Summary**: This is the foundational paper for understanding scars in the PXP model. It identifies the exact algebraic structure of the scar states and provides the necessary framework to calculate their energies and overlaps with the $Z_2$ state ($|1010...\rangle$), which is essential for solving the specific problem regarding the subspace $\mathcal{D}_0^+$.

## 2. New exact eigenstates in the Lesanovsky model, proximity to integrability and the PXP model, and approximate scar states
- **arXiv ID**: 1911.11305
- **Author**: Daniel K. Mark, Cheng-Ju Lin, Olexei I. Motrunich
- **Date**: 2019-11-26
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.11305
- **Short Summary**: This paper extends the analysis of exact eigenstates in models related to the PXP model (specifically the Lesanovsky model), providing deeper insight into the nature of approximate scar states and their properties in the thermodynamic limit.

## 3. Stability of quantum many-body scars on PXP model
- **arXiv ID**: 2407.14691
- **Author**: Alessandra Chioquetta, Raphael Campos Drumond
- **Date**: 2024-07-19
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14691
- **Short Summary**: A very recent study (2024) that investigates the stability of quantum many-body scars in the PXP model under various perturbations. It analyzes fidelity and correlation revivals, offering current perspectives on the robustness of these states.

## 4. Unraveling PXP Many-Body Scars through Floquet Dynamics
- **arXiv ID**: 2312.16288
- **Author**: Giuliano Giudici, Federica Maria Surace, Hannes Pichler
- **Date**: 2023-12-26
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.16288
- **Short Summary**: This recent paper investigates the origin of PXP scars by analyzing their behavior under periodic (Floquet) driving, providing a modern dynamical perspective on characterizing these non-thermal eigenstates.

## 5. Driving quantum many-body scars in the PXP model
- **arXiv ID**: 2204.13718
- **Author**: Ana Hudomal, Jean-Yves Desaules, Bhaskar Mukherjee, Guo-Xian Su, Jad C. Halimeh, Zlatko Papić
- **Date**: 2022-04-28
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.13718
- **Short Summary**: Explores the engineering of quantum many-body scars in the PXP model via periodic driving, relevant for understanding how external fields can manipulate the overlap and energy spectrum of scar states.
