

The following list contains 5 relevant arXiv papers that cover the necessary theoretical framework for solving the problem: the One-Axis Twisting (OAT) dynamics, the effects of specific dissipation terms (dephasing and spin-flips), the semi-classical limit for large $N$ ($10^6$), and recent optimizations.

**1. Spin Squeezing of One-Axis Twisting Model in The Presence of Phase Dephasing**
*   **arXiv ID**: 1312.3743
*   **Author**: C. G. Ji, Y. C. Liu, G. R. Jin
*   **Date**: 2013-12-13
*   **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.3743
*   **Short Summary**: This paper directly addresses the OAT Hamiltonian combined with the specific $\hat{L}_{z,j}$ dephasing term mentioned in the problem. It provides analytical results for the optimal initial state and squeezing limits in the presence of many-body phase dephasing.

**2. Spin squeezing of the non-Hermitian one-axis twisting model**
*   **arXiv ID**: 1504.01984
*   **Author**: Ling-Na Wu, Guang-Ri Jin, Li You
*   **Date**: 2015-04-08
*   **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.01984
*   **Short Summary**: This work investigates spin squeezing in the OAT model under non-Hermitian dynamics, which corresponds to the conditional evolution of an open system with dissipation (spin-flips/decay). It is crucial for modeling the $\hat{L}_{+,j}$ and $\hat{L}_{-,j}$ terms.

**3. Revisiting the impact of dissipation on time-reversed one-axis-twist quantum-sensing protocols**
*   **arXiv ID**: 2309.02291
*   **Author**: Martin Koppenhöfer, A. A. Clerk
*   **Date**: 2023-09-05
*   **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.02291
*   **Short Summary**: A very recent paper (2023) that analyzes the impact of general dissipation on OAT quantum sensing protocols. It provides modern context and bounds for squeezing under noisy conditions.

**4. Semiclassical Lindblad master equation for spin dynamics**
*   **arXiv ID**: 2101.10700
*   **Author**: J. Dubois, Ulf Saalmann, Jan M. Rost
*   **Date**: 2021-01-26
*   **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.10700
*   **Short Summary**: This paper is essential for handling the large particle number ($N=10^6$) specified in the problem. It derives the semi-classical limit (Fokker-Planck equation) for the Lindblad master equation of spin systems, allowing for analytical or efficient numerical solutions for the time evolution of the moments.

**5. Dynamical spin squeezing: combining fast one-axis twisting and deep two-axis counter-twisting**
*   **arXiv ID**: 1409.0683
*   **Author**: Tomas Opatrny
*   **Date**: 2014-09-02
*   **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0683
*   **Short Summary**: This paper provides detailed analysis of the dynamical evolution of the squeezing parameter under the OAT Hamiltonian, helping to determine the optimal time $t$ for squeezing before dissipation dominates.
