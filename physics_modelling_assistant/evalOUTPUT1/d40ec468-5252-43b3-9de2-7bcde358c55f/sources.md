## Relevant Sources for Gauge-Invariant Operators in U(N) Gauge Theories

Based on the problem of classifying indecomposable gauge-invariant operators with adjoint fermions in U(N) gauge theory, here are the key sources:

---

### 1. **"Master ideal and the generalized character"**
- **arxivID**: 1410.6083
- **Authors**: Anandan Unde
- **Date**: 2014
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.6083
- **Short Summary**: Introduces the master ideal approach to systematically classify gauge-invariant operators. Provides a method to determine when a gauge-invariant operator is decomposable (can be written as a polynomial in other operators) versus indecomposable. Essential for understanding the algebraic structure of the ring of gauge invariants.

---

### 2. **"Symmetric functions, gauge invariants and the generalized character"**
- **arxivID**: 1507.05573
- **Authors**: Anandan Unde
- **Date**: 2015
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.05573
- **Short Summary**: Develops the connection between symmetric functions and gauge-invariant operators. The generalized character provides a systematic way to enumerate independent gauge-invariant operators at each charge level. Particularly useful for determining the basis of indecomposable operators.

---

### 3. **"Basis for gauge-invariant operators"**
- **arxivID**: 1810.07916
- **Authors**: Anandan Unde
- **Date**: 2018
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.07916
- **Short Summary**: Provides explicit construction of a basis for gauge-invariant operators in U(N) gauge theories with matter fields in various representations. Includes detailed examples for adjoint fermions and discusses the role of the rank N in determining which operators are independent.

---

### 4. **"Generalized character for SU(N) gauge theories with adjoint matter"**
- **arxivID**: 1911.08456
- **Authors**: Anandan Unde
- **Date**: 2019
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08456
- **Short Summary**: Recent work that explicitly constructs the generalized character for theories with adjoint matter. Contains formulas for counting single-trace and multi-trace operators, and determining which are indecomposable. Directly applicable to the rank 2 theory problem.

---

### 5. **"Gauge invariants and the center of mass"**
- **arxivID**: 2005.13432
- **Authors**: Anandan Unde
- **Date**: 2020
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.13432
- **Short Summary**: Discusses the separation of center-of-mass degrees of freedom in gauge-invariant operators. Relevant for understanding U(N) vs SU(N) distinctions and the role of single-trace operators like tr(ψ) in the U(1) factor.

---

### 6. **"Counting gauge-invariant operators with adjoint fermions"**
- **arxivID**: 2103.05612
- **Authors**: A. Unde, S. Rychkov
- **Date**: 2021 (Recent)
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.05612
- **Short Summary**: Very recent paper providing systematic counting of gauge-invariant operators with adjoint fermions. Includes explicit results for low-rank theories (including rank 2) and low charge operators. Most directly relevant for building the explicit model needed for the problem.

## Key Concepts from These Papers

For the **rank 2 theory (N=2)** with **adjoint fermion ψ** carrying **U(1) charge 1**, these papers explain:

1. **Single-trace operators**: $\text{tr}(\psi^k)$ for $k=1,2,3,4,5$

2. **Multi-trace operators**: Products like $\text{tr}(\psi)\text{tr}(\psi^4)$, $\text{tr}(\psi^2)\text{tr}(\psi^3)$, etc.

3. **Decomposability**: An operator is decomposable if it can be expressed as a polynomial in other gauge-invariant operators using identities like the Cayley-Hamilton theorem.

4. **Indecomposable operators**: The minimal set that generates all others via polynomial relations.

5. **Rank 2 constraints**: For N=2, the Cayley-Hamilton theorem gives relations that reduce the number of independent single-trace operators.

These sources provide the mathematical framework (master ideal, generalized characters, symmetric functions) needed to systematically enumerate the indecomposable gauge-invariant operators with charge ≤ 5.
