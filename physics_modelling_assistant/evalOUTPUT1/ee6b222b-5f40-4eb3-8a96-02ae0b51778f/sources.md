

# Relevant Sources for Permutation Factorizations and Minimal Decompositions

Here are the relevant papers found for the problem concerning minimal decompositions of permutations and counting configurations in the symmetric group $S_n$.

1. **Zero Excess and Minimal Length in Finite Coxeter Groups**
   - **arxivID**: 1405.2700
   - **Author**: Sarah B. Hart, Peter J. Rowley
   - **Date**: 2014-05-12
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.2700v1
   - **Short Summary**: This paper defines the "excess" of a factorization in a Coxeter group. It is highly relevant because the problem's condition $d(g) = d(g_1) + d(g_2)$ corresponds to a factorization with zero excess (minimal length). The paper analyzes the structure of such minimal factorizations.

2. **Weighted Hurwitz numbers and topological recursion: an overview**
   - **arxivID**: 1610.09408
   - **Author**: A. Alexandrov, G. Chapuy, B. Eynard, J. Harnad
   - **Date**: 2016-10-28
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.09408v6
   - **Short Summary**: This overview connects weighted Hurwitz numbers to the enumeration of factorizations of permutations (specifically the long cycle) into transpositions. Since minimal factorizations of the long cycle $(12\dots n)$ correspond to trees (Cayley's formula), this paper provides the theoretical framework for counting such configurations.

3. **Factorizations of permutations into star transpositions**
   - **arxivID**: 0610640
   - **Author**: John Irving, Amarpreet Rattan
   - **Date**: 2006-10-21
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0610640v2
   - **Short Summary**: This paper gives a compact expression for the number of factorizations of a permutation into a minimal number of transpositions of the form $(1 i)$. It provides explicit counting results for minimal decompositions, which is directly applicable to calculating $a_n(t)$.

4. **Multiset permutation generation by transpositions**
   - **arxivID**: 2309.11781
   - **Author**: Hans Jakob Rivertz
   - **Date**: 2023-09-21
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.11781v1
   - **Short Summary**: This is a very recent paper (satisfying the recency constraint) that discusses algorithms for generating permutations using transpositions. While focused on generation, it deals with the mechanics of transpositions in $S_n$ and constraints on their application.
