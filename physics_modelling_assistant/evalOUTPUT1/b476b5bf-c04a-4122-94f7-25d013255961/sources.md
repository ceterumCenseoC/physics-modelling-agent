

# Relevant Sources for Kitaev Honeycomb Model Research

- **arxivID**: 0904.2771
**Author**: Alexei Kitaev, Chris Laumann
**Date**: 2009-04-20
**url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0904.2771v1
**short summary**: Comprehensive lecture notes by the original model's author that pedagogically cover the exact solution of the 2D Kitaev honeycomb model. It details the mapping to Majorana fermions and $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge fluxes, explaining the emergence of exponential ground state degeneracy ($4^N$) and the rigorous definition of flux-free versus vison sectors. Essential for deriving how periodic boundary conditions on finite Bravais lattices split or preserve these degeneracies.

- **arxivID**: 2109.13883
**Author**: Tatiana A. Bespalova, Oleksandr Kyriienko
**Date**: 2021-09-28
**url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.13883v1
**short summary**: A recent paper focusing on quantum simulation and ground state preparation for the honeycomb Kitaev model. It explicitly addresses finite-size lattice constructions and periodic boundary conditions, detailing the symmetries required to stabilize the flux-free sector and providing exact protocols to isolate the degenerate ground states on small lattices. Highly relevant for computing precise finite-size ground state energies.

- **arxivID**: 0806.1588
**Author**: G. Baskaran, Diptiman Sen, R. Shankar
**Date**: 2008-06-10
**url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0806.1588v2
**short summary**: Investigates the ground state properties, exact correlation functions, and degeneracy scaling of the Kitaev model. It analyzes the geometry of the minimum energy surface and demonstrates how lattice topology and boundary constraints dictate the number of accessible flux sectors, providing necessary theoretical backing for small-system exact diagonalization results.
