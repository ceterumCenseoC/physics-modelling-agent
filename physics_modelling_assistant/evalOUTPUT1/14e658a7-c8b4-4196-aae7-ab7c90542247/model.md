**
The tunneling energy $t$ and contact interaction $U$ are given by:

$$
t = \frac{4}{\sqrt{\pi}} E_R \left( \frac{\alpha E^2}{E_R} \right)^{3/4} \exp\left( -2 \sqrt{\frac{\alpha E^2}{E_R}} \right)
$$

$$
U = \frac{\sqrt{2} \hbar^2 a_s}{\sqrt{\pi} m l_{\text{osc}}^2 l_z} = \frac{8\pi^2 \hbar^2 a_s}{\sqrt{\pi} m \lambda^2} \sqrt{\frac{\alpha E^2}{E_R}} \frac{1}{l_z}
$$

where:
- $E_R = \frac{2\pi^2 \hbar^2}{m \lambda^2}$ is the recoil energy,
- $V_0 = \alpha E^2$ is the potential depth,
- $l_{\text{osc}} = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi} \left( \frac{E_R}{2V_0} \right)^{1/4}$ is the harmonic oscillator length in the lattice plane,
- $l_z$ is the harmonic oscillator length characterizing the confinement in the $z$-direction.
