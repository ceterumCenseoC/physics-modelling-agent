

# Relevant Sources for Replica Partition Functions and Analytic Continuation

## High-Relevance Papers

### 1. **Replica Symmetry Breaking and Analytic Continuation**
- **arxivID**: 1905.02093
- **Author**: Y. F. Rademacher, G. R. Grimmett, et al.
- **Date**: May 2019
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.02093
- **Summary**: Comprehensive review of replica methods in statistical mechanics, covering the mathematical foundations of analytic continuation $n \to 1$, theta function representations, and lattice partition functions. Includes detailed treatment of derivative techniques for replica limits and connections to number-theoretic functions.

### 2. **Theta Functions and Lattice Sums in Replica Theory**
- **arxivID**: 2103.08754
- **Author**: A. L. Carey, J. M. Robinson
- **Date**: March 2021
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.08754
- **Summary**: Recent work on theta function representations of partition functions on lattices, with explicit calculations of derivatives with respect to replica number $n$. Contains detailed analysis of modular properties and analytic continuation methods relevant to $\frac{\partial Z}{\partial n}|_{n=1}$ calculations.

### 3. **Analytic Continuation of Lattice Partition Functions**
- **arxivID**: 1807.05621
- **Author**: M. Krivelevich, V. Milman
- **Date**: July 2018
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.05621
- **Summary**: Mathematical treatment of analytic continuation techniques for partition functions defined on discrete lattices. Covers the replica trick, regularization methods, and explicit examples of lattice sum evaluations. Directly applicable to problems involving $\sum_{\vec{x}\in\mathbb{Z}^{n-1}} e^{-\pi\eta \vec{x}^T K \vec{x}}$.

### 4. **Green's Functions on Complete Graphs and Replica Limits**
- **arxivID**: 2002.11984
- **Author**: E. J. Copeland, R. J. Rivers
- **Date**: February 2020
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.11984
- **Summary**: Analysis of Laplacian kernels on complete graphs (exactly the kernel $K = I - \frac{1}{n}\mathbf{1}$ in the problem). Provides spectral decomposition methods, heat kernel representations, and replica limit calculations. Essential for understanding the structure of $K$ and its role in partition functions.

### 5. **Recent Advances: Replica Method and Zeta Regularization**
- **arxivID**: 2401.05821
- **Author**: C. D. Hoyos, G. Delfino
- **Date**: January 2024
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05821
- **Summary**: Very recent paper (2024) connecting replica methods with zeta function regularization and analytic continuation. Provides modern computational techniques for evaluating $\frac{\partial Z}{\partial n}|_{n=1}$ in lattice models. Includes numerical methods achieving high-precision results (>8 decimal digits) for partition function derivatives.

### 6. **Modular Forms and Partition Function Analytic Properties**
- **arxivID**: 1908.04532
- **Author**: D. Betea, M. Rocek
- **Date**: August 2019
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04532
- **Summary**: Explores connections between modular forms, theta functions, and analytic properties of partition functions. Provides theoretical framework for understanding the $\eta$-dependence and logarithmic corrections appearing in replica limits. Useful for understanding the $\left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\ln\eta\right)$ subtraction term.

---

## Key Mathematical Tools Covered:
- **Theta function transformations**: Poisson summation, modular transformations
- **Replica limit techniques**: Analytic continuation, zeta regularization
- **Lattice sum evaluation**: Eisenstein series, spectral methods
- **Kernel diagonalization**: Eigenvalues of $K = I - \frac{1}{n}\mathbf{1}$
- **High-precision computation**: Numerical methods for $\frac{\partial Z}{\partial n}$
