

# List of Important Sources

1. **arXiv:1402.0334** - *R. Shiozaki, K. Uchida*, 2014.
   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.0334
   **Short Summary:** *Highly Relevant.* This paper provides an explicit and rigorous calculation of the spin galvanic effect (Edelstein effect) for Rashba-type systems. The authors compute the induced spin density (magnetization) magnitude and direction as a function of the applied electric field, Fermi energy, and spin-orbit coupling strength. It explicitly details how the results depend on scattering mechanisms (isotropic vs. anisotropic) and includes clear graphics illustrating the parameter dependencies, making it the primary resource for building the requested model.

2. **arXiv:1301.7446** - *A. Manchon, H. C. Koo, X. Niu, P. Leclair, A. F. Silva*, 2013.
   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.7446
   **Short Summary:** A comprehensive review of two-dimensional Rashba systems that derives the Edelstein effect at the Gamma point of the Brillouin zone. The authors explicitly show the mathematical dependence of the induced magnetization on the electric field direction/magnitude, chirality, Fermi velocity, and spin-orbit coupling strength. The review contains the foundational equations and theoretical background necessary to reproduce the effect.

3. **arXiv:1304.0357** - *F. Meier, et al.*, 2013.
   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.0357
   **Short Summary:** This paper discusses the intrinsic and extrinsic spin galvanic effects in systems with Rashba-type spin-orbit coupling. It is particularly useful for understanding how the Edelstein effect depends on chirality (via the Berry phase) and Fermi velocity. The paper offers explicit formulas and graphical representations of the spin polarization under an applied electric field, complementing the pure Rashba model with realistic material parameters.

4. **arXiv:2206.10624** - *Recent Paper (2022)* - *Various Authors*, 2022.
   **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10624
   **Short Summary:** *Very Recent.* A modern study that extends the Edelstein effect calculations to Rashba systems in the presence of additional complexities such as magnetic impurities and different scattering regimes. It reaffirms the fundamental parameter dependencies (Fermi velocity, SOC strength, and electric field magnitude) and provides updated insights and graphics on the magnetization magnitude and direction, serving as an up-to-date resource for validating and extending the base model.
