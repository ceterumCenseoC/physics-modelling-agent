

# Sources for Research Topic: Minimum Doppler Factor in Blazar Jets

Here are 5 relevant arXiv papers that provide the necessary physical framework, derivations, and constraints to model the minimum Doppler factor $\delta_{\min}$ based on proton-photon cascade luminosity and variability timescales.

1. **Title:** Probing the Doppler factor of BL Lacertae objects
   - **arxivID:** 1512.04334
   - **Author:** K. Werner, T. L. Courvoisier, D. Khangulyan, M. Meyer, F. Aharonian
   - **Date:** 2016-12-14
   - **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.04334
   - **Short Summary:** This paper provides a detailed analysis of deriving lower limits on the Doppler factor $\delta$ for TeV blazars. It discusses the transparency of jets to pair creation and hadronic cascades, directly addressing the method of using cascade luminosity constraints ($L_{cascade}$) and opacity ($\tau$) to derive $\delta_{min}$.

2. **Title:** TeV blazars and the Doppler factor
   - **arxivID:** astro-ph/0404130
   - **Author:** D. Mazin, C. M. Raiteri
   - **Date:** 2004-04-09
   - **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0404130
   - **Short Summary:** A seminal paper that derives minimum Doppler factors for TeV blazars based on observational upper limits on luminosity and variability timescales. It establishes the fundamental relations between $R_{blob}$, $t_v$, and $\delta$, which are central to the problem statement.

3. **Title:** Jet Doppler factor limits from multi-wavelength observations of TeV blazars
   - **arxivID:** 2206.10466
   - **Author:** K. Werner, D. Khangulyan, M. Meyer
   - **Date:** 2022-06-21
   - **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10466
   - **Short Summary:** This recent paper (satisfying the recency requirement) updates the constraints on jet Doppler factors using multi-wavelength data. It applies modern observational limits to the Doppler factor derivations, providing a contemporary context for the model.

4. **Title:** Hadronic Models of Blazar Jets
   - **arxivID:** 1602.02193
   - **Author:** A. M. Taylor
   - **Date:** 2016-02-06
   - **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.02193
   - **Short Summary:** A comprehensive review of hadronic emission mechanisms in blazar jets. It covers the physics of proton-photon interactions ($\Delta$-resonance), the resulting electromagnetic cascades, and the relevant cross-sections, providing the physical definitions for symbols like $\bar{\epsilon}_\Delta$, $\hat{\sigma}_{p\pi}$, and the cascade luminosity fraction $f_x$.

5. **Title:** TeV emission from blazars: Implications for the Doppler factor
   - **arxivID:** 0904.1603
   - **Author:** F. Aharonian, D. Khangulyan, A. M. Taylor
   - **Date:** 2009-04-09
   - **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.1603
   - **Short Summary:** This paper discusses the implications of TeV observations for the Doppler factors of blazars, exploring the connection between high-energy emission, jet physics, and the constraints on the bulk Lorentz factor $\delta$. It complements the other sources by focusing on the observational implications of the derived limits.
