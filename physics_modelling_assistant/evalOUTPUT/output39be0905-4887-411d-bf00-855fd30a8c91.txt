$$ \Lambda = \frac{k_- \lambda^+ + k_+ \lambda^-}{k_+ + k_-} + \mathcal{O}\left(\frac{\sigma^4}{\bar v_b^4}\right) $$

**Explanation:**
*   **$\beta$**: The parameter $\beta$ governs the cell-size control strategy (e.g., timer vs. sizer). While $\beta$ significantly shapes the steady-state distribution of cell sizes, the exponential population growth rate $\Lambda$ is robust to this variation and independent of $\beta$ to first order due to the decoupling of size homeostasis from biomass accumulation.
*   **$\sigma^2$**: The division noise $\sigma^2$ introduces variability in cell cycle durations. However, for small noise, these fluctuations average out across the population. The first-order correction to $\Lambda$ due to $\sigma^2$ is zero, meaning the population-level fitness depends only on the average growth rate dynamics, not the division threshold noise.
