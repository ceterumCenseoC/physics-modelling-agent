

# Relevant Papers for Twisted Bilayer MoTe$_2$ Continuum Model and Topology

1. **Interplay between topology and correlations in the second moiré band of twisted bilayer MoTe2**
   - **arxivID**: 2406.09687
   - **Author**: Fan Xu, Xumin Chang, Jiayong Xiao, Yixin Zhang, Feng Liu, Zheng Sun, Ning Mao, Nikolai Peshcherenko, Jiayi Li, Kenji Watanabe, Takashi Taniguchi, Bingbing Tong, Li Lu, Jinfeng Jia, Dong Qian, Zhiwen Shi, Yang Zhang, Xiaoxue Liu, Shengwei Jiang, Tingxin Li
   - **Date**: 2024-06-14
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.09687v2
   - **Short Summary**: This paper investigates the interplay between topology and electronic correlations in the moiré bands of twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$. It is highly relevant for determining Chern numbers and understanding the topological phases (such as fractional topological insulators) that arise in the second moiré band, which directly relates to the problem of calculating Chern numbers for the top bands.

2. **Transfer learning relaxation, electronic structure and continuum model for twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$**
   - **arxivID**: 2311.07533
   - **Author**: Ning Mao, Cheng Xu, Jiangxu Li, Ting Bao, Peitao Liu, Yong Xu, Claudia Felser, Liang Fu, Yang Zhang
   - **Date**: 2023-11-13
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.07533v4
   - **Short Summary**: This work develops a continuum model for twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$, accounting for lattice relaxation effects. It provides the theoretical framework and parameters (like hopping $w$ and potential $V$) necessary to construct the Hamiltonian and calculate topological invariants like Chern numbers and the quantum metric, making it essential for the continuum model approach described in the problem.

3. **Twist-angle evolution from valley-polarized fractional topological phases to valley-degenerate superconductivity in twisted bilayer MoTe2**
   - **arxivID**: 2603.16412
   - **Author**: Zheng Sun, Fan Xu, Jiayi Li, Yifan Jiang, Jingjing Gao, Cheng Xu, Tongtong Jia, Kehao Cheng, Jinyang Zhang, Wanghao Tian, Kenji Watanabe, Takashi Taniguchi, Jinfeng Jia, Shengwei Jiang, Yang Zhang, Yuanbo Zhang, Shiming Lei, Xiaoxue Liu, Tingxin Li
   - **Date**: 2026-03-17
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.16412v1
   - **Short Summary**: This paper explores the twist-angle dependent evolution of topological phases in twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$. It discusses valley-polarized fractional topological phases and superconductivity, providing insights into the Chern numbers and topological properties of the moiré bands at various twist angles, including those near $3.5^\circ$.
