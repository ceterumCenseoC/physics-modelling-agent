The torsional oscillation frequency $\omega_t$ and coupling strength $g$ are derived as:

$$ \omega_t = \sqrt{ \frac{15 P_0 (\alpha_{\parallel} - \alpha_{\perp})}{\pi^2 c w_0^2 \rho a b^2 (a^2 + b^2)} } $$

$$ g = \frac{15 P_0 \alpha_{\parallel}^2}{8 \pi^3 c w_0^2 \rho a b^2 (a^2 + b^2) R^3 \omega_t} $$

where the polarizabilities are defined by:
$$ \alpha_{\parallel} = \frac{4}{3}\pi a b^2 \epsilon_0 \frac{\epsilon_r - 1}{1 + L_{\parallel} (\epsilon_r - 1)}, \quad \alpha_{\perp} = \frac{4}{3}\pi a b^2 \epsilon_0 \frac{\epsilon_r - 1}{1 + L_{\perp} (\epsilon_r - 1)} $$
