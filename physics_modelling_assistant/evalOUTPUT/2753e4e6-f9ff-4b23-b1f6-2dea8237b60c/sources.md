

1. **Precision and Privacy in Distributed Quantum Sensing: A Quantum Fisher Information Duality**
   - **arxivID**: 2605.20765
   - **Author**: Farhad Farokhi
   - **date**: 2026-05-20
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.20765v1
   - **short summary**: Establishes a quantum Fisher information (QFI) duality for distributed quantum sensor networks with local phase encoding. It bounds the sum of QFIs for orthogonal parameter estimations, providing a direct theoretical framework for calculating the QFI of scaled average parameters in noisy, spatially separated sensor networks.

2. **Private and Robust States for Distributed Quantum Sensing**
   - **arxivID**: 2407.21701
   - **Author**: Luís Bugalho, Majid Hassani, Yasser Omar, Damian Markham
   - **date**: 2024-07-31
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.21701v2
   - **short summary**: Investigates distributed quantum sensing protocols for estimating functions of multiple parameters encoded in spatially separated probes. The paper analyzes robust entangled states (including GHZ-like configurations) and their performance under noise, directly relevant to modeling noisy distributed sensing networks.

3. **A Framework for Spatial Quantum Sensing**
   - **arxivID**: 2602.12193
   - **Author**: Luís Bugalho, Yasser Omar, Damian Markham
   - **date**: 2026-02-12
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.12193v2
   - **short summary**: Proposes a comprehensive framework for spatial quantum sensing, focusing on non-local estimators in quantum sensor networks. It details the precision advantages of entangled probe states over classical strategies and provides mathematical tools for analyzing distributed parameter estimation under dephasing and other noise channels.
