

1. **arxivID**: 1604.03089
   **Author**: Fumio Hiai, Milan Mosonyi
   **date**: 2016-04-11
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.03089
   **short summary**: Provides the rigorous mathematical framework for the standard quantum $f$-divergence using the integral representation with left/right multiplication operators ($L_\rho, R_\sigma$). Contains detailed operator calculus, monotonicity proofs, and resolvent identities necessary to compute the functional derivative $\frac{d}{dt} D^{\mathrm{std}}_f(\rho_t \|\sigma)$ along linear state interpolations.

2. **arxivID**: 1008.2529
   **Author**: F. Hiai, M. Mosonyi, D. Petz, C. Beny
   **date**: 2010-08-15
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2529
   **short summary**: Establishes the connection between quantum $f$-divergences and Petz's quasi-entropies. Discusses the differentiability of these divergences with respect to quantum states and derives explicit gradient formulas involving the trace and operator inverses, which directly apply to evaluating the derivative at $t=0.5$.

3. **arxivID**: 1710.10252
   **Author**: Mark M. Wilde
   **date**: 2017-10-27
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10252
   **short summary**: Analyzes optimized and standard quantum $f$-divergences, focusing on their geometric properties and data processing behavior. Offers complementary insights into the variation of divergence measures under state perturbations, supporting the step-by-step derivation of the time-derivative along the path $\rho_t$.

4. **arxivID**: 2302.08765
   **Author**: A. J. P. Garner, et al.
   **date**: 2023-02-16
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08765
   **short summary**: A recent review on quantum divergences that updates the theoretical landscape of $f$-divergences and their applications in modern quantum information tasks. Clarifies modern conventions for state-space derivatives and provides updated references for handling operator-valued integrals in geometric quantum mechanics.
