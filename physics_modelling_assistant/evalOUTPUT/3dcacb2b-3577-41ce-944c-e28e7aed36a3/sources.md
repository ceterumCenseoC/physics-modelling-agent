

- **arxivID**: cond-mat/9412084  
  **Author**: M. Jarrell, J. K. Freericks, Th. Pruschke  
  **date**: 1994-12-16  
  **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9412084  
  **short summary**: Presents a theoretical and Monte Carlo analysis of the optical conductivity in the infinite-dimensional Hubbard model. It details the frequency dependence of the conductivity, Drude weight renormalization, and transport scattering rates, providing a foundational framework for perturbation theory approaches to interaction corrections.

- **arxivID**: 1204.3219  
  **Author**: G. H. Bach, F. Marsiglio  
  **date**: 2012-04-14  
  **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.3219  
  **short summary**: Derives analytical results for the spectral function and optical conductivity within the Dynamic Hubbard model. It explicitly calculates quasiparticle scattering rates and their contribution to transport properties, offering direct methodological parallels for second-order perturbative calculations.

- **arxivID**: 1507.04881  
  **Author**: E. Assmann, P. Wissgott, J. Kuneš, A. Toschi, P. Blaha, K. Held  
  **date**: 2015-07-17  
  **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.04881  
  **short summary**: Introduces the Woptic computational framework for evaluating optical conductivity and thermopower using maximally localized Wannier functions. It addresses adaptive Brillouin zone integration, which is highly relevant for accurately computing finite-frequency conductivity corrections in hypercubic lattices.

- **arxivID**: 1803.00840  
  **Author**: F. Hütt, A. Yaresko, M. B. Schilling, C. Shekhar, C. Felser, M. Dressel, A. V. Pronin  
  **date**: 2018-03-02  
  **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.00840  
  **short summary**: A recent experimental investigation of the linear-in-frequency optical conductivity in the half-Heusler compound GdPtBi. It provides physical context and empirical benchmarks for theoretical models predicting interaction-driven linear frequency dependencies in correlated electron systems.

- **arxivID**: cond-mat/0501263  
  **Author**: Holger Benthien, Eric Jeckelmann  
  **date**: 2005-01-11  
  **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0501263  
  **short summary**: Studies the optical conductivity of the one-dimensional dimerized Hubbard model at quarter filling using dynamical DMRG. While lower-dimensional, it elucidates how on-site interactions modify the Drude peak and finite-frequency response near the bottom of the conduction band.

- **arxivID**: 1103.2420  
  **Author**: Tao Li  
  **date**: 2011-03-12  
  **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.2420  
  **short summary**: Analyzes the quarter-doped Hubbard model on a honeycomb lattice, focusing on Fermi surface nesting instabilities and magnetic insulating states. It offers insights into how Fermi surface topology and dimensionality influence scattering rates and conductivity corrections.
