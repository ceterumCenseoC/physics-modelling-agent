

Here are 5 highly relevant papers from arXiv that provide the theoretical framework and specific models needed to solve the problem of finding the asymptotic population growth rate $\Lambda$ for stochastic cell size control and division noise:

1. **arxivID**: 1611.07989
   **Author**: Jie Lin, Ariel Amir
   **date**: 2016-11-23
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.07989
   **short summary**: Establishes a rigorous quantitative connection between the population growth rate and the generation times of single cells, explicitly accounting for mother-daughter correlations induced by cell size control. This paper provides the foundational perturbation methods to derive $\Lambda$ to first order in noise variance ($\sigma^2$) and is essential for analyzing how different size control parameters ($\beta$) stabilize population growth.

2. **arxivID**: 2209.14683
   **Author**: Yaïr Hein, Farshid Jafarpour
   **date**: 2022-09-29
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14683
   **short summary**: Studies the asymptotic decoupling of population growth rate and cell size distribution in the presence of stochastic growth rate fluctuations. It offers analytical tools and integral equation approaches to evaluate the Malthusian parameter $\Lambda$ when the single-cell growth rate $\lambda_t$ follows complex stochastic processes, directly applicable to the two-state telegraph noise described in the problem.

3. **arxivID**: 2601.05193
   **Author**: Arthur Genthon, Philipp Thomas
   **date**: 2026-01-08
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05193
   **short summary**: *Very recent paper.* Presents unified stochastic maps that integrate fluctuating single-cell growth rates and size-dependent noise mechanisms with generalized cell size control. The framework directly addresses how the regulation parameter $\beta$ and division noise $\xi$ modulate the transition from single-cell dynamics to population-level exponential growth, providing updated insights into size homeostasis under extrinsic noise.

4. **arxivID**: 1809.10217
   **Author**: Farshid Jafarpour
   **date**: 2018-09-26
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.10217
   **short summary**: Investigates how cell size regulation (sizer, adder, timer, and mixed models) induces dynamics and potential oscillations in the population growth rate. The paper explores the effect of generation time correlations on $\Lambda$ and helps explain the stabilizing vs. destabilizing roles of different $\beta$ values in the presence of stochastic growth environments.

5. **arxivID**: 1606.00535
   **Author**: César Augusto Vargas-García, Mohammad Soltani, Abhyudai Singh
   **date**: 2016-06-02
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.00535
   **short summary**: Models cell size homeostasis using a stochastic hybrid systems approach, providing a comprehensive framework for analyzing size control strategies under multiplicative growth noise. The mathematical treatment of the division threshold $v_d$ as a function of birth size $v_b$ and noise $\xi$ aligns closely with the problem's setup and aids in deriving the statistical properties of the division cycle.
