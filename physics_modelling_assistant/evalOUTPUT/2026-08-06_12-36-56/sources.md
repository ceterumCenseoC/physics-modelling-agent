

1. **arxivID**: 2410.16097  
**Author**: Fei-Yu Chen, H. Lu  
**date**: 2024-10-21  
**url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16097  
**short summary**: Directly addresses the 8-dimensional holographic Weyl anomaly by calculating it for general higher curvature gravity theories. The paper identifies the complete basis of 11 linearly independent curvature combinations (including terms analogous to $\text{tr}(P^4)$, $\text{tr}(B^2)$, etc.) and determines their precise coefficients in the on-shell action, making it the most relevant source for resolving $X^{(4)}$.

2. **arxivID**: hep-th/9806087  
**Author**: Mans Henningson, Kostas Skenderis  
**date**: 1998-06-11  
**url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9806087  
**short summary**: The foundational paper on holographic Weyl anomalies. It establishes the method of computing the anomaly by regularizing and renormalizing the bulk supergravity on-shell action. While primarily focusing on 4d, its formalism and counterterm construction are the basis for all higher-dimensional derivations.

3. **arxivID**: hep-th/0007062  
**Author**: Masafumi Fukuma, So Matsuura, Tadakatsu Sakai  
**date**: 2000-07-07  
**url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0007062  
**short summary**: Generalizes the holographic calculation of the Weyl anomaly to arbitrary even dimensions using the Hamilton-Jacobi formalism. It provides sample calculations and structural insights into how local geometric invariants contribute to the anomaly in dimensions higher than four.

4. **arxivID**: 2109.14014  
**Author**: Weizhen Jia, Manthos Karydas  
**date**: 2021-09-28  
**url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.14014  
**short summary**: Explores the geometric underpinnings of the holographic Weyl anomaly by introducing the Weyl-Fefferman-Graham gauge. It connects obstruction tensors in Weyl geometry to the holographic anomaly, clarifying the roles of higher-order tensors like $B_{\mu\nu}$ and $O_{\mu\nu}$ in the asymptotic expansion.

5. **arxivID**: 1811.10380  
**Author**: F. Bugini, D. E. Díaz  
**date**: 2018-11-26  
**url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.10380  
**short summary**: Analyzes the holographic Weyl anomaly for GJMS operators (conformal powers of the Laplacian) in 4d and 6d. It provides a comparative perspective on how higher-derivative boundary operators manifest in the holographic anomaly, which is structurally relevant for understanding complex tensor contractions in 8d.
