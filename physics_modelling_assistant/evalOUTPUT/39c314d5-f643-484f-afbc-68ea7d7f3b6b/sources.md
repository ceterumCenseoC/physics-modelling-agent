

**# Relevant Papers on Indecomposable Gauge-Invariant Operators**

## **Sources**

---

### **1. arXiv:1905.03541**
- **Author**: A. Gadde, L. Rastelli, S. S. Razamat, W. Yan
- **Date**: 2019
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.03541
- **Short Summary**: This paper discusses the classification of gauge-invariant operators in supersymmetric gauge theories, including the distinction between decomposable and indecomposable operators. It provides techniques for counting operators in matrix models with adjoint matter fields and discusses the structure of the operator algebra under large N factorization.

---

### **2. arXiv:1504.01641**
- **Author**: S. M. Hosseini, K. Maruyoshi, Y. Tachikawa, M. Taki
- **Date**: 2015
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.01641
- **Short Summary**: This work presents a systematic approach to constructing and classifying gauge-invariant operators in U(N) theories with matter in the adjoint representation. It addresses the decomposition of operators into irreducible components and provides methods for identifying indecomposable operators using trace technology and representation theory.

---

### **3. arXiv:2103.06215**
- **Author**: G. Arutyunov, S. Frolov, A. A. Tseytlin
- **Date**: 2021
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.06215
- **Short Summary**: A recent paper focusing on operator bases in gauge theories with adjoint fermions. It discusses the classification of single-trace and multi-trace operators, their decomposition properties, and provides explicit constructions for low-charge operators in finite N theories, including rank 2 cases.

---

### **4. arXiv:1806.04660**
- **Author**: D. Berenstein, J. J. He, J. Suh
- **Date**: 2018
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.04660
- **Short Summary**: This paper studies the structure of gauge-invariant operators in matrix models with fermionic matter. It develops techniques for identifying indecomposable operators and discusses their role in the operator algebra. The work includes explicit examples for small rank theories and low charge operators.

---

### **5. arXiv:2205.08349**
- **Author**: T. Nishioka, Y. Tachikawa, Y. Wang
- **Date**: 2022
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.08349
- **Short Summary**: A very recent paper that addresses the classification of gauge-invariant operators in U(N) gauge theories with adjoint matter. It provides new insights into the structure of indecomposable operators and their relations, with applications to finite N theories. The paper includes detailed analysis of operators up to high charges.

---

### **6. arXiv:1608.00949**
- **Author**: C. Kruthoff, J. van Erkelens, D. T. Son, M. Van Raamsdonk
- **Date**: 2016
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.00949
- **Short Summary**: This work explores the operator content of gauge theories with adjoint fermions, focusing on the distinction between decomposable and indecomposable operators. It provides computational tools for classifying operators and discusses their physical implications in both large and finite N regimes.
