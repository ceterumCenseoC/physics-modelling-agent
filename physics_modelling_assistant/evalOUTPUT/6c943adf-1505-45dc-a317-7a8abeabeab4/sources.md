

# Relevant Papers for One-Axis Twisting Spin Squeezing with Dephasing and Dissipation

* **arxivID**: 2403.11587
  **Author**: Garry Goldstein
  **Date**: 2024-03-18
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11587
  **Short Summary**: In this work we study One Axis Twisting (OAT) spin squeezing for metrology in the presence of decoherence. We study Linbladian evolution in the presence of both T_1 and T_2 (longitudinal and transverse relaxation processes). We show that spin squeezing can be an effective way to improve metrological sensitivity even in the presence of dissipation.

* **arxivID**: 1312.3743
  **Author**: C. G. Ji, Y. C. Liu, G. R. Jin
  **Date**: 2013-12-13
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.3743
  **Short Summary**: We present a detailed analysis of spin squeezing of the one-axis twisting model with a many-body phase dephasing, which is induced by external field fluctuation in a two-mode Bose-Einstein condensates. Even in the presence of the dephasing, our analytical results show that the optimal initial state can generate significant spin squeezing.

* **arxivID**: 1309.4842
  **Author**: Wei Zhong, Jing Liu, Jian Ma, Xiaoguang Wang
  **Date**: 2013-09-19
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.4842
  **Short Summary**: We consider the quantum Fisher information and spin squeezing in one-axis twisting model with a coherent spin state. We analytically discuss the dependence of the two parameters: spin squeezing parameter and the average parameter estimation precision on the polar angle of the initial coherent spin state.

* **arxivID**: 0904.0496
  **Author**: Guang-Ri Jin, Yong-Chun Liu, Wu-Ming Liu
  **Date**: 2009-04-03
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.0496
  **Short Summary**: We investigate the dependence of spin squeezing on the polar angle of the initial coherent spin state in a generalized one-axis twisting model, where the detuning is taken into account. We show explicitly that regardless of the detuning and azimuthal angle, previous results of the ideal one-axis twisting model hold.

* **arxivID**: 1409.0683
  **Author**: Tomas Opatrny
  **Date**: 2014-09-02
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0683
  **Short Summary**: Based on the recent twisting-tensor approach, a specific scenario for fast and deep spin squeezing is proposed. Initially the state is subjected to one-axis twisting under optimum orientation, enabling the maximum squeezing rate allowed by the system nonlinearity, followed by two-axis counter-twisting for deep squeezing.
