The coupling constants are derived from the dipole-dipole interaction between the nanoparticles mediated by the Gaussian traps, considering the relative optical phase and longitudinal displacement.

$$k_1 = \frac{G \cos(k d_0) \cos(\phi_1 - \phi_2)}{k d_0}$$
$$k_2 = \frac{G \sin(k d_0) \sin(\phi_1 - \phi_2)}{k d_0}$$

where the coupling strength parameter $G$ is given by:
$$G = \frac{\alpha^2 k^5 \sqrt{P_1 P_2}}{2 \pi^2 \epsilon_0^2 c w_0^2}$$
(assuming identical polarizabilities $\alpha_1 = \alpha_2 = \alpha$; otherwise $\alpha^2$ is replaced by $\alpha_1 \alpha_2$).

Here:
*   $k$ is the wave vector.
*   $d_0$ is the equilibrium separation distance.
*   $\Delta \phi_0 = \phi_1 - \phi_2$ is the phase difference between the two lasers at the focal plane.
*   $P_1, P_2$ are the powers of the two traps.
*   $w_0$ is the beam waist.
*   $\epsilon_0$ is the vacuum permittivity and $c$ is the speed of light.
