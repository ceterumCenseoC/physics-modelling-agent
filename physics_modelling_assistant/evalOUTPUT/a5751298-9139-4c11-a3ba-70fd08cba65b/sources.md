

# Relevant Sources for LaMET Pion PDF Matching and DGLAP Evolution

1. **arxivID**: `2502.15384`
   - **Author**: Dao-yu Zhang, Chuang Huang, Wei-jie Fu
   - **Date**: 2025-02-21
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.15384v1
   - **Short Summary**: This very recent paper develops an approach to calculate the valence-quark quasi parton distribution function (quasi-PDF) and quasi parton distribution amplitude (quasi-PDA) for the pion at large longitudinal momentum using the functional renormalization group (fRG) within LaMET. It provides critical insights into the theoretical framework and matching procedures specifically tailored for pion systems, making it highly relevant for deriving the pion PDF from quasi-PDFs and validating the discretization/matching methodology.

2. **arxivID**: `1803.04393`
   - **Author**: Jiunn-Wei Chen, Luchang Jin, Huey-Wen Lin, Yu-Sheng Liu, Yi-Bo Yang, Jian-Hui Zhang, Yong Zhao
   - **Date**: 2018-03-12
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.04393v2
   - **Short Summary**: Presents a lattice-QCD calculation of the unpolarized parton distribution function using ensembles at the physical pion mass with large boost momenta within the LaMET framework. The paper details the explicit application of the perturbative matching kernel and scale evolution techniques, providing a practical blueprint for converting quasi-PDFs to standard PDFs at a target scale like $\mu = 2$ GeV and handling the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme conversions.

3. **arxivID**: `2007.15075`
   - **Author**: Keh-Fei Liu
   - **Date**: 2020-07-29
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.15075v2
   - **Short Summary**: Discusses the phenomenological definition of partons and addresses structural issues in the context of higher-order evolution equations and LaMET. It clarifies the classification of parton degrees of freedom and the rigorous matching formalism, offering theoretical backing for implementing the $C^{(1)}$ matching kernel with plus distributions and understanding renormalization scheme dependencies.

4. **arxivID**: `hep-ph/0503019`
   - **Author**: B. I. Ermolaev, M. Greco, S. I. Troyan
   - **Date**: 2005-03-02
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0503019v2
   - **Short Summary**: Focuses on non-singlet structure functions by combining leading logarithm resummation at small-$x$ with standard DGLAP evolution. This paper is essential for understanding how to properly resum large logarithmic terms during the scale evolution from the quasi-PDF matching scale to the target $\mu$, ensuring accurate PDF extraction when power corrections are neglected and the 1-loop $\alpha_s$ running is applied.
