

# Important Sources

- **arxivID:** 2506.06700
  - **Author:** A. S. Holevo, A. V. Utkin
  - **date:** 2025-06-07
  - **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06700
  - **short summary:** *Very recent.* Directly addresses the computation of accessible information for ensembles of quantum states. Applies modern optimality criteria to specific ensembles, providing essential theoretical tools for optimizing the Holevo quantity over classical-quantum (cq) states.

- **arxivID:** 2003.01942
  - **Author:** Junaid ur Rehman, Youngmin Jeong, Jeong San Kim, Hyundong Shin
  - **date:** 2020-03-04
  - **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.01942
  - **short summary:** Focuses on calculating the Holevo capacity of discrete Weyl channels. Details the numerical optimization procedures required over input ensembles to maximize Holevo information, highly relevant for deriving optimal probability distributions $\{p_x\}$ and state parameters.

- **arxivID:** 1512.09047
  - **Author:** M. E. Shirokov
  - **date:** 2015-12-30
  - **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.09047
  - **short summary:** Establishes tight continuity bounds for the Holevo quantity and quantum conditional mutual information. Provides rigorous mathematical foundations for the stability of the optimization landscape over discrete ensembles of quantum states.

- **arxivID:** 1405.4066
  - **Author:** V. Giovannetti, A. S. Holevo, A. Mari
  - **date:** 2014-05-16
  - **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.4066
  - **short summary:** Covers the Gaussian optimizer conjecture and majorization properties for bosonic channels. Offers advanced techniques for maximizing Holevo information, which can be adapted for parameterized density matrices like the provided $\rho_x$.

- **arxivID:** 2111.05355
  - **Author:** Xiao-Liang Qi, Zhou Shangnan, Zhenbin Yang
  - **date:** 2021-11-09
  - **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05355
  - **short summary:** Explores Holevo information as an upper bound for accessible classical information within ensemble theories. Discusses optimization over complex parameter spaces, providing conceptual parallels for reducing multi-parameter optimizations to single-variable functions $f(x)$.
