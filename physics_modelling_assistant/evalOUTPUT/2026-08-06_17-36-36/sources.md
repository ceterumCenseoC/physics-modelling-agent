

# Relevant Sources for Z_N Parafermion Tunneling and Braiding Protocols

1. **arxivID:** 2309.14411
**Author:** Junyi Cao, Angela Kou, Eduardo Fradkin
**Date:** 2023-09-25
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.14411v2
**Short Summary:** This recent paper investigates signatures of parafermion zero modes in fractional quantum Hall-superconductor heterostructures. It directly addresses parafermion and Cooper pair tunneling, backscattering, and the resulting $4\pi m$-periodic Josephson effects. The work provides essential theoretical models for calculating phase accumulation ($\phi_{ij}$) and ground-state fusion channels ($k_{ij}$) during tunneling processes, making it highly relevant for modeling the four-stage tunneling protocol described in the problem.

2. **arxivID:** 1907.12799
**Author:** Solofo Groenendijk, Alessio Calzona, Hugo Tschirhart, Edvin G. Idrisov, Thomas L. Schmidt
**Date:** 2019-07-30
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.12799v2
**Short Summary:** Focuses on the braiding of $\mathbb{Z}_{2n}$ parafermions emerging as bound states in fractional quantum Hall systems. The paper details how finite chemical potential and tunneling interactions affect the braiding statistics and phase accumulation. It offers critical insights into the non-Abelian braiding operations and the evolution of fusion channels $q$ under sequential tunneling Hamiltonians $H_{ij}$, directly applicable to the step-by-step protocol in the problem setup.

3. **arxivID:** 1709.02740
**Author:** Yuval Vinkler-Aviv, Piet W. Brouwer, Felix von Oppen
**Date:** 2017-09-08
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.02740v2
**Short Summary:** Explores $\mathbb{Z}_4$ parafermions realized in interacting quantum spin Hall Josephson junctions coupled to impurity spins. The study analyzes how Josephson phases and tunneling amplitudes dictate the ground-state manifold and fusion rules. This provides a concrete physical realization and mathematical framework for the phase-dependent tunneling Hamiltonian $H_{ij}=t(e^{-i\phi_{ij}/N}\alpha_i^\dagger\alpha_j+H.c.)$ and its role in determining the phase shift between initial and final ground states.

4. **arxivID:** 1209.0472
**Author:** Paul Fendley
**Date:** 2012-09-03
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1209.0472v2
**Short Summary:** A foundational work on parafermionic edge zero modes in $Z_n$-invariant spin chains. It rigorously defines the algebra of $Z_N$ parafermion operators, their fusion rules, and topological ground-state degeneracy. This paper supplies the essential theoretical basis for understanding the zero-mode operators $\alpha_i$, their permutation properties, and the fusion channel $q$ between unpaired modes, which is necessary for deriving the exact phase relationship in the problem.
