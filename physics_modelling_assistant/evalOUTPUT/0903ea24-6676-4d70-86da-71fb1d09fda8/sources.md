

# Relevant Papers for Holevo Information Optimization over cq States

1. **arxivID**: 2506.06700
   - **Author**: A. S. Holevo, A. V. Utkin
   - **Date**: 2025-06-07
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06700
   - **Short summary**: This very recent paper addresses the fundamental problem of computing accessible information (Holevo quantity) for ensembles of quantum states. It presents a novel optimality criterion for ensembles and applies it to derive the maximum Holevo information for specific state configurations, directly relevant to optimizing cq states $\chi=\sum_x p_x|x\rangle\langle x|\otimes\rho_x$.

2. **arxivID**: 2003.01942
   - **Author**: Junaid ur Rehman, Youngmin Jeong, Jeong San Kim, Hyundong Shin
   - **Date**: 2020-03-04
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.01942
   - **Short summary**: Focuses on the Holevo capacity of discrete Weyl channels, which involves maximizing the Holevo quantity over input ensembles. The paper details the numerical and analytical optimization techniques required to find the optimal probability distributions $\{p_x\}$ and states $\rho_x$, providing a strong methodological basis for solving the given optimization problem.

3. **arxivID**: 1512.09047
   - **Author**: M. E. Shirokov
   - **Date**: 2015-12-30
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.09047
   - **Short summary**: Investigates tight continuity bounds for the Holevo quantity and capacities of quantum channels with respect to discrete ensembles. It offers rigorous mathematical analysis on how the Holevo information behaves under variations in the ensemble, which is useful for understanding the stability and bounds of the optimization function $f(x)$ over the interval $[0,1]$.
