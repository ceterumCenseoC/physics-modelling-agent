

# Important Sources for AdS3/BCFT2 Black Hole Geodesic One-Point Functions

1. **arxivID:** 2601.09397  
**Author:** Parijat Dey, Arundhati Goldar, Nirmalya Kajuri  
**Date:** 2026-01-14  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09397  
**Short summary:** This very recent paper explicitly investigates the striking relation between thermal boundary one-point functions and bulk geodesic lengths in holographic black hole setups. It demonstrates that in the large conformal-dimension limit, the one-point function of a primary operator is determined by the exponential of the regularized geodesic length from the boundary insertion point into the bulk black hole geometry. This is directly applicable to deriving the functional dependence on the black hole radius $r_0$ and the operator dimension.

2. **arxivID:** 2109.00079  
**Author:** Jani Kastikainen, Sanjit Shashi  
**Date:** 2021-08-31  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.00079  
**Short summary:** This paper computes 1-point and 2-point correlation functions in holographic Boundary Conformal Field Theory (BCFT) using the geodesic approximation. The holographic model consists of a massive scalar field (dual to a boundary primary operator) coupled to a Karch-Randall brane that acts as a rigid boundary in the bulk AdS space. It provides explicit evaluations of geodesic Witten diagrams and boundary reflection/transmission terms, which are essential for determining the one-point function's dependence on the brane tension $\eta$ and the scalar mass $m$.

3. **arxivID:** 2104.07039  
**Author:** Hao Geng, Severin Lüst, Rashmish K. Mishra, David Wakeham  
**Date:** 2021-04-14  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.07039  
**Short summary:** Focusing on the AdS3/BCFT2 duality, this work studies two-dimensional BCFTs with boundaries dual to AdS3 geometries with Karch-Randall branes. At finite temperature, the authors analyze the bulk black hole geometry and the behavior of bulk geodesics terminating on or reflecting off the branes. This provides the foundational geometric setup and equations of motion for calculating thermal one-point functions in the presence of a black hole horizon and brane boundaries.

4. **arxivID:** 2109.01895  
**Author:** Timothy J. Hollowood, S. Prem Kumar, Andrea Legramandi, Neil Talwar  
**Date:** 2021-09-04  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.01895  
**Short summary:** This paper explores entanglement and quantum extremal surfaces in black hole backgrounds coupled to BCFTs. While primarily focused on entropy evolution and "islands", it details the precise behavior of bulk geodesics and extremal surfaces in the presence of black hole horizons and BCFT boundaries, offering complementary insights into how the bulk metric parameters (like $r_0$ and $\eta$) modify the geometric quantities that exponentiate into boundary correlators.
