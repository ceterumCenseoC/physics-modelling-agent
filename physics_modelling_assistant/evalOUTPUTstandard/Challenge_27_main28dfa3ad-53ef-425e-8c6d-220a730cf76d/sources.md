

# Relevant Sources for One-Axis Twisting Spin Squeezing with Dissipation

Here are 4 relevant papers selected from the search results that allow for the modeling of the One-Axis Twisting (OAT) system with the specified dissipation terms ($\gamma$ and $\gamma_z$) and the calculation of the Wineland spin-squeezing parameter.

1. **One Axis Twisting (OAT) spin squeezing for metrology**
   - **arxivID**: 2403.11587
   - **Author**: Garry Goldstein
   - **Date**: 2024-03-18
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11587
   - **Short summary**: This is the most recent and critical paper for the problem setup. It explicitly studies OAT spin squeezing for metrology using **Lindblad evolution** in the presence of both $T_1$ (spin-flip, corresponding to $\gamma$) and $T_2$ (dephasing, corresponding to $\gamma_z$) relaxation processes. It provides the necessary framework to model the dissipative dynamics and evaluate the metrological advantage (squeezing) under the exact noise conditions specified.

2. **Spin Squeezing of One-Axis Twisting Model in The Presence of Phase Dephasing**
   - **arxivID**: 1312.3743
   - **Author**: C. G. Ji, Y. C. Liu, G. R. Jin
   - **Date**: 2013-12-13
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.3743
   - **Short summary**: This paper offers a detailed analysis of spin squeezing in the one-axis twisting model specifically with **many-body phase dephasing**. It provides analytical results and insights into how the dephasing rate affects the optimal squeezing and the optimal time, which is essential for modeling the $\hat{L}_{z,j}$ term in the presence of the $\hat{S}^z\hat{S}^z$ Hamiltonian.

3. **Dynamical spin squeezing: combining fast one-axis twisting and deep two-axis counter-twisting**
   - **arxivID**: 1409.0683
   - **Author**: Tomas Opatrny
   - **Date**: 2014-09-02
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0683
   - **Short summary**: This paper provides a comprehensive discussion on the **dynamics** of the one-axis twisting Hamiltonian $\hat{H}=\chi\hat{S}^z\hat{S}^z$. It details the evolution of the spin state and the squeezing parameter $\xi^2$ over time, which serves as the baseline for understanding the coherent evolution part of the problem before adding dissipation.

4. **Quantum Fisher information and spin squeezing in one-axis twisting model**
   - **arxivID**: 1309.4842
   - **Author**: Wei Zhong, Jing Liu, Jian Ma, Xiaoguang Wang
   - **Date**: 2013-09-19
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.4842
   - **Short summary**: This work investigates the **Quantum Fisher information** and the spin squeezing parameter in the OAT model. It discusses the dependence of the squeezing parameter on the initial coherent spin state (specifically the polar angle), which is relevant for verifying the initial condition (coherent spin state in $+x$ direction) and calculating the variance $\langle\Delta S_{\perp}^2\rangle$.
