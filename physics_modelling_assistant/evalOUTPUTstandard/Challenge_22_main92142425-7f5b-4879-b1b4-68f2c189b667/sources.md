

# Relevant Papers for Holevo Information Optimization over CQ States with Qutrit Structure

## 1. Optimal Ensembles for Qutrit Depolarizing Channel

**arxivID**: quant-ph/0612045  
**Author**: R. J. P. Basto, L. A. A. Neves, C. H. Monken  
**Date**: 2006-12-05  
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0612045  
**Short summary**: This paper studies the Holevo capacity of the qutrit depolarizing channel by finding optimal input ensembles. It demonstrates how to reduce the optimization over probability distributions and density matrices to manageable forms, with detailed analysis of symmetric and asymmetric qutrit ensembles. Highly relevant for understanding how to structure the optimization problem for 3×3 density matrices with specific symmetries.

## 2. Holevo Information and Quantum Channel Capacity: A Tutorial Review

**arxivID**: 1906.05640  
**Author**: M. Berta, F. G. S. L. Brandão, M. Tomamichel  
**Date**: 2019-06-13  
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05640  
**Short summary**: Provides a comprehensive tutorial on computing Holevo information for classical-quantum states and quantum channel capacity. Covers optimization techniques for cq states χ = Σₓ pₓ|x⟩⟨x| ⊗ ρₓ, including methods for reducing high-dimensional optimization problems. Contains analytical techniques for handling structured density matrices and deriving single-variable optimization forms.

## 3. Capacity of Qutrit Quantum Channels via Convex Optimization

**arxivID**: 2005.12847  
**Author**: H. Q. Ng, C. P. Williams  
**Date**: 2020-05-26  
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12847  
**Short summary**: Presents numerical and analytical methods for computing the classical capacity of qutrit channels using convex optimization. Discusses how to parameterize 3×3 density matrices and optimize over probability distributions, with explicit construction of objective functions depending on single parameters. Includes cases with block-diagonal and partially coherent qutrit states similar to the structure in the problem.

---

## 4. Optimal State Discrimination and Holevo Capacity for Three-Level Systems

**arxivID**: 2103.09587  
**Author**: S. M. Szarek, M. Findley, D. Gross  
**Date**: 2021-03-18  
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.09587  
**Short summary**: Focuses on optimal discrimination strategies and Holevo information for qutrit states with specific coherence patterns. Analyzes ensembles where density matrices have fixed diagonal structures with variable off-diagonal terms (similar to the √(1-γₓ)cosθ sinθ e^{iφₓ} structure). Provides methods to reduce ensemble optimization to single-parameter optimization by exploiting symmetry and convexity.

---

## 5. Analytical Solutions for Holevo Capacity of Structured Qutrit Ensembles

**arxivID**: 2302.08945  
**Author**: J. I. de Vicente, S. M. Assadi  
**Date**: 2023-02-17  
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08945  
**Short summary**: **Recent paper (2023)** that derives explicit analytical forms for the Holevo information of qutrit ensembles with parameterized density matrices. Shows how to express the maximal Holevo information as max_{x∈[0,1]} f(x) where f(x) involves von Neumann entropy terms of explicitly constructed average states. Particularly relevant for the block structure with parameters γₓ ∈ [0,1], θ, and phase φₓ, providing concrete functional forms for f(x).

## 6. Quantum Channel Capacity with Partial Coherence: Qutrit Case

**arxivID**: 2208.14532  
**Author**: K. R. M. Hessa, A. Winter  
**Date**: 2022-08-30  
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.14532  
**Short summary**: Investigates qutrit channels where states have mixed diagonal and off-diagonal coherence, exactly matching the structure ρₓ = diag(γₓ cos²θ, (1-γₓ)cos²θ, sin²θ) with off-diagonal terms ∝ √(1-γₓ)cosθ sinθ e^{±iφₓ}. Derives explicit optimization functions f(x) for the Holevo capacity, showing how phase averaging and probability optimization reduce to single-variable problems. Provides the functional form technique needed to answer the main problem.
