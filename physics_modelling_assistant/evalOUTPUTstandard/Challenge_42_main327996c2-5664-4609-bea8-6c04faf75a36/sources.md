

- **arXiv ID**: 0812.1795
  **Author**: S. Adam, E. H. Hwang, E. Rossi, S. Das Sarma
  **Date**: 2008-12-10
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0812.1795v1
  **Summary**: This comprehensive review discusses the physics of charged impurities in graphene, including the formation of electron-hole puddles, screening effects, and transport properties. It is essential for determining the scaling exponents $\alpha$ and $\beta$ for puddle size $\xi$ and plateau width $\Delta V_g$, and for understanding the long-range nature of Coulomb scattering in graphene.

- **arXiv ID**: 2010.07178
  **Author**: José Luis Hernando, Yuriko Baba, Elena Díaz, Francisco Domínguez-Adame
  **Date**: 2020-10-14
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.07178v1
  **Summary**: This recent paper theoretically addresses the impact of random non-magnetic impurities on the surface states of 3D topological insulators. It provides critical insights into scattering mechanisms (long-range vs short-range) and mean free path comparisons in TIs, directly addressing the latter part of the problem setup.

- **arXiv ID**: 1111.6280
  **Author**: Marco Gibertini, Andrea Tomadin, Francisco Guinea, Mikhail I. Katsnelson, Marco Polini
  **Date**: 2011-11-27
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.6280v2
  **Summary**: This study investigates electron-hole puddles in graphene, exploring whether they arise solely from charged impurities or also from substrate corrugations. It offers valuable context for modeling the puddle formation and the role of the 3D substrate in creating charge inhomogeneities.

- **arXiv ID**: 0910.4604
  **Author**: Rudro R. Biswas, Alexander V. Balatsky
  **Date**: 2009-10-26
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0910.4604v3
  **Summary**: This paper analyzes the modification of local electronic structure caused by impurities on the surface of 3D topological insulators. It helps in understanding how charged impurities affect the Dirac point and density of states in TIs, relevant for determining if a similar plateau to graphene appears.

- **arXiv ID**: 1109.6439
  **Author**: Gerald Schubert, Holger Fehske
  **Date**: 2011-09-29
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1109.6439v2
  **Summary**: This numerical study confirms the formation of electron-hole puddles and metal-to-insulator transitions in disordered graphene nanoribbons. It supports the understanding of puddle dynamics and disorder effects in low-dimensional systems.

- **arXiv ID**: 1103.3413
  **Author**: D. Hsieh, Y. Xia, L. Wray, D. Qian, J. H. Dil, F. Meier, L. Patthey, J. Osterwalder, G. Bihlmayer, Y. S. Hor, R. J. Cava, M. Z. Hasan
  **Date**: 2011-03-17
  **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1103.3413v1
