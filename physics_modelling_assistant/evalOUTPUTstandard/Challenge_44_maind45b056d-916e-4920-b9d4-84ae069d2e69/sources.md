

# Relevant Sources for Kitaev Honeycomb Model Research

## 1. **Physical solutions of the Kitaev honeycomb model**
- **arxivID**: 1105.4573v2
- **Author**: Fabio L. Pedrocchi, Stefano Chesi, Daniel Loss
- **Date**: 2011-05-23
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.4573
- **Short summary**: This paper investigates the exact solution of the honeycomb model proposed by Kitaev and derives an explicit formula for the projector onto the physical subspace. The physical states are characterized by the parity of the total occupation of the fermionic eigenmodes. It is highly relevant for determining the flux-free sector, ground state degeneracy, and exact energy calculations on finite lattices with periodic boundary conditions.

## 2. **Topological phases and quantum computation**
- **arxivID**: 0904.2771v1
- **Author**: Alexei Kitaev, Chris Laumann
- **Date**: 2009-04-20
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.2771
- **Short summary**: A collection of lecture notes providing a pedagogical introduction to topological phenomena and the exact solution of the 2D Kitaev honeycomb model. It details the mapping to Majorana fermions coupled to static $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge fields, which is foundational for computing the ground state energy and analyzing the flux-free sector on a 3x2 Bravais lattice.

## 3. **Tensor network simulation of the Kitaev-Heisenberg model at finite temperature**
- **arxivID**: 1906.02220v2
- **Author**: Piotr Czarnik, Anna Francuz, Jacek Dziarmaga
- **Date**: 2019-06-05
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.02220
- **Short summary**: The most recent paper in this set, investigating models on hexagonal lattices using exact environment full update (eeFU) tensor networks. While focusing on the Kitaev-Heisenberg extension, the numerical techniques and lattice setups discussed can be adapted or simplified to the pure isotropic Kitaev limit to verify exact ground state energies and degeneracies.

## 4. **Spin-S Kitaev model: Classical Ground States, Order by Disorder and Exact Correlation Functions**
- **arxivID**: 0806.1588v2
- **Author**: G. Baskaran, Diptiman Sen, R. Shankar
- **Date**: 2008-06-10
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.1588
- **Short summary**: Studies the spin-S Kitaev model using spin wave theory, uncovering the geometry of minimum energy surfaces and Cartesian ground states. Offers complementary insights into ground state degeneracies and exact correlation functions that grow exponentially with system size, useful for cross-validating the $S=1/2$ isotropic limit.
