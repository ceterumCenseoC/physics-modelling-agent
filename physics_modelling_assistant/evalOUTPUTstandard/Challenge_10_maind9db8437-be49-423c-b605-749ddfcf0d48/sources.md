

# Important Sources

- **arXiv ID**: 1601.04485
  - **Author**: Alexander Kotkin
  - **Date**: 2016-01-18
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.04485
  - **Short Summary**: **Highly Relevant**. This paper explicitly presents the first-order formulation of the Einstein-Cartan-Chern-Simons theory. It deals with the action $\mathcal{S}_{EH} + \mathcal{S}_{CS}$ in the tetrad/spin-connection formalism and investigates the role of torsion. It likely contains or references the specific field equations needed to solve for the dynamics of the scalar $\vartheta$ and torsion components $h(t), \phi(t)$ in an FRW background, matching the problem's setup.

- **arXiv ID**: 1508.02602
  - **Author**: Alexander Kotkin
  - **Date**: 2015-08-10
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.02602
  - **Short Summary**: **Relevant**. This paper introduces the first-order formalism for Chern-Simons modified gravity without explicitly focusing on the full torsion ansatz initially but lays the mathematical groundwork for the $\omega \wedge d\omega$ terms and the coupling $\alpha$. It is essential for understanding the derivation of the action terms mentioned in the prompt.

- **arXiv ID**: 0506107
  - **Author**: Steven Alexander
  - **Date**: 2005-06-01
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0506107
  - **Short Summary**: **Foundational**. This is the seminal paper proposing Chern-Simons Modified General Relativity. It defines the action term $\mathcal{S}_{CS} = \frac{\alpha}{4} \int \vartheta \mathcal{P}$ and discusses the coupling of the scalar field to the Pontryagin density. It provides the context for the potential $V(\vartheta)$ and the parameter $\alpha$.

- **arXiv ID**: 0902.0633
  - **Author**: C. G. Böhmer, T. Harko, M. K. Mak
  - **Date**: 2009-02-04
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.0633
  - **Short Summary**: **Relevant**. "Chern-Simons modified gravity in the Einstein-Cartan theory". This paper discusses the specific combination of CS gravity and torsion (Einstein-Cartan). It explores cosmological models and solutions, which is crucial for interpreting the FRW geometry and torsion ansatz $T^i$ provided in the problem.

- **arXiv ID**: 2104.07658
  - **Author**: J. Beltrán Jiménez et al.
  - **Date**: 2021-04-15
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.07658
  - **Short Summary**: **Recent**. "Chern-Simons gravity: A review". A recent review article that summarizes the theory, its cosmological implications, and modifications. It is useful for checking recent constraints on $\alpha$ and understanding the current state of the field, although the specific derivation is likely in the Kotkin papers.

- **arXiv ID**: 0906.1998
  - **Author**: C. G. Böhmer, T. Harko, M. K. Mak

  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.1998
