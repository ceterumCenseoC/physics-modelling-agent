

# List of Important Sources

- **arxivID**: 2608.07386
  - **Author**: Ilaria Andrei, Christian Dioguardi, Damianos Iosifidis, Laur Järv, Antonio Racioppi, Margus Saal
  - **Date**: 2026-08-07
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.07386v1
  - **Short Summary**: This paper investigates single-field slow-roll inflation in metric-affine gravity where a scalar field is non-minimally coupled to the Ricci scalar and Nieh-Yan-like terms. It is the most relevant source as it directly addresses the problem setup of deriving equations of motion and calculating cosmological parameters (like e-folds) in a model involving torsion, a scalar field, and the Nieh-Yan term.

- **arxivID**: 2007.12595
  - **Author**: Miklos Långvik, Juha-Matti Ojanperä, Sami Raatikainen, Syksy Rasanen
  - **Date**: 2020-07-24
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.12595v2
  - **Short Summary**: This study analyzes Higgs inflation with the inclusion of Holst and Nieh-Yan terms in the action. It explores how these terms modify the classical equations of motion for a scalar field in an FRW background, providing a critical framework for understanding the dynamics of the scalar field coupled to Nieh-Yan torsion.

- **arxivID**: 1002.0669
  - **Author**: Kinjal Banerjee
  - **Date**: 2010-02-03
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1002.0669v2
  - **Short Summary**: This paper examines the relationship between the Holst and Nieh-Yan terms in General Relativity with torsion. It demonstrates that the Nieh-Yan term affects the classical equations of motion in the presence of matter, serving as a foundational reference for the theoretical structure of the action in the Palatini formalism.

- **arxivID**: 2308.00578
  - **Author**: Ida M. Rasulian, Mahdi Torabian
  - **Date**: 2023-08-01
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.00578v2
  - **Short Summary**: This work discusses the contribution of torsion to the chiral anomaly via the Nieh-Yan term. It provides a rigorous treatment of the Nieh-Yan term's properties, which is useful for correctly defining the action and the torsion ansatz in the problem setup.

- **arxivID**: 2112.02003
  - **Author**: Manuel Valle, Miguel A. Vazquez-Mozo
  - **Date**: 2021-12-03
  - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.02003v2
  - **Short Summary**: This paper studies nondissipative transport induced by the Nieh-Yan anomaly, computing torsional terms in the equilibrium partition function. It offers additional insight into the physical behavior of the Nieh-Yan term in first-order formulations of gravity.
