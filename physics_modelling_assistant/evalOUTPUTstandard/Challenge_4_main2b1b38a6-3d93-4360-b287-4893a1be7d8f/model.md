# Mathematical Model for OAM and Helicity Calculation in High-Harmonic Generation

## 1. System Definition and Physical Constraints

High-harmonic generation (HHG) is a non-perturbative process where an intense driving field ionizes an atom or molecule, accelerates the free electron in the laser field, and forces a recollision that results in the emission of a high-energy photon. In this model, we consider a driving field composed of three temporally delayed pulses focused into a gas jet.

**Physical Parameters of the Driving Field:**
*   **Pulse 1 ($t=0$ fs):**
    *   Center Wavelength: $\lambda = 800$ nm
    *   Polarization: Left Circular (LCP)
    *   Topological Charge (OAM): $\ell_1 = -1$
    *   Helicity (SAM): $\sigma_1 = +1$
*   **Pulse 2 ($t=30$ fs):**
    *   Center Wavelength: $\lambda = 800$ nm
    *   Polarization: Right Circular (RCP)
    *   Topological Charge (OAM): $\ell_2 = 2$
    *   Helicity (SAM): $\sigma_2 = -1$
*   **Pulse 3 ($t=60$ fs):**
    *   Center Wavelength: $\lambda = 800$ nm
    *   Polarization: Left Circular (LCP)
    *   Topological Charge (OAM): $\ell_3 = 1$
    *   Helicity (SAM): $\sigma_3 = +1$

**Conservation Laws:**
Based on the conservation of angular momentum in isotropic media (such as a gas jet), the total angular momentum projected along the propagation axis ($z$) must be conserved [1, 2]. This consists of two components: the Orbital Angular Momentum ($\ell$) and the Spin Angular Momentum ($\sigma$).

## 2. Model Derivation: Composite Driving Field Properties

Since the pulses are temporally delayed by 30 fs (which is shorter than the 50 fs pulse duration FWHM), there is significant temporal overlap. They are spatially overlapped in the gas jet. In the regime of high-harmonic generation, the process is predominantly determined by the properties of the driving field at the moment of ionization and recollision. For a composite field of identical pulses interfering in the medium, we treat the effective angular momentum of the driver as the coherent sum of the individual components.

### Step 1: Determine the Net Spin Angular Momentum (Helicity)
The helicity $\sigma$ corresponds to the circular polarization state.
*   $\sigma = +1$ corresponds to Left Circular Polarization (LCP).
*   $\sigma = -1$ corresponds to Right Circular Polarization (RCP).

The field consists of two LCP pulses ($\sigma = +1$) and one RCP pulse ($\sigma = -1$). The resulting net polarization is dominated by the LCP component. Experimentally, HHG driven by resolved circular fields is suppressed unless the field has a net circular polarization [2]. Thus, the effective helicity of the driving field is:

$$ \sigma_{\text{drive}} = +1 $$

### Step 2: Determine the Net Orbital Angular Momentum (OAM)
The topological charge $\ell$ of the structured light defines its phase singularity $e^{i\ell\phi}$. When multiple vortices are superimposed, the resulting field is determined by the sum of their topological charges, provided they are co-axial [3]. The effective OAM of the composite driver is:

$$ \ell_{\text{net}} = \sum_{j=1}^{3} \ell_j $$
$$ \ell_{\text{net}} = \ell_1 + \ell_2 + \ell_3 $$
$$ \ell_{\text{net}} = (-1) + 2 + 1 $$
$$ \ell_{\text{net}} = 2 $$

## 3. calculation of the 23rd Harmonic Properties

According to the conservation laws and perturbative scaling relations for HHG [1, 3]:
1.  The harmonic order $q$ represents the energy scaling: $E_q = q \hbar \omega$.
2.  The OAM of the $q$-th harmonic, $\ell_q$, scales linearly with the harmonic order $q$.
    $$ \ell_q = q \cdot \ell_{\text{drive}} $$
3.  The helicity of the harmonic follows the selection rules of the driving field. For a circularly polarized driver, the harmonic preserves the helicity.
    $$ \sigma_q = \sigma_{\text{drive}} $$

### Calculation for Harmonic Order $q = 23$:

**Orbital Angular Momentum ($\ell_{23}$):**
Using the derived net OAM of the driving field $\ell_{\text{net}} = 2$:
$$ \ell_{23} = 23 \cdot \ell_{\text{net}} $$
$$ \ell_{23} = 23 \cdot 2 $$
$$ \ell_{23} = 46 $$

**Helicity ($\sigma_{23}$):**
Using the derived net helicity of the driving field $\sigma_{\text{drive}} = +1$:
$$ \sigma_{23} = \sigma_{\text{drive}} $$
$$ \sigma_{23} = +1 $$

## 4. Final Model Results

The model predicts the following properties for the 23rd harmonic order generated by the structured light sequence:

*   **Orbital Angular Momentum (OAM):** $\ell_{23} = 46\hbar$
*   **Helicity ($\sigma$):** $+1$ (Left Circular)
