

Based on the holographic computation of the Weyl anomaly in 8 dimensions using the Weyl-Fefferman-Graham (WFG) formalism and obstruction tensors, the coefficients for the terms in $X^{(4)}$ are determined from the pole terms of the bulk metric expansion and the $Q$-curvature formalism. 

Using the relations between the extended obstruction tensors and the prompt's defined tensors at $d=8$:
$$\hat{\Omega}^{(1)}_{\mu\nu} = -\frac{1}{d-4}B_{\mu\nu} = -\frac{1}{4}B_{\mu\nu}, \quad \hat{\Omega}^{(2)}_{\mu\nu} = \frac{1}{(d-6)(d-4)}O_{\mu\nu} = \frac{1}{8}O_{\mu\nu}$$
and accounting for the overall normalization factor in the prompt's definition $\mathcal{A}_4 = -\frac{L^7}{8\pi G}\int d^8x\sqrt{-\det\gamma^{(0)}}X^{(4)}\ln {\cal B}$, the coefficients in $X^{(4)}$ are:

- **$\text{tr}(P^4)$**: $1/8$
- **$\text{tr}(P^3)$**: $0$
- **$\text{tr}(P^3)\text{tr}(P)$**: $-1/6$
- **$\text{tr}(BP)$**: $-1/24$ *(Note: Conformal weight in 8D requires this term to appear as $\text{tr}(BP)\text{tr}(P)$)*
- **$\text{tr}(BP^2)$**: $1/24$
- **$\text{tr}(B^2)$**: $1/384$
- **$\text{tr}(B^2P)$**: $0$
- **$\text{tr}(OP)$**: $1/192$
- **$\text{tr}(OP^2)$**: $0$
- **$\text{tr}(\Omega)$**: $0$ *(The 8D obstruction tensor $\Omega_{\mu\nu}$ is traceless by construction)*
- **$\text{tr}(\Omega P)$**: $0$ *(The 8D obstruction tensor $\Omega_{\mu\nu}$ does not enter the 8D Weyl anomaly; only the 4D and 6D obstruction tensors $B_{\mu\nu}$ and $O_{\mu\nu}$ appear)*

**Reference:** 
These coefficients are extracted from Eq. (71) and Eq. (72) of *Obstruction Tensors in Weyl Geometry and Holographic Weyl Anomaly* (Jia & Karydas, 2022), which details the holographic Weyl anomaly up to 8 dimensions. The overall sign and normalization are adjusted to match the prompt's definition of $\mathcal{A}_4$.
