

# Continuum Model for Twisted Bilayer MoTe$_2$

## Model Hamiltonian and Parameters
The low-energy electronic structure of twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$ at the K valley is described by the following $2 \times 2$ continuum Hamiltonian in layer space [1,2]:

$$
\mathcal{H} = \int d^2 r\ ( c^\dagger_{\boldsymbol{r},b}, c^\dagger_{\boldsymbol{r},t})\left(
\begin{array}{cc}
\frac{\hbar^2 \nabla^2}{2 m^*} +2 V \sum_{i=1}^3 \cos(\boldsymbol{g}_i\cdot \boldsymbol{r}- \,\psi) &  w \sum_{i=1}^3 \,e^{-i\,\boldsymbol{q}_i\cdot \boldsymbol{r}} \\
w \sum_{i=1}^3 \,e^{i\,\boldsymbol{q}_i\cdot \boldsymbol{r}}  & \frac{\hbar^2 \nabla^2}{2 m^*} + 2 V \sum_{i=1}^3 \cos(\boldsymbol{g}_i\cdot \boldsymbol{r} + \,\psi)
\end{array}
\right) \left(\begin{matrix} c_{\boldsymbol{r},b} \\ c_{\boldsymbol{r},t}\end{matrix}\right)
$$

**Physical Parameters:**
* Effective mass: $m^* = 0.6 \, m_e$ (where $m_e$ is the free electron mass) [1,2]
* Moiré potential amplitude: $V = 16.5 \text{ meV}$
* Potential phase: $\psi = -105.9^\circ$
* Interlayer tunneling strength: $w = -18.8 \text{ meV}$
* Monolayer lattice constant: $a_0 = 3.52 \text{ \AA }$
* Twist angle: $\theta = 3.5^\circ$

## Moiré Reciprocal Lattice and Momentum Vectors
The moiré superlattice period is defined by the twist angle as [1,2]:
$$
a_M = \frac{a_0}{2 \sin\left( \frac{\theta}{2} \right)}
$$
The three primary moiré reciprocal lattice vectors $\boldsymbol{g}_i$ (where $i=1,2,3$) are generated by three-fold rotation symmetry $C_3$ from the base vector:
$$
\boldsymbol{g}_1 = \frac{4 \pi}{\sqrt{3} a_{M}} (1,0)^T, \quad \boldsymbol{g}_i = C_3^{i-1} \boldsymbol{g}_1
$$
The interlayer momentum transfer vectors $\boldsymbol{q}_i$ are similarly rotated:
$$
\boldsymbol{q}_1 = |\boldsymbol{g}_1| (0, 1/\sqrt{3})^T, \quad \boldsymbol{q}_i = C_3^{i-1} \boldsymbol{q}_1
$$

## Topological Invariants and Quantum Metric
For a generic isolated set of $N$ bands, the projector $P_{\boldsymbol{k}}$ is constructed from the periodic part of the Bloch states. The topological characterization requires:

1. **Chern Numbers:** For $\theta = 3.5^\circ$, the task is to determine the Chern numbers of the top three bands. The global sign is arbitrary due to convention [2].
2. **Quantum Metric:** Defined in momentum space as [2]:
   $$
   g_{ij}(\boldsymbol{k}) = \frac{1}{2}\mathrm{Tr}[\partial_{k_i} P_{\boldsymbol{k}} \partial_{k_j} P_{\boldsymbol{k}}]
   $$
3. **Wannier Spread Trace:** The gauge-invariant part of the Wannier spread for the isolated band set is proportional to the integral of the trace of the quantum metric over the first Brillouin zone [2]:
   $$
   \mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\mathcal{G} = \int d^2 k\ \mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}[g(\boldsymbol{k})]
   $$
   The target quantity is the numerical evaluation of $\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\mathcal{G}$ for the top electron band, rounded to two decimal places.

## Numerical Evaluation Conventions
To compute $\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\mathcal{G}$ and the band structure, the following discretization and basis conventions are specified:

* **Momentum Mesh:** The Brillouin zone is sampled with $L \times L$ points ($L=60$):
  $$
  \boldsymbol{k} = \left\{ \left(\frac{l_1}{L}-\frac{1}{2}\right) \boldsymbol{b}_1 + \left(\frac{l_2}{L}-\frac{1}{2}\right) \boldsymbol{b}_2 \;\Big|\; l_1,l_2 = 0,1,\dots,L-1 \right\}
  $$
  where $\boldsymbol{b}_1 = \boldsymbol{g}_1$ and $\boldsymbol{b}_2 = \boldsymbol{g}_1 + \boldsymbol{g}_2$.
* **Kinetic Energy Constant:** $\hbar/(2 m_e) = 7619.96423 \text{ meV} \cdot \AA^2$ [1].
* **Fourier Transformation:** Field operators are expanded as:
  $$
  c^\dagger_{\boldsymbol{r},l} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{ \mathcal{V}}} \sum_{\boldsymbol{k},\boldsymbol{Q}} e^{-\mathrm{i} (\boldsymbol{k}-\boldsymbol{Q})\cdot \boldsymbol{r} } c^\dagger_{\boldsymbol{k}-\boldsymbol{Q},l}
  $$
  where $\mathcal{V}$ is the sample volume.
* **Plane-Wave Cutoffs ($\boldsymbol{Q}$ selection):**
  * For top layer ($t$): $\boldsymbol{Q}-\boldsymbol{q}_1$ must be a reciprocal lattice vector, with $|\boldsymbol{Q}| < 4.1 |\boldsymbol{b}_1|$.
  * For bottom layer ($b$): $\boldsymbol{Q}+\boldsymbol{q}_1$ must be a reciprocal lattice vector, with $|\boldsymbol{Q}| < 4.1 |\boldsymbol{b}_1|$ [1,2].
