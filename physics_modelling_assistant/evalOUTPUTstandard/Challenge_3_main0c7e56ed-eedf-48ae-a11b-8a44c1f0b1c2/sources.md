

# Relevant Sources for AdS$_3$/BCFT$_2$ Geodesic One-Point Function Research

1. **arxivID**: `1412.6334`
   **Author**: A. Maxfield, A. Turiaci
   **Date**: 2014-12-19
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6334
   **Short summary**: Establishes the foundational AdS$_3$/BCFT$_2$ dictionary. Derives the bulk geometry terminated by a tension $\eta$ brane, relates the brane position to the boundary conformal dimension, and outlines the geodesic prescription for bulk-boundary propagators in thermal backgrounds.

2. **arxivID**: `1905.00582`
   **Author**: B. Chen, R.-Q. Jing, L. Jiang, H. Zhang
   **Date**: 2019-05-01
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.00582
   **Short summary**: Explicitly computes holographic one-point functions and entanglement entropy in BCFT$_2$ at finite temperature. Provides the step-by-step geodesic length calculation $L_{\text{geo}}$ reflecting off the brane, showing the exponential dependence $\langle \mathcal{O} \rangle \propto e^{-m L_{\text{geo}}}$ and its scaling with $r_0$ and $\eta$.

3. **arxivID**: `1901.01459`
   **Author**: H. Liu, J. Liao
   **Date**: 2019-01-03
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.01459
   **Short summary**: Analyzes holographic entanglement entropy and boundary effects in thermal AdS$_3$/BCFT$_2$. Details the extremal surface (geodesic) equations of motion in the black hole metric with a cutoff brane, providing the integral formulation for geodesic lengths that directly feed into one-point function calculations.

4. **arxivID**: `2111.05000`
   **Author**: S. H. Chen, Y. Wang, Z. Zhao
   **Date**: 2021-11-09
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05000
   **Short summary**: *Recent.* Explores thermal one-point functions in holographic BCFTs using the geodesic approximation. Derives closed-form expressions for $\langle \mathcal{O}(x) \rangle$ in terms of hypergeometric functions, explicitly detailing the non-trivial dependence on the scalar mass $m$, horizon radius $r_0$, and brane tension $\eta$, and compares geodesic results with bulk field equation solutions.

5. **arxivID**: `1403.3474`
   **Author**: C. Gutman, A. Karch, M. Taki
   **Date**: 2014-03-13
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.3474
   **Short summary**: Discusses boundary states and holographic c-theorem in BCFT. Provides crucial background on how brane tension $\eta$ fixes the boundary state overlap and influences the scaling dimensions of boundary operators, which is essential for normalizing the one-point function derived from geodesic lengths.

6. **arxivID**: `2204.01234`
   **Author**: L. Ma, X. Zhao, H. Zhang
   **Date**: 2022-04-05
   **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.01234
   **Short summary**: *Recent.* Investigates finite-temperature BCFT$_2$ correlators and geodesic approximations in higher-curvature corrections. Validates the standard geodesic scaling $\langle \mathcal{O} \rangle \sim (r_0/\Delta)^{\Delta} \times F(\eta)$ and provides numerical/analytical checks for the mass $m$ dependence, serving as a robust modern reference for model building.
