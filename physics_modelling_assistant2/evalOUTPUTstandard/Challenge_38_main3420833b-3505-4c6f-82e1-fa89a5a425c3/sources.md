

# Relevant Sources for Hatsugai-Kohmoto Model Scattering and Temperature Dependence

1. **arXiv ID**: 2407.00156
   **Author**: Daniele Guerci, Giorgio Sangiovanni, Andrew J. Millis, Michele Fabrizio
   **Date**: 2024-06-28
   **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00156
   **Summary**: This paper directly addresses transport and scattering processes in the Hatsugai-Kohmoto (HK) model. It demonstrates that standard Kubo formulas fail to yield sensible transport coefficients and thermodynamic susceptibilities in momentum-local interaction models. This work is critical for correctly formulating the scattering rate integral $I(T)$, as it details how to properly handle the propagating modes and avoid double-counting or gauge-violating errors when computing the energy phase space integral in the finite-temperature regime.

2. **arXiv ID**: 2401.08746
   **Author**: Peizhi Mai, Jinchao Zhao, Gaurav Tenkila, Nico A. Hackner, Dhruv Kush, Derek Pan, Philip W. Phillips
   **Date**: 2024-01-16
   **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.08746
   **Summary**: The authors establish a rigorous mapping between the strongly correlated Hubbard model and the exactly solvable Momentum-Mixing Hatsugai-Kohmoto (MMHK) model. This is essential for your problem setup in the $U \gg W$ limit, as it validates using the HK framework to model strong interactions where the chemical potential crosses the lower Hubbard band. The paper provides the exact analytical machinery and thermodynamic relations needed to evaluate propagators and scattering integrals without uncontrolled perturbative approximations.

3. **arXiv ID**: 2512.03148
   **Author**: Yuting Bai, Philip W. Phillips
   **Date**: 2025-12-02
   **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03148
   **Summary**: A very recent paper that formally proves the equivalence between the MMHK model and the Twisted Hubbard model. By leveraging rigorous proofs that twisted models share identical bulk physics, it solidifies the theoretical foundation for using HK-type models to study finite-temperature properties and scattering rates in the strong-coupling limit. This supports the validity of your phase space integral approach in the specified regime $k_B T \gg \epsilon_1$ and provides up-to-date justification for the model's exact solvability.

4. **arXiv ID**: cond-mat/9705068
   **Author**: D. Lidsky, J. Shiraishi, Y. Hatsugai, M. Kohmoto
   **Date**: 1997-05-08
   **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9705068
   **Summary**: This foundational paper introduces and generalizes the Hatsugai-Kohmoto model, detailing its exact solvability and non-Fermi liquid ground states. It provides the baseline definitions for the Hamiltonian, the dispersion relations, and the exact diagonalization techniques required to compute occupation numbers $n_{k\sigma}$ and their temperature dependencies. These exact thermal distributions are the direct inputs required to evaluate the statistical averages in your scattering phase space integral $I(T)$.
