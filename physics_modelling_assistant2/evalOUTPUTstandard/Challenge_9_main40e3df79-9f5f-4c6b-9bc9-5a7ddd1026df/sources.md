

# Relevant Sources for Nieh-Yan Torsion Cosmology and Inflation

- **arxivID**: 2007.12595
  **Author**: Miklos Långvik, Juha-Matti Ojanperä, Sami Raatikainen, Syksy Rasanen
  **date**: 2020-07-24
  **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.12595v2
  **short summary**: Explores inflation in the context of Loop Quantum Gravity by incorporating both Holst and Nieh-Yan terms into the first-order Palatini action. The paper explicitly derives the torsion contributions to the Friedmann equations and scalar field dynamics in an FRW background, providing the necessary framework to compute e-folds and analyze the impact of torsion ansatzes on cosmic expansion.

- **arxivID**: 1809.07563
  **Author**: Flavio Bombacigno, Giovanni Montani
  **date**: 2018-09-20
  **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.07563v2
  **short summary**: Analyzes Palatini `R^2` gravity augmented with a Nieh-Yan term, solving the torsion field equations algebraically. It demonstrates how the Nieh-Yan coupling modifies the effective potential and background evolution in cosmology, offering direct methods for implementing the torsion-decomposition ansatz and deriving modified Friedmann equations for scalar-driven expansion.

- **arxivID**: 2301.02847
  **Author**: Mingzhe Li, Haomin Rao
  **date**: 2023-01-07
  **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.02847v2
  **short summary**: *(Recent)* Investigates Nieh-Yan modified teleparallel gravity coupled to a dynamical axion-like field. The study details how the Nieh-Yan density interacts with torsion components in a cosmological setting, discussing parity-violating effects and background evolution. It closely matches the specified action structure and provides insights into the dynamics of axion potentials with torsional couplings.

- **arxivID**: 2308.00578
  **Author**: Ida M. Rasulian, Mahdi Torabian
  **date**: 2023-08-01
  **url**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.00578v2
  **short summary**: *(Recent)* Clarifies the topological and dynamical role of the Nieh-Yan term in the presence of torsion. It addresses conditions under which the Nieh-Yan integral yields non-trivial contributions to field equations, which is crucial for correctly handling the boundary and topological aspects of the action when deriving equations of motion for the scalar field and metric variables.
