

# Important Sources

1. **arxivID**: 2311.07533v4
   - **Author**: Ning Mao, Cheng Xu, Jiangxu Li, Ting Bao, Peitao Liu, Yong Xu, Claudia Felser, Liang Fu, Yang Zhang
   - **Date**: 2023-11-13
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.07533v4
   - **Short Summary**: Directly addresses the simplified continuum model and electronic structure of twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$. Provides detailed analysis of lattice relaxation effects, moiré band structures, and continuum model parameters highly relevant to the Hamiltonian and topological band characterization in the problem setup.

2. **arxivID**: 2603.16374v1
   - **Author**: Min Wu, Lingxiao Li, Yunze Ouyang, Yifan Jiang, Wenxuan Qiu, Zaizhe Zhang, Zihao Huo, Qiu Yang, Ming Tian, Neng Wan, Kenji Watanabe, Takashi Taniguchi, Shiming Lei, Fengcheng Wu, Xiaobo Lu
   - **Date**: 2026-03-17
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.16374v1
   - **Short Summary**: *Very recent* experimental work observing a reconstructed Chern insulator in twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$. Discusses the emergence of topological flat bands, Chern number quantization, and band isolation at twist angles around $3.5^\circ$, providing crucial physical context for evaluating topological invariants and quantum metrics.

3. **arxivID**: 2111.11434v1
   - **Author**: Jonah Herzog-Arbeitman, Aaron Chew, Dmitri K. Efetov, B. Andrei Bernevig
   - **Date**: 2021-11-22
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.11434v1
   - **Short Summary**: Investigates reentrant correlated insulators in twisted bilayer graphene using the Bistritzer-MacDonald continuum model. Demonstrates how continuum models accurately capture topological band structures and integer quantum anomalous Hall phases, offering methodological parallels for calculating Chern numbers and Wannier spreads in moiré systems.

4. **arxivID**: 1909.01545v2
   - **Author**: Leon Balents
   - **Date**: 2019-09-04
   - **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.01545v2
   - **Short Summary**: Derives a general continuum Hamiltonian for bilayer systems with arbitrary smooth lattice deformations. Subsumes standard twisted bilayer continuum models and provides the rigorous theoretical framework for constructing momentum-space projectors, evaluating gauge-invariant quantities like the quantum metric, and handling Fourier transformation conventions in moiré lattices.
