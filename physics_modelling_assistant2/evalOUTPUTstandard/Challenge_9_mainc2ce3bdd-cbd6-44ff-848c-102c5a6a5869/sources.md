

# Important Sources for Nieh-Yan Torsion Cosmology and Inflation

Here are the most relevant papers for your research topic, sorted by relevance to the problem setup (Nieh-Yan action, torsion, scalar field, FRW cosmology, and Palatini formulation).

### 1. **Higgs inflation with the Holst and the Nieh-Yan term**
- **arxivID**: `2007.12595`
- **Author**: Miklos Långvik, Juha-Matti Ojanperä, Sami Raatikainen, Syksy Räsänen
- **Date**: 2020-07-24
- **URL**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.12595](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.12595)
- **Short Summary**: This paper explicitly studies inflationary models that include the Nieh-Yan term and the Holst term, which are relevant to Loop Quantum Gravity. It demonstrates how these torsional terms couple to scalar fields (like the Higgs or an axion) and affect the dynamics of inflation. It is highly relevant for deriving the modified Friedmann equations and scalar field equations of motion in the presence of Nieh-Yan torsion.

### 2. **Torsion, Scalar Field, Mass and FRW Cosmology**
- **arxivID**: `gr-qc/0603109`
- **Author**: Prasanta Mahato
- **Date**: 2006-03-28
- **URL**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0603109](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0603109)
- **Short Summary**: This work investigates the Einstein-Cartan theory with a scalar field and an axial vector torsion in an FRW universe. It derives the field equations for a scalar field non-minimally coupled to torsion, providing a foundational framework for understanding how torsion contributions ($T^A$) modify the standard cosmological dynamics, similar to the ansatz provided in your problem setup.

### 3. **Non-minimally coupled scalar field cosmology with torsion**
- **arxivID**: `1704.04563`
- **Author**: Antonella Cid, Fernando Izaurieta, Genly Leon, Perla Medina, Daniela Narbona
- **Date**: 2017-04-15
- **URL**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.04563](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.04563)
- **Short Summary**: The authors present a generalized Brans-Dicke Lagrangian including a non-minimally coupled Gauss-Bonnet term without imposing the vanishing torsion condition. The study highlights how torsion is dynamically related to the scalar field evolution, offering insights into solving the coupled differential equations for $a(t)$ and $\vartheta(t)$ in a first-order formulation.

### 4. **The chiral torsional anomaly and the Nieh-Yan invariant with and without boundaries** *(Very Recent)*
- **arxivID**: `2409.06766`
- **Author**: Johanna Erdmenger, Ioannis Matthaiakakis, René Meyer, Dmitri Vassilevich
- **Date**: 2024-09-10
- **URL**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06766](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06766)
- **Short Summary**: This very recent paper examines the role of the Nieh-Yan invariant in the presence of torsion and its contribution to the chiral anomaly. It provides a rigorous heat-kernel analysis of the Nieh-Yan term, which is crucial for correctly formulating the action $S_{NY}$ and understanding its physical implications in theories with torsion and scalar fields, such as axion inflation.
