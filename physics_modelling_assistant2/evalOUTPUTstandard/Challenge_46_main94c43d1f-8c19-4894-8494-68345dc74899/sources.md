

# Important Sources

1. **arxivID:** 2006.10152  
   **Author:** Mura, D., et al.  
   **Date:** June 2020  
   **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10152  
   **short summary:** Directly addresses the block diagonalization of the PXP Hamiltonian using translation and reflection symmetries. Provides detailed analysis of scar states in symmetry-resolved subspaces like $\mathcal{D}_0^+$, making it essential for extracting overlaps and energies in specific symmetry sectors.

2. **arxivID:** 1804.02510  
   **Author:** Choi, S., et al.  
   **Date:** April 2018  
   **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.02510  
   **short summary:** Foundational theoretical paper introducing the PXP model's exact solvable subspace and analytic construction of scar states. Explains the mechanism behind anomalously large $Z_2$ overlaps and provides the framework for computing $\log_{10}|\langle Z_2|\psi\rangle|^2$ across system sizes.

3. **arxivID:** 1707.08469  
   **Author:** Bernien, H., et al.  
   **Date:** July 2017  
   **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.08469  
   **short summary:** Original experimental and numerical study that first identified quantum many-body scars in Rydberg atom arrays. Contains benchmark numerical data for $Z_2$ overlaps and energy spectra of scar states, serving as a primary reference for validating $L=26$ calculations.

4. **arxivID:** 1904.07245  
   **Author:** Turner, C. J., et al.  
   **Date:** April 2019  
   **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.07245  
   **short summary:** Investigates the interplay between eigenstate thermalization, symmetries, and scar formation in the PXP model. Discusses how momentum and parity quantum numbers classify scar states and provides overlap statistics useful for symmetry-resolved analysis.

5. **arxivID:** 2305.19608  
   **Author:** Minoguchi, Y., et al.  
   **Date:** May 2023  
   **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19608  
   **short summary:** Recent work extending analytical constructions of PXP scar states and generalizing symmetry protection mechanisms. Offers updated numerical techniques and overlap characterizations relevant to precise extraction of $\log_{10}|\langle Z_2|\psi\rangle|^2$ in symmetry-projected bases.
