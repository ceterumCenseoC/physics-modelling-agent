

Based on the analysis of the provided literature, specifically the framework for Quantum Fisher Information (QFI) in distributed sensing networks under dephasing noise [1], we can derive the exact expression for the QFI of the scaled average parameter $\theta_1$.

### 1. QFI Matrix for Dephasing Noise in Distributed Networks
According to [1], for a distributed quantum sensing network with separable encoding dynamics generated by $\sigma_z$ operators, the QFI matrix elements $Q_{\mu\nu}$ for nodes $\mu$ and $\nu$ under dephasing noise are given by:
$$
Q_{\mu\nu}(\rho_\theta) = 4 \frac{(\lambda_+ - \lambda_-)^2}{\lambda_+ + \lambda_-} a_\mu a_\nu
$$
where $a_\mu$ is the number of qubits (resources) at node $\mu$, and $\lambda_+, \lambda_-$ are the eigenvalues associated with the symmetric and antisymmetric subspaces of the GHZ state in the network. The term $(\lambda_+ - \lambda_-)$ represents the effective coherence (or visibility) of the distributed GHZ state, while $(\lambda_+ + \lambda_-)$ represents the total weight of the state in this coherent subspace.

### 2. Application to the Scaled Average Parameter $\theta_1$
The sensing objective is to estimate $\theta_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}} \sum_{i=1}^d x_i$. The QFI for a linear combination of parameters is calculated as $\mathcal{F}(\theta_1) = \sum_{i,j=1}^d c_i c_j Q_{ij}$, with coefficients $c_i = 1/\sqrt{d}$. Substituting the expression for $Q_{ij}$:
$$
\mathcal{F}(\theta_1) = \frac{1}{d} \sum_{i=1}^d \sum_{j=1}^d \left( 4 \frac{(\lambda_+ - \lambda_-)^2}{\lambda_+ + \lambda_-} a_i a_j \right) = \frac{4 (\lambda_+ - \lambda_-)^2}{d (\lambda_+ + \lambda_-)} \left( \sum_{i=1}^d a_i \right)^2
$$
Given that each of the $d$ nodes contains $n$ qubits, we have $a_i = n$ for all $i$, and the total number of qubits is $\sum a_i = nd$. Assuming the state is normalized within the relevant coherent subspace, $\lambda_+ + \lambda_- = 1$. The expression simplifies to:
$$
\mathcal{F}(\theta_1) = 4 d n^2 (\lambda_+ - \lambda_-)^2
$$

### 3. Incorporating Initial Fidelity and Dynamical Dephasing
The initial probe state is an $nd$-qubit depolarized GHZ state with fidelity $F(n) = F k^{n-1}$. This initial imperfection scales the effective coherence by $F(n)$. 
During the sensing dynamics of duration $t$, each of the $nd$ qubits undergoes independent single-qubit dephasing at rate $\gamma$. As established in open quantum systems theory and referenced in [1], dephasing on $N$ qubits causes the off-diagonal coherence terms to decay exponentially as $e^{-N\gamma t}$. Here, $N = nd$. 
Thus, the total effective coherence factor $(\lambda_+ - \lambda_-)$ becomes:
$$
\lambda_+ - \lambda_- = F(n) e^{-nd \gamma t} = F k^{n-1} e^{-nd \gamma t}
$$

### 4. Final Expression in terms of $q$
We are given the variable $q = \frac{1 + e^{-\gamma t}}{2}$, which implies $e^{-\gamma t} = 2q - 1$. Substituting this into the coherence factor:
$$
\lambda_+ - \lambda_- = F k^{n-1} (2q - 1)^{nd}
$$
Squaring this term and substituting it back into the simplified QFI expression $\mathcal{F}(\theta_1) = 4 d n^2 (\lambda_+ - \lambda_-)^2$ yields the final result:

$$
\boxed{Q_{\theta_1} = 4 d n^2 F^2 k^{2n-2} (2q - 1)^{2nd}}
$$
