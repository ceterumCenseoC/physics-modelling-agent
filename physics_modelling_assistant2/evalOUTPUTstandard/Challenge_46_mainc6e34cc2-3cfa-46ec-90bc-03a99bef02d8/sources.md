

# Relevant Sources for PXP Model and Quantum Many-Body Scars Research

- **arxivID**: 2204.02899
- **Author**: Marko Ljubotina, Barbara Roos, Dmitry A. Abanin, Maksym Serbyn
- **Date**: 2022-04-06
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02899
- **Summary**: This paper investigates the optimal steering of quantum states in the PXP model, providing detailed analysis of overlaps with the $Z_2$ state $|1010\dots\rangle$ and spectral properties of scar eigenstates. It is highly relevant for building models to compute scar energies and logarithmic overlaps in specific symmetry sectors.

- **arxivID**: 2407.14691
- **Author**: Alessandra Chioquetta, Raphael Campos Drumond
- **Date**: 2024-07-19
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14691
- **Summary**: Focuses on the stability of quantum many-body scars under perturbations in the PXP model. It numerically computes fidelities and correlation functions to monitor state evolution, offering direct insights into how scar states overlap with specific product states like $Z_2$ and their energy distributions.

- **arxivID**: 2410.18913
- **Author**: Aron Kerschbaumer, Marko Ljubotina, Maksym Serbyn, Jean-Yves Desaules
- **Date**: 2024-10-24
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18913
- **Summary**: A very recent study exploring quantum many-body scars beyond the standard PXP model in Rydberg atom simulators. It benchmarks scar phenomena against the PXP Hamiltonian, discussing spectral signatures, translation/reflection symmetries, and overlaps with crystalline $Z_2$ states, making it essential for modern implementations of the problem.

- **arxivID**: 2204.13718
- **Author**: Ana Hudomal, Jean-Yves Desaules, Bhaskar Mukherjee, Guo-Xian Su, Jad C. Halimeh, Zlatko Papić
- **Date**: 2022-04-28
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.13718
- **Summary**: Examines periodic driving techniques to engineer and identify quantum scars in the PXP model. The work provides extensive numerical data on scar state energies and their overlaps with initial product states, useful for validating models in symmetry-decomposed subspaces.

- **arxivID**: 2312.16288
- **Author**: Giuliano Giudici, Federica Maria Surace, Hannes Pichler
- **Date**: 2023-12-26
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.16288
- **Summary**: Unravels PXP many-body scars through Floquet dynamics, offering a detailed look at the spectral structure and eigenstate overlaps. It discusses how scars evade thermalization and maintain large overlaps with specific symmetry-adapted states, complementing static spectral analysis for the $\mathcal{D}_0^+$ subspace.
