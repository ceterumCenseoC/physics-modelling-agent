

# Relevant Sources for High-Harmonic Generation with Structured Light

1. **Spin constrained orbital angular momentum control in high-harmonic generation**
   - **arxivID**: 1810.05894
   - **Author**: F. Kong, C. Zhang, H. Larocque, F. Bouchard, Z. Li, M. Taucer, G. Brown, T. J. Hammond, E. Karimi, P. B. Corkum
   - **date**: 2018-10-13
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05894
   - **short summary**: Directly addresses the conservation and scaling of orbital angular momentum (OAM) and spin angular momentum (helicity) during the HHG up-conversion process. Provides rigorous selection rules and spin-orbit interaction models essential for calculating the OAM ($\ell_q$) and helicity ($\sigma_q$) of specific harmonic orders driven by complex, structured light fields.

2. **Controlling Photon Transverse Orbital Angular Momentum in High Harmonic Generation**
   - **arxivID**: 2308.05398
   - **Author**: Yiqi Fang, Shengyue Lu, Yunquan Liu
   - **date**: 2023-08-10
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.05398
   - **short summary**: A very recent study investigating HHG driven by spatiotemporal optical vortex pulses. It provides contemporary theoretical frameworks for OAM conservation, scaling laws, and the generation of structured EUV light, supporting the modeling of time-delayed pulse sequences with specific topological charges.

3. **High orbital angular momentum harmonic generation**
   - **arxivID**: 1611.03203
   - **Author**: J. Vieira, R. M. G. M. Trines, E. P. Alves, R. A. Fonseca, J. T. Mendonça, R. Bingham, P. Norreys, L. O. Silva
   - **date**: 2016-11-10
   - **url**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.03203
   - **short summary**: Explores fundamental mechanisms for generating and amplifying high OAM harmonics by manipulating topological charges independently of other laser properties. Offers foundational scaling laws and angular momentum conservation principles directly applicable to calculating the properties of high-order harmonics like the 23rd order.
