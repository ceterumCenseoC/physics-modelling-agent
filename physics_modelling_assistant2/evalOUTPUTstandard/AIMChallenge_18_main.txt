Two dielectric nanoparticles are deeply trapped in two Gaussian optical traps that propagate along the $z$-axis, both characterized by the wave vector $k$ and the Rayleigh range $z_R$. Let the distance between the two nanoparticles be $d$, satisfying the far-field condition $kd \gg 1$. The polarizabilities of the two nanoparticles are $\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_2$, respectively.
